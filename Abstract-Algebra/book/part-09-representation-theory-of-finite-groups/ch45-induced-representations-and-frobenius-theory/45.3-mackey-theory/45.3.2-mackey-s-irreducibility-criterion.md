# 45.3.2 Mackey's Irreducibility Criterion

When is an induced representation $\mathrm{Ind}^G_H W$ irreducible? The first orthogonality relation, Frobenius reciprocity, and the Mackey formula combine to give a precise and usable criterion.

\begin{theorem}[Mackey's Irreducibility Criterion]
Let $H \leq G$ and$W$ an irreducible representation of$H$. Then$\mathrm{Ind}^G_H W$ is irreducible if and only if for every$s \in G \setminus H$, the representations${}^s W|_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}$ and$W|_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}$ of$H \cap s^{-1}Hs$ have no common irreducible constituent.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By first orthogonality, $\mathrm{Ind}^G_H W$ is irreducible iff$\langle \chi_{\mathrm{Ind} W}, \chi_{\mathrm{Ind} W} \rangle_G = 1$. By Frobenius reciprocity:
$$\langle \chi_{\mathrm{Ind} W}, \chi_{\mathrm{Ind} W} \rangle_G = \langle \chi_W, \chi_{\mathrm{Res}^G_H \mathrm{Ind}^G_H W} \rangle_H.$$
Applying the Mackey formula (with $K = H$) to expand$\mathrm{Res}^G_H \mathrm{Ind}^G_H W$:
$$= \langle \chi_W, \bigoplus_{s \in H \backslash G / H} \chi_{\mathrm{Ind}^H_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}({}^sW|_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs})} \rangle_H = \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H} \langle \chi_W, \mathrm{Ind}^H_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}({}^sW) \rangle_H.$$
Applying Frobenius reciprocity again (for the pair $H \cap s^{-1}Hs \leq H$):
$$= \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H} \langle \chi_W|_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}, \chi_{{}^sW|_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}} \rangle_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}.$$
The $s = e$ double coset contributes$\langle \chi_W|_H, \chi_W|_H \rangle_H = 1$ (since$W$ is irreducible). So$\mathrm{Ind}^G_H W$ is irreducible iff the remaining terms all vanish, i.e., iff$\langle W|_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs}, {}^sW|_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs} \rangle_{H \cap s^{-1}Hs} = 0$ for every$s \notin H$.
\end{proof}

The condition on double cosets can be stated more geometrically: the induced representation is irreducible when $W$ cannot be "compared" to any of its conjugates${}^sW$ upon restricting both to the intersection$H \cap s^{-1}Hs$. When the conjugates of$H$ in$G$ all have trivial intersection with$H$ (as occurs for Frobenius complements), the intersections$H \cap s^{-1}Hs$ are trivial for$s \notin H$, and any representation restricted to the trivial group is just a direct sum of trivial representations. In this extreme case, the inner product condition is always satisfied, and any irreducible$W$ of$H$ induces to an irreducible of$G$.

\begin{example}
For $G = AGL(1,p)$ and$H = \mathbb{F}_p^* \cong C_{p-1}$ (the stabilizer of 0): the Frobenius complement condition gives$H \cap s^{-1}Hs = \{e\}$ for all$s \notin H$. By Mackey's criterion,$\mathrm{Ind}^G_H W$ is irreducible for any irreducible (hence 1-dimensional) character$W$ of$H$.
\par
These induced representations have dimension $[G:H] = p$. They account for all irreducibles of$G$ not factored through$G/K = H$ (the inflations from$H$). Together with the$(p-1)$ 1-dimensional characters from$H$, they give$1 + (p-1) = p$ irreducibles total, matching the number of conjugacy classes of$AGL(1,p)$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}
For $G = D_4$ and$H = \langle r \rangle \cong C_4$ (cyclic rotation subgroup of order 4):$[G:H] = 2$. The non-trivial double coset representative is$s =$ any reflection, say$s$. Intersection:$H \cap s^{-1}Hs$. Since$srs^{-1} = r^{-1}$,$s^{-1}Hs = \{e, r^{-1}, r^{-2}, r^{-3}\} = H$. So$H \cap s^{-1}Hs = H \cap H = H$. The irreducibility criterion: we need$\langle W|_H, {}^sW|_H \rangle_H = 0$, i.e.,$W \ncong {}^sW$ as$H$-representations. For$W$ the character$\chi_k$ of$C_4$ with$\chi_k(r) = i^k$:${}^sW(r) = W(srs^{-1}) = W(r^{-1}) = \chi_k(r^{-1}) = i^{-k} = \chi_{-k}$. So${}^sW \cong \chi_{-k}$. Thus$W \ncong {}^sW$ iff$\chi_k \ncong \chi_{-k}$ iff$k \not\equiv -k \pmod{4}$ iff$k \not\equiv 0, 2 \pmod{4}$. For$k = 1$ or$k = 3$:$\mathrm{Ind}^{D_4}_{C_4} \chi_k$ is irreducible (giving the 2-dimensional irreducible$\chi_5$). For$k = 0, 2$ (real characters):$\mathrm{Ind}^{D_4}_{C_4} \chi_k$ is reducible.
\end{example}

Mackey's criterion is the primary tool for proving that specific induced representations are irreducible. In practice, one enumerates the double cosets $H \backslash G / H$, computes the intersections$H \cap s^{-1}Hs$ for representatives$s$ of each non-trivial double coset, and checks the orthogonality of restrictions.
