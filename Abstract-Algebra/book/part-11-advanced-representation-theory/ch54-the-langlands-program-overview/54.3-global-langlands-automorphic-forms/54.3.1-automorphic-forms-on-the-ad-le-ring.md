# 54.3.1 Automorphic Forms on $GL_n(\mathbb{A})$; the Adèle Ring

The global Langlands program requires a single framework that combines information from all primes simultaneously, including the archimedean prime $\infty$. The *adèle ring*$\mathbb{A}_K = \mathbb{A}_K^{fin} \times \prod_{v \mid \infty} K_v$ is the restricted product of all local fields$K_v$ over the places$v$ of$K$, where the "restricted" condition means that almost all components lie in the corresponding ring of integers$\mathcal{O}_v$. The adèle ring is a locally compact ring containing$K$ as a discrete cocompact subring, and the quotient$GL_n(K) \backslash GL_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ is a rich space carrying both analytic and arithmetic information.

\begin{definition}
An *automorphic form* on $GL_n$ over a number field$K$ is a function$f: GL_n(\mathbb{A}_K) \to \mathbb{C}$ satisfying:
\begin{itemize}
\item Left $GL_n(K)$-invariance:$f(\gamma g) = f(g)$ for all$\gamma \in GL_n(K)$.
\item $(K_\infty, \mathfrak{g}_\infty)$-finiteness:$f$ is smooth at archimedean places and$K_\infty$-finite.
\item $Z(\mathfrak{g}_\infty)$-finiteness:$f$ is annihilated by an ideal of finite codimension in the center$Z(U(\mathfrak{g}_\infty))$.
\item Right $\mathcal{K}$-invariance:$f(gk) = f(g)$ for some open compact$\mathcal{K} \subset GL_n(\mathbb{A}_K^{fin})$.
\item Moderate growth: $|f(g)| \leq C \|g\|^N$ for some$C, N$.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

**Example 1.** For $K = \mathbb{Q}$ and$n = 2$: a classical modular form of weight$k$ and level$N$ gives an automorphic form on$GL_2(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}})$. Specifically, if$f(\tau) = \sum_{n \geq 1} a_n e^{2\pi i n\tau}$ is a cuspidal modular eigenform of weight$k$ and level$N$, the automorphic form$\phi_f: GL_2(\mathbb{A}) \to \mathbb{C}$ is defined by$\phi_f(g) = (f|_k g_\infty)(i)$ (for$g = \gamma g_\infty k_f \in GL_2(\mathbb{Q}) \cdot GL_2(\mathbb{R})^+ \cdot K_0(N)$), where$f|_k$ is the weight-$k$ action of$GL_2(\mathbb{R})$. The condition$Z(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$-finiteness corresponds to$f$ being an eigenfunction of the Laplacian$\Delta$ (the Casimir element acting on functions on$\mathfrak{h}$).

The *automorphic representations* are the irreducible smooth subquotients of the space of automorphic forms.

\begin{theorem}[Flath's Factorization Theorem]
Every automorphic representation $\pi$ of$GL_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ decomposes as a restricted tensor product:
$$\pi \cong \bigotimes_v' \pi_v$$
over all places $v$ of$K$, where$\pi_v$ is an irreducible admissible representation of$GL_n(K_v)$, and$\pi_v$ is spherical (has a$GL_n(\mathcal{O}_v)$-fixed vector) for almost all$v$.
\end{theorem}

**Example 2.** For $K = \mathbb{Q}$ and$f$ a cuspidal eigenform of weight$k$ and level$N$ with Hecke eigenvalues$\lambda_p$ for$p \nmid N$: the automorphic representation$\pi_f = \bigotimes'_v \pi_v$ has:
- $\pi_\infty$: the discrete series representation of$GL_2(\mathbb{R})$ with lowest weight$k$ (parameterized by its Harish-Chandra parameter, essentially$k$).
- $\pi_p$ for$p \nmid N$: the unramified principal series$\mathrm{Ind}(\alpha_p \boxtimes \beta_p)$ where$\alpha_p, \beta_p$ are determined by$\lambda_p = \alpha_p + \beta_p$ and$\alpha_p \beta_p = p^{k-1}$ (the Satake parameters).
- $\pi_p$ for$p \mid N$: a ramified representation (principal series, special, or supercuspidal) determined by the local behavior of$f$ at$p$.

\begin{definition}
An automorphic representation $\pi$ of$GL_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ is *cuspidal* if the constant terms of its matrix coefficients along all proper parabolic subgroups vanish. Cuspidal representations are the "atoms" of the automorphic spectrum — they cannot be constructed from automorphic representations of smaller groups.
\end{definition}

**Example 3.** The Ramanujan conjecture: for a cuspidal automorphic representation $\pi$ of$GL_n(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}})$, the unramified Satake parameters$\alpha_{p,1}, \ldots, \alpha_{p,n}$ (the eigenvalues of the unramified Hecke operators at$p$) are expected to satisfy$|\alpha_{p,i}| = 1$ (purity). For$GL_1$, this is trivially true. For$GL_2$ (modular forms), Ramanujan conjectured$|a_p| \leq 2p^{(k-1)/2}$ for holomorphic forms of weight$k$; this was proved by Deligne using the Weil conjectures. For higher$GL_n$, the Ramanujan conjecture is related to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis and remains open in full generality.

\begin{proposition}
Every cuspidal automorphic representation of $GL_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ has a central character$\omega_\pi: K^* \backslash \mathbb{A}_K^* \to \mathbb{C}^*$, and the$L$-function$L(s, \pi)$ converges absolutely for$\mathrm{Re}(s) \gg 0$, extends meromorphically to all of$\mathbb{C}$, and satisfies a functional equation$L(s, \pi) = \varepsilon(s, \pi) L(1-s, \tilde\pi)$ relating$\pi$ to its contragredient$\tilde\pi$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}
The adèlic framework is essential for the Langlands program: it combines all local data into a single coherent global object. The key point is that the double quotient $GL_n(K) \backslash GL_n(\mathbb{A}_K) / (\text{open compact at finite places}) \times K_\infty$ is a finite union of locally symmetric spaces (like the upper half-plane$\mathfrak{h}$ for$GL_2$), and automorphic forms are functions on this double quotient. The Hecke operators, which generate the commutative algebra of$GL_n(\mathcal{O}_v)$-biinvariant functions on$GL_n(K_v)$ at unramified places, act on automorphic forms and diagonalize the cuspidal spectrum, giving the Hecke eigenvalues that encode the arithmetic information (Frobenius eigenvalues, traces of Galois representations).
\end{remark}
