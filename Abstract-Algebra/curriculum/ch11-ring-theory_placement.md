## Content in curriculum/ not fully covered in book/

- The curriculum has a unified treatment of the **Chinese Remainder Theorem** for rings (not just for $\mathbb{Z}$) as a consequence of coprime ideals. The book covers CRT for $\mathbb{Z}$ in number theory but the general ring-theoretic form in terms of coprime ideals is only implicit in `ch24` (Noetherian rings). → Add a subsection on CRT for rings to `ch21`.
- The curriculum discusses **prime and maximal ideals** and the spectrum $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ as a geometric object. The book has prime/maximal ideals in `ch21` but does not introduce $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ as a space. → Add an introductory section on $\mathrm{Spec}$ as motivation for commutative algebra and algebraic geometry.
