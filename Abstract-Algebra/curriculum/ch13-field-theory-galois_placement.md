## Content in curriculum/ not fully covered in book/

- The curriculum contains a **self-contained proof of the insolvability of the general quintic** via the Galois group of $x^5 - 2$ being $S_5$ and the non-solvability of $S_5$. The book discusses solvability by radicals in `ch32` but the proof of $\mathrm{Gal}(x^5 - ax - b) = S_5$ for generic $a, b$ is not given. → Add a subsection to `32.1` with this complete worked proof.
- The curriculum has a brief discussion of **Galois cohomology and its connection to $H^2$** (linking field theory to the homological algebra of Part VIII). The book does not have this forward connection explicitly noted in Part VI.
- The curriculum discusses the **primitive element theorem** explicitly (every separable extension is simple). The book has this but it is presented somewhat tersely; the curriculum's proof using the counting argument deserves to be in the book at the same level of detail.
