## Content in curriculum/ not fully covered in book/

- The curriculum discusses **Lie group homomorphisms and the relationship between Lie group morphisms and Lie algebra morphisms** in more detail than `46.3`. The fundamental theorem — that a Lie algebra homomorphism between the Lie algebras of simply-connected Lie groups integrates to a Lie group homomorphism — is stated but not proved in the book.
- The curriculum has a detailed treatment of the **classification of compact, connected, simply connected Lie groups** (the ADE classification connection). The book defers classification to Part XI. A brief preview in `ch48` would strengthen Part X.
- The curriculum discusses the **Killing form and its non-degeneracy for semisimple algebras** as central to Cartan's criterion. The book covers this but the centrality of the Killing form could be emphasised more.
