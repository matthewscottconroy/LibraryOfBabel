# The Cathedrals: Integrated Research Projects

Each cathedral is a substantial, original research contribution that cannot be built without every tier of the ladder beneath it. They are not exercises — they are real work.

---

## What Makes a Cathedral

A cathedral project:
- Addresses a genuine research question (not a textbook problem)
- Integrates knowledge and methods from multiple tiers
- Produces something that could be submitted as a manuscript
- Is yours — your question, your approach, your interpretation

The seven cathedrals below span the major modes of research in this field. You don't build all seven. You choose one, build it carefully, and let it teach you what you still need to learn.

## Choosing Your Cathedral

Consider:
- What biological question do you find most compelling?
- What experimental context do you have access to (wet lab, compute cluster, collaborators)?
- What mode of contribution do you want to make — experimental design, modeling, software?

There is no wrong choice. Each cathedral teaches things no other will.
