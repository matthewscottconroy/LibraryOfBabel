## How to Use This Text

The chapters are modular but ordered. Part I should be mastered first — not skimmed but *owned*. Parts II and III can be studied in parallel once the foundations are solid. Part IV is the synthesis; do not rush to it. Parts V and VI assume fluency in both pillars. Part VII connects everything to your ongoing research projects.

**Notation:** Mathematical notation follows standard conventions. Inline mathematics uses `$...$` and display mathematics uses `$$...$$`. Definitions, theorems, proofs, examples, and exercises are numbered by chapter and position (e.g., Definition 3.2.1, Theorem 7.4.2).

**Proof convention:** Proofs marked *(proof sketch)* are abbreviated to give the key idea. Full proofs can be found in the referenced primary sources. Proofs marked *(proof)* are complete.
