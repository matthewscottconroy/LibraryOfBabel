# Chapter Notes — Chapter 34

Sofic entropy is recent enough that the original papers are essential reading — this isn't a theory that has been fully processed into textbooks yet.

**The original papers.** Gromov introduced sofic groups in *Endomorphisms of Symbolic Algebraic Varieties* (J. European Math. Soc., 1999); the term "sofic" was coined by Weiss in *Sofic groups and dynamical systems* (Sankhyā, 2000). Both papers are short and worth reading directly.

**Bowen's sofic entropy.** The foundational paper is: *Measure conjugacy invariants for actions of countable sofic groups* (J. AMS, 2010). This is the paper where everything starts — it defines sofic entropy, proves the Bernoulli classification, and identifies the key open problems. It's a 30-page paper that you can read in a few hours.

**Kerr-Li.** The topological sofic entropy and proof of independence from the sofic approximation: *Entropy and the variational principle for actions of sofic groups* (Inventiones Math., 2011). Kerr and Li have written extensively on this topic; their book *Ergodic Theory: Independence and Dichotomies* (Springer, 2016) is the current comprehensive reference.

**Seward's Rokhlin entropy.** The key papers are: *Krieger's finite generator theorem for actions of countable groups I* (Invent. Math., 2019) and *II* (J. d'Analyse Math., 2020). The second paper proves the equivalence with sofic entropy. These papers develop remarkable new techniques.

**Operator algebra connections.** The relationship between sofic entropy, von Neumann algebras, and free probability is explored in Hayes's papers; see: *A random matrix approach to the Peterson-Thom conjecture* (Indiana Math. J., 2021). This is frontier research connecting sofic groups to deep questions about II$_1$ factors.

**Open problems.** The question of whether all groups are sofic is the outstanding open problem. The question of whether sofic entropy depends on the sofic approximation for non-Bernoulli actions is also open in general. The most recent papers by Bowen, Hayes, and others address specific cases. If you want to work on this area, read Bowen's blog (https://lewisbowen.wordpress.com) for current discussions.
