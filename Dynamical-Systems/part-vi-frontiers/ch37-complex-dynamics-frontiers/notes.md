# Chapter Notes — Chapter 37

**Douady-Hubbard theory.** The foundational papers are: *Étude dynamique des polynômes complexes I, II* (Publications Mathématiques d'Orsay, 1984-1985). These are the papers that introduced external rays, the Böttcher coordinate, the Mandelbrot set as parameter space, and polynomial-like maps. Douady's 1983 ICM address is also essential reading.

**Thurston's theorem.** Thurston never published his topological characterization directly; it appears in Douady-Hubbard's: *A proof of Thurston's topological characterization of rational functions* (Acta Math., 1993). The proof there is by iterating a "pullback map" on Teichmüller space and showing it converges to a rational function.

**Renormalization and Feigenbaum universality.** Lyubich's key papers: *Dynamics of quadratic polynomials I, II* (Acta Math., 1997) and *Feigenbaum-Coullet-Tresser universality and Milnor's hairiness conjecture* (Annals of Math., 1999). These papers finally proved the renormalization fixed point rigorously. McMullen's *Complex Dynamics and Renormalization* (Princeton University Press, 1994) provides a book-length treatment.

**MLC and Yoccoz.** Yoccoz never published a paper specifically on MLC; his proof of local connectivity at finitely renormalizable points appears in Hubbard's account: *Local connectivity of Julia sets and bifurcation loci* in *Topological Methods in Modern Mathematics* (Publish or Perish, 1993). Kahn-Lyubich's extension: *Local connectivity of Julia sets for unicritical polynomials* (Annals of Math., 2009).

**Lambda lemma.** Mañé-Sad-Sullivan: *On the dynamics of rational maps* (Annals of Science École Normale Supérieure, 1983). Słodkowski: *Holomorphic motions and polynomial hulls* (Proc. AMS, 1991).

**Parabolic implosion.** Douady's original lectures on parabolic implosion are collected in Tan Lei's volume *The Mandelbrot Set, Theme and Variations* (Cambridge, 2000). Lavaurs's work on horn maps and parabolic implosion is in his 1989 thesis.

**Shishikura's dimension results.** *The Hausdorff dimension of the boundary of the Mandelbrot set and Julia sets* (Annals of Math., 1998). This paper showed $\dim_H \partial \mathcal{M} = 2$.
