# Chapter Notes — Chapter 38

**QMA and Local Hamiltonian.** Kitaev's QMA-completeness result is in his lecture notes *Classical and quantum computation* (AMS Graduate Studies in Mathematics, 2002), co-authored with Shen and Vyalyi. The reduction to 2-local on a line: Hallgren-Nagaj-Narayanaswami (2013).

**Undecidability of spectral gap.** Cubitt-Pérez-García-Wolf: *Undecidability of the spectral gap* (Nature, 2015) and the full paper in *Forum of Mathematics, Pi* (2015). This is a landmark paper — read the Nature letter for the ideas, then the full paper for the proof.

**MIP\* = RE.** Ji-Natarajan-Vidick-Wright-Yuen: *MIP* = RE* (2020, arXiv:2001.04383). The proof is 165 pages. The implication that Connes' embedding conjecture is false: Connes conjectured in 1976 that every tracial von Neumann algebra embeds into an ultrapower of the hyperfinite II$_1$ factor; MIP\* = RE gives a counterexample via a quantum correlation that cannot be approximated by finite-dimensional systems.

**NLTS and Quantum PCP.** Anshu-Breuckmann-Nirkhe: *NLTS Hamiltonians from good quantum codes* (STOC 2023, also arXiv:2206.13228). The quantum PCP conjecture is surveyed in Aharonov-Arad's notes (arXiv:1309.7495).

**Shor's algorithm.** The original: *Polynomial-time algorithms for prime factorization and discrete logarithms on a quantum computer* (SIAM J. Comp., 1997). Grover's: *A fast quantum mechanical algorithm for database search* (STOC, 1996). The BBBV lower bound: Bennett-Bernstein-Brassard-Vazirani (SIAM J. Comp., 1997).

**Area laws.** Hastings's area law in 1D: *An area law for one-dimensional quantum systems* (J. Stat. Mech., 2007). The 2D area law is open; for current progress see Anshu (2022). Tensor networks: Orús's review *A practical introduction to tensor networks* (Annals of Physics, 2014).

**Lindblad's theorem.** *On the generators of quantum dynamical semigroups* (Comm. Math. Phys., 1976). Quantum dynamical systems and ergodicity: Attal-Joye-Pillet (eds.), *Open Quantum Systems I, II, III* (Springer, 2006).
