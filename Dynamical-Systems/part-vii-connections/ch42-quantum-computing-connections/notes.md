# Chapter Notes — Chapter 42

**Adiabatic quantum computation.** Farhi-Goldstone-Gutmann-Sipser: *Quantum computation by adiabatic evolution* (MIT-CTP-2936, 2000; arXiv:quant-ph/0001106). The equivalence with gate-based QC: Aharonov-van Dam-Kempe-Landau-Lloyd-Regev (SIAM J. Comp., 2007). The adiabatic theorem: Born-Fock (1928), but see also Jansen-Ruskai-Seiler for modern bounds.

**ETH and quantum chaos.** The original papers: Deutsch's *Quantum statistical mechanics in a closed system* (Phys. Rev. A, 1991) and Srednicki's *Chaos and quantum thermalization* (Phys. Rev. E, 1994). The comprehensive review: D'Alessio-Kafri-Polkovnikov-Rigol *From quantum chaos and eigenstate thermalization to statistical mechanics and thermodynamics* (Advances in Physics, 2016) — highly recommended.

**Berry-Tabor / BGS conjecture.** Berry-Tabor: *Level clustering in the regular spectrum* (Proc. R. Soc., 1977). Bohigas-Giannoni-Schmit: *Characterization of chaotic quantum spectra and universality of level fluctuation laws* (Phys. Rev. Lett., 1984). Gutzwiller's *Chaos in Classical and Quantum Mechanics* (Springer, 1990) is the book.

**Quantum error correction.** The foundational papers: Shor's *Scheme for reducing decoherence in quantum computer memory* (Phys. Rev. A, 1995); Steane's *Error correcting codes in quantum theory* (Phys. Rev. Lett., 1996); Knill-Laflamme's conditions in *Theory of quantum error-correcting codes* (Phys. Rev. A, 1997). Kitaev's toric code: *Fault-tolerant quantum computation by anyons* (Annals of Physics, 2003). The threshold theorem connection to statistical mechanics: Dennis-Kitaev-Landahl-Preskill (J. Math. Phys., 2002).

**Quantum phase transitions.** Sachdev's *Quantum Phase Transitions* (Cambridge, 2001) is the comprehensive reference. Cubitt-Pérez-García-Wolf's undecidability result (Nature, 2015). Dynamical phase transitions: Heyl's *Dynamical quantum phase transitions: a review* (Rep. Prog. Phys., 2018).

**Variational quantum algorithms.** QAOA: Farhi-Goldstone-Gutmann (arXiv:1411.4028, 2014). Barren plateaus: McClean-Romero-Babbush-Aspuru-Guzik (Nature Commun., 2018). The recent review Blekos et al. (2024) surveys the state of QAOA.
