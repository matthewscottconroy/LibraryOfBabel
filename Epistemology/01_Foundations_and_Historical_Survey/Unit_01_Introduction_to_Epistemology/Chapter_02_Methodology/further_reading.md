# Further Reading and Primary References

## Primary Sources

- Plato. *Meno* and *Phaedo*. The dialogues that employ the method of definition and hypothetical inquiry.
- Descartes, René. *Rules for the Direction of the Mind* (Regulae). (c. 1628). Descartes's methodological treatise, setting out the rules of clear and distinct perception.
- Descartes, René. *Discourse on Method*. (1637). The accessible introduction to Descartes's method, including the famous autobiographical account.
- Hegel, G.W.F. *Phenomenology of Spirit*. (1807). Introduces the dialectical method; knowledge advances through the negation and sublation of incomplete standpoints.
- Rawls, John. "Outline of a Decision Procedure for Ethics." *Philosophical Review* 60 (1951): 177–197. Early statement of the reflective equilibrium method.
- Rawls, John. *A Theory of Justice*. Harvard University Press, 1971. Part I develops reflective equilibrium as a methodological approach.

## Key Secondary Sources

- Chalmers, David, David Manley, and Ryan Wasserman, eds. *Metametaphysics: New Essays on the Foundations of Ontology*. Oxford University Press, 2009. Includes important essays on philosophical methodology.
- DePaul, Michael and William Ramsey, eds. *Rethinking Intuition: The Psychology of Intuition and Its Role in Philosophical Inquiry*. Rowman and Littlefield, 1998. Cross-disciplinary examination of the role of intuitions.
- Goodman, Nelson. *Fact, Fiction, and Forecast*. 4th ed. Harvard University Press, 1983. Chapter 3 introduces the idea of reflective equilibrium as a general epistemological method.
- Sosa, Ernest. *A Virtue Epistemology: Apt Belief and Reflective Knowledge*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Defends the role of intuition and intellectual virtue in epistemological methodology.
- Williamson, Timothy. *The Philosophy of Philosophy*. Blackwell, 2007. A sustained defense of philosophical methodology using thought experiments and conceptual analysis.
- Nichols, Shaun, ed. *The Architecture of the Imagination*. Oxford University Press, 2006. Essays on the cognitive bases of thought experiments.

## On Thought Experiments Specifically

- Brown, James Robert. *The Laboratory of the Mind: Thought Experiments in the Natural Sciences*. Routledge, 1991.
- Sorensen, Roy. *Thought Experiments*. Oxford University Press, 1992.
- Cooper, Rachel. "Thought Experiments." *Metaphilosophy* 36 (2005): 328–347.
