# Further Reading and Primary References

## Primary Sources

- Heraclitus. *Fragments*. (~500 BCE). DK B1–B126. Translations by Charles Kahn (*The Art and Thought of Heraclitus*, Cambridge, 1979) or T.M. Robinson (*Heraclitus: Fragments*, Toronto, 1987).
- Parmenides. *On Nature*. (~475 BCE). DK B1–B19. In McKirahan, Richard. *Philosophy Before Socrates*. Hackett, 2010.
- Plato. *Theaetetus*. The dialogue discusses Protagoras's relativism and the Heraclitean flux doctrine in epistemological context.
- Sextus Empiricus. *Against the Mathematicians* (Adversus Mathematicos). Discusses the Pre-Socratic background to skepticism.

## Secondary Sources

- Barnes, Jonathan. *The Presocratic Philosophers*. Routledge, 1979. Comprehensive philosophical analysis of the Pre-Socratics, including their epistemological commitments.
- Guthrie, W.K.C. *A History of Greek Philosophy*. Vol. 1–2. Cambridge University Press, 1962. Standard scholarly reference.
- Hussey, Edward. *The Presocratics*. Duckworth, 1972. Accessible introduction with philosophical emphasis.
- Kirk, G.S., J.E. Raven, and M. Schofield. *The Presocratic Philosophers*. 2nd ed. Cambridge University Press, 1983. Standard scholarly edition with texts and commentary.
- McKirahan, Richard. *Philosophy Before Socrates*. 2nd ed. Hackett, 2010. Best single-volume introduction combining texts and analysis.
- Palmer, John. *Parmenides and Presocratic Philosophy*. Oxford University Press, 2009. Detailed study of Parmenides's epistemological significance.

## On Protagoras and Relativism

- Burnyeat, M.F. "Protagoras and Self-Refutation in Plato's *Theaetetus*." *Philosophical Review* 85 (1976): 172–195.
- Kerferd, G.B. *The Sophistic Movement*. Cambridge University Press, 1981.
