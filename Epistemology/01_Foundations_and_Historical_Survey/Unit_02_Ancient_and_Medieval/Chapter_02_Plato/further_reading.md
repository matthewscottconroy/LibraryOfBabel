# Further Reading and Primary References

## Primary Sources

- Plato. *Meno*. Trans. G.M.A. Grube. In *Five Dialogues*, Hackett, 2002. The paradox of inquiry and the theory of recollection.
- Plato. *Phaedo*. The argument from recollection; theory of Forms; the soul as seat of knowledge.
- Plato. *Republic*, Books V–VII. The theory of Forms, the Divided Line, the Allegory of the Cave, the Sun.
- Plato. *Theaetetus*. The primary text for Platonic epistemology; systematic examination of knowledge.
- Plato. *Sophist*. The problem of not-being; the theory of false statement and false belief.
- Plato. *Timaeus*. Cosmology and the distinction between knowledge (of eternal Forms) and belief (of temporal becoming).

## Key Secondary Sources

- Annas, Julia. *An Introduction to Plato's Republic*. Oxford University Press, 1981. Especially chapters on the epistemology of Forms and the Divided Line.
- Bostock, David. *Plato's Theaetetus*. Oxford University Press, 1988. Detailed philosophical commentary on the primary epistemological dialogue.
- Fine, Gail. *Plato on Knowledge and Forms*. Oxford University Press, 2003. Collected essays by a leading Plato scholar on epistemology and metaphysics.
- Fine, Gail, ed. *Plato*. 2 vols. Oxford University Press, 1999. Comprehensive collection of scholarly articles.
- McDowell, John. *Plato: Theaetetus*. Oxford University Press, 1973. Influential translation and commentary.
- Morrow, Glenn R. and John M. Dillon. *Proclus's Commentary on Plato's Parmenides*. Princeton, 1987.
- Nehamas, Alexander. "Plato on the Imperfection of the Sensible World." *American Philosophical Quarterly* 12 (1975): 105–117.
- White, Nicholas. *Plato on Knowledge and Reality*. Hackett, 1976.

## On Recollection

- Scott, Dominic. *Recollection and Experience: Plato's Theory of Learning and Its Successors*. Cambridge University Press, 1995.
