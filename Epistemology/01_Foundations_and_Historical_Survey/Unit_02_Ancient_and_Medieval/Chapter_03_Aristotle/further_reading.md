# Further Reading and Primary References

## Primary Sources

- Aristotle. *Posterior Analytics*. Trans. Jonathan Barnes. Oxford University Press, 1994. The central epistemological text: scientific knowledge, demonstration, induction.
- Aristotle. *Nicomachean Ethics*, Book VI. On the intellectual virtues: *episteme*, *nous*, *sophia*, *phronesis*, *techne*.
- Aristotle. *Metaphysics*, Books I and IV. On the desire to know, the first principles, and the principle of non-contradiction.
- Aristotle. *De Anima* (On the Soul). The theory of perception and intellect.
- Aristotle. *Topics* and *Sophistical Refutations*. On dialectical reasoning and the fallacies that corrupt it.

## Key Secondary Sources

- Barnes, Jonathan. *Aristotle's Posterior Analytics*. 2nd ed. Oxford University Press, 1993. The definitive scholarly commentary.
- Barnes, Jonathan. *Aristotle: A Very Short Introduction*. Oxford University Press, 2000. Accessible overview.
- Frede, Michael and Gisela Striker, eds. *Rationality in Greek Thought*. Oxford University Press, 1996.
- Hamlyn, D.W. *Aristotle's De Anima, Books II and III*. Oxford University Press, 1993. The perception and intellect chapters.
- Irwin, Terence. *Aristotle's First Principles*. Oxford University Press, 1988. Comprehensive treatment of Aristotle's foundationalism.
- Lesher, J.H. "The Meaning of Nous in the Posterior Analytics." *Phronesis* 18 (1973): 44–68.
- McKirahan, Richard. *Principles and Proofs: Aristotle's Theory of Demonstrative Science*. Princeton University Press, 1992.
- Tiles, J.E. "Why the Triangle Has 2 Right Angles Kath' Hauto." *Phronesis* 28 (1983): 1–16.

## On Aristotle's Epistemology

- Modrak, Deborah. *Aristotle: The Power of Perception*. University of Chicago Press, 1987.
- Taylor, C.C.W. "Aristotle's Epistemology." In *Epistemology*, ed. Stephen Everson. Cambridge University Press, 1990.
