## Primary Sources

- Galen Strawson, "Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 13, 2006. Argues that if physicalism is true and phenomenal consciousness exists, panpsychism follows.

- Philip Goff, *Galileo's Error: Foundations for a New Science of Mind*, Pantheon, 2019. Accessible book-length defense of panpsychism; argues that Galileo's exclusion of consciousness from physics was a methodological error.

- Philip Goff, *Consciousness and Fundamental Reality*, Oxford University Press, 2017. Technical defense of panpsychism; develops Russellian monism and addresses the combination problem.

- David Chalmers, "Panpsychism and Panprotopsychism," in Alter and Nagasawa (eds.), *Consciousness in the Physical World*, Oxford University Press, 2015. Clarifies and defends Russellian monism as a form of panpsychism.

- Bertrand Russell, *The Analysis of Matter*, Kegan Paul, 1927. Early statement of structuralism about physics and the claim that physics reveals only the structure, not the intrinsic nature, of matter.

- Hedda Hassel Morch, "Is Consciousness Intrinsic? A Problem for the Combination Problem," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 27, 2020. Addresses the combination problem from within panpsychism.

- Thomas Nagel, "Panpsychism," in *Mortal Questions*, Cambridge University Press, 1979. Early sympathetic treatment by Nagel.

- William James, "The Compounding of Consciousness," in *A Pluralistic Universe*, Longmans, Green, 1909. The earliest clear statement of the combination problem for panpsychism.

## Key Secondary Sources

- Torin Alter and Yujin Nagasawa (eds.), *Consciousness in the Physical World: Perspectives on Russellian Monism*, Oxford University Press, 2015. The essential anthology; covers Russellian monism, combination problem, and alternatives.

- Daniel Stoljar, *Ignorance and Imagination*, Oxford University Press, 2006. Defends panprotopsychism as an alternative to panpsychism proper.

- Sam Coleman, "Consciousness as a Guide to Personal Persistence," *Topoi* 31, 2012. Addresses the combination problem and cosmopsychism as an alternative.

- Eric Schwitzgebel, "The Weirdness of the World," in *The Weirdness of the World*, Princeton University Press, 2023. Defends a form of "crazy metaphysics" including panpsychism as less improbable than alternatives.

- Philip Goff, William Seager, and Sean Allen-Hermanson, "Panpsychism," *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2022. Comprehensive and current survey.

- Luke Roelofs, *Combining Minds*, Oxford University Press, 2019. The most thorough treatment of the combination problem; develops a constitution-based approach.

- Annaka Harris, *Conscious*, Harper, 2019. Popular but philosophically serious defense of panpsychism as the most coherent view of consciousness.
