# Chapter 36: Further Reading and References

---

## Primary Sources

**Oppenheimer, J.R. and Volkoff, G.M. (1939). "On Massive Neutron Cores." *Physical Review*, 55, 374–381.**
Derives the TOV equation and computes the maximum mass for a neutron star, finding $\sim 0.7 M_\odot$ for an ideal neutron gas (modern calculations with realistic equations of state give $2$–$3 M_\odot$).

**Tolman, R.C. (1934). *Relativity, Thermodynamics, and Cosmology.* Oxford University Press. (Reprinted Dover, 1987.)**
Tolman's systematic treatment of relativistic thermodynamics and the equilibrium conditions in GR. The hydrostatic equation (Tolman equation) appears here. Still a valuable reference.

**Chandrasekhar, S. (1931). "The Maximum Mass of Ideal White Dwarfs." *Astrophysical Journal*, 74, 81–82.**
The short original paper deriving the Chandrasekhar limit. Submitted from a steamship en route to England.

**Tolman, R.C. and Ehrenfest, P. (1930). "Temperature Equilibrium in a Static Gravitational Field." *Physical Review*, 36, 1791–1798.**
Proves that in hydrostatic equilibrium in GR, temperature is not uniform: $T\sqrt{-g_{00}} = \text{const}$ (the Tolman-Ehrenfest theorem). Temperature is lower where the gravitational potential is lower — the gravitational analog of the Carnot efficiency argument.

**Peters, P.C. and Mathews, J. (1963). "Gravitational Radiation from Point Masses in a Keplerian Orbit." *Physical Review*, 131, 435–440.**
Derives the power radiated in gravitational waves by a binary system (the quadrupole formula). The result that predicts the orbital decay of the Hulse-Taylor binary pulsar.

## Textbooks

**Misner, C.W., Thorne, K.S., and Wheeler, J.A. (1973). *Gravitation.* W.H. Freeman.**
Chapter 5 on the stress-energy tensor. Box 5.1 on energy-momentum for dust, perfect fluid, and EM field. Chapter 22 on the variational definition. Chapter 36 on pulsars and neutron stars.

**Shapiro, S.L. and Teukolsky, S.A. (1983). *Black Holes, White Dwarfs, and Neutron Stars: The Physics of Compact Objects.* Wiley.**
The standard text for compact object astrophysics. Chapters 2–4 on white dwarf structure (Chandrasekhar limit); Chapters 8–10 on neutron star structure (TOV equation, equations of state). Appendix F on the variational principle for the stress-energy tensor.

**Wald, R.M. (1984). *General Relativity.* University of Chicago Press.**
Chapter 4 on the stress-energy tensor and energy conditions. The most rigorous treatment of the energy conditions and their implications.

## On Neutron Star Physics

**Özel, F. and Freire, P. (2016). "Masses, Radii, and the Equation of State of Neutron Stars." *Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics*, 54, 401–440.**
Comprehensive review of observational constraints on neutron star masses and radii, and what they imply for the nuclear equation of state.

**Abbott, B.P. et al. (LIGO/Virgo, 2018). "GW170817: Measurements of neutron star radii and equation of state." *Physical Review Letters*, 121, 161101.**
The neutron star radius measurement from the tidal deformability of the GW170817 binary neutron star merger: $R_{1.4} = 11.9^{+1.4}_{-1.4}$ km. First constraint on the nuclear equation of state from gravitational wave observation.

**Demorest, P.B. et al. (2010). "A two-solar-mass neutron star measured using Shapiro delay." *Nature*, 467, 1081–1083.**
Discovery of the 2 $M_\odot$ neutron star PSR J1614-2230 using the Shapiro delay in the pulsar timing. Ruled out all soft nuclear equations of state.
