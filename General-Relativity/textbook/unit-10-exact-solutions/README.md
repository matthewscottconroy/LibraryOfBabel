# Unit X: Exact Solutions of the Einstein Equations

---

## Unit Introduction

The Einstein field equations are ten coupled nonlinear PDEs. In full generality, they are intractable. But with symmetry assumptions — spherical symmetry, axial symmetry, time translation symmetry — exact analytical solutions can be found.

The exact solutions of GR are not merely mathematical curiosities. They describe the geometry of real astrophysical objects and are the laboratory in which GR's predictions can be tested against observation.

**Schwarzschild (1916)**: The unique spherically symmetric vacuum solution. Describes the geometry outside any spherically symmetric mass distribution: a static star, a spherically symmetric black hole. Its key features: the Schwarzschild radius rₛ = 2GM/c² where the metric coefficient changes sign; geodesic motion predicting the perihelion precession of Mercury (43''/century, confirmed); light deflection near the Sun (1.75'', confirmed by the 1919 eclipse expedition of Eddington); gravitational redshift (confirmed by Pound-Rebka 1959); and the event horizon of the Schwarzschild black hole.

**Kerr (1963)**: The unique stationary axisymmetric vacuum solution, describing a rotating black hole. Contains frame-dragging — the dragging of inertial frames by the rotation of the source. Has an ergosphere (region where no observer can remain stationary) in addition to the event horizon. The Penrose process extracts energy from the ergosphere. Astrophysical black holes are believed to be Kerr black holes.

**Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker (FLRW)**: The exact solution for a homogeneous, isotropic universe. Derived from the cosmological principle. Describes an expanding (or contracting) universe; predicts the Big Bang singularity; the basis of modern cosmology. Contains three cases: flat (k=0), positively curved (k=+1), negatively curved (k=−1) spatial geometry.

**Reissner-Nordström**: Charged non-rotating black hole. **Kerr-Newman**: Charged rotating black hole (the most general stationary black hole, by the no-hair theorem). **de Sitter/Anti-de Sitter**: Vacuum solutions with positive/negative cosmological constant. AdS space is the arena of the AdS/CFT correspondence.
