The deepest surprise in this entire unit is the dictionary between algebra and topology: that loops in a space form a group, that the group structure is determined by the homotopy type of the space, and that this correspondence is not an analogy but a theorem — one that becomes a *definition* in Homotopy Type Theory. What algebraic topologists spend decades building as a bridge between two subjects, HoTT collapses into a single identification: the fundamental group of a type is its loop space, and the loop space *is* the type of identity proofs.

The entries in the correspondence table are worth reading slowly. A group element corresponds to a loop; multiplication corresponds to path concatenation; the identity element corresponds to the constant loop; inverses correspond to reversed paths. These parallels hold because the group axioms hold for homotopy classes of paths — not on the nose, but up to homotopy. Path concatenation is associative up to a homotopy, and that homotopy is itself a path of paths with its own algebraic structure. This "up to homotopy" is precisely what drives the HoTT program: instead of quotienting out the homotopies, we track them. Associativity is not an equation; it is a *witness*. The witness has coherences. The coherences have coherences. The resulting infinite tower of structure is exactly what higher category theory and HoTT are designed to manage.

You are looking here at the vocabulary you will need for everything that follows. Free groups appear as fundamental groups of graphs. Group presentations appear as CW complexes. Quotient groups appear as covering spaces. Each algebraic concept will resurface, translated, in the language of types and identity proofs.
