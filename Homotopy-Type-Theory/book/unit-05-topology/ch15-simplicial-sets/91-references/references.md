# References: Simplicial Sets

## Primary Texts on Simplicial Sets

**Goerss, Paul G., and John F. Jardine.** *Simplicial Homotopy Theory.* Birkhäuser, 1999.
The comprehensive modern reference on simplicial homotopy theory. Covers simplicial sets, Kan complexes, model categories, simplicial groups, and the homotopy theory of diagrams. Dense but thorough; essentially every theorem cited in this chapter appears here with full proofs.

**May, J. Peter.** *Simplicial Objects in Algebraic Topology.* University of Chicago Press, 1967; reprinted 1992.
The classic introductory text. Covers simplicial sets, Kan complexes, and the geometric realization adjunction. Clear and concise; an excellent starting point before moving to Goerss-Jardine.

**Friedman, Greg.** "An Elementary Illustrated Introduction to Simplicial Sets." *Rocky Mountain Journal of Mathematics* 42, no. 2 (2012): 353–423. Available at arXiv:0809.4221.
An accessible, illustrated introduction to simplicial sets and Kan complexes. Strongly recommended as a first reading before the more technical texts.

**Bergner, Julia E.** "A Survey of $(\infty,1)$-Categories." In *Towards Higher Categories*, ed. John C. Baez and J. Peter May. Springer, 2010.
A survey of the different models for $(\infty,1)$-categories, including quasi-categories (Joyal), Kan complexes (simplicial sets), and Segal categories. Essential for understanding the broader context of Kan complexes.

## For the Model Structure

**Quillen, Daniel G.** *Homotopical Algebra.* Springer Lecture Notes in Mathematics 43, 1967.
The founding text. Part I develops the abstract theory of model categories; Part II proves the model structure on simplicial sets and the Quillen equivalence with topological spaces.

**Hovey, Mark.** *Model Categories.* American Mathematical Society, 1999.
The modern comprehensive reference on model categories, with careful attention to the simplicial set model structure. Includes the monoidal model structure and the machinery needed for stable homotopy theory.

**Dwyer, W.G., P.S. Hirschhorn, D.M. Kan, and J.H. Smith.** *Homotopy Limit Functors on Model Categories and Homotopical Categories.* American Mathematical Society, 2004.
The definitive reference on homotopy limit and colimit constructions in model categories.

## For Higher Category Theory

**Lurie, Jacob.** *Higher Topos Theory.* Annals of Mathematics Studies 170. Princeton University Press, 2009. Available at arXiv:math/0608040.
The foundational text on $(\infty,1)$-categories and $(\infty,1)$-toposes. Chapter 1 covers quasi-categories; later chapters develop the theory of $(\infty,1)$-toposes and the object classifier (which is the $\infty$-categorical version of univalence).

**Lurie, Jacob.** *Higher Algebra.* 2017. Available at math.ias.edu/~lurie.
The companion to HTT, covering algebra in the $\infty$-categorical setting: $A_\infty$ rings, $E_\infty$ rings, spectra, and the foundations of derived algebraic geometry.

**Joyal, André.** "Quasi-categories and Kan Complexes." *Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra* 175 (2002): 207–222.
The foundational paper on quasi-categories as models for $(\infty,1)$-categories. Joyal's approach to the horn-filling condition for inner horns is the basis for the quasi-category model.

## For the HoTT Model

**The HoTT Book.** *Homotopy Type Theory: Univalent Foundations of Mathematics.* IAS, 2013. Available at homotopytypetheory.org.
Chapter 2 develops the type theory whose consistency the simplicial model establishes. Appendix A covers formal type theory.

**Kapulkin, Chris, and Peter LeFanu Lumsdaine.** "The Simplicial Model of Univalent Foundations (after Voevodsky)." *Journal of the European Mathematical Society* 23, no. 6 (2021): 2071–2126. arXiv:1211.2851.
The definitive written account of the Voevodsky simplicial set model. Proves that the model satisfies all the axioms of HoTT including univalence. Essential reading for anyone who wants to understand the model in detail.

**Shulman, Michael.** "Univalence for Inverse Diagrams and Homotopy Canonicity." *Mathematical Structures in Computer Science* 25, no. 5 (2015): 1203–1277.
Develops the model structure for diagrams of simplicial sets and proves univalence for these models. Shows how the simplicial set model generalizes.

## For Cubical Sets and Computational HoTT

**Cohen, Cyril, Thierry Coquand, Simon Huber, and Anders Mörtberg.** "Cubical Type Theory: A Constructive Interpretation of the Univalence Axiom." In *21st International Conference on Types for Proofs and Programs (TYPES 2015)*, 2015. arXiv:1611.02108.
The foundational paper on cubical type theory. Shows how to give univalence a computational interpretation using cubes rather than simplices.

**Angiuli, Carlo, et al.** "Cartesian Cubical Type Theory." In *Proceedings of the 34th Annual ACM/IEEE Symposium on Logic in Computer Science (LICS 2019)*.
A cleaner formulation of cubical type theory that has been implemented in the proof assistant `redtt`.

## Historical Sources

**Eilenberg, Samuel, and J.A. Zilber.** "Semi-simplicial complexes and singular homology." *Annals of Mathematics* 51, no. 3 (1950): 499–513.
The paper introducing simplicial sets (in the form of "semi-simplicial complexes") and degeneracy maps.

**Kan, Daniel M.** "Abstract Homotopy I." *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* 41 (1955): 1092–1096; "Abstract Homotopy II." *ibid.* 42 (1956): 255–258.
The papers introducing Kan complexes and the horn-filling condition.

**Grothendieck, Alexander.** "Pursuing Stacks." 1983. Published in *Documents Mathématiques* 20, Société Mathématique de France, 2022.
Grothendieck's letter to Quillen outlining the homotopy hypothesis and the program of higher category theory. Prophetic and profound; written in an informal style that makes it accessible despite its technical ambitions.
