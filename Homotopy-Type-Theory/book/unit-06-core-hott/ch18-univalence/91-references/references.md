# References: Univalence

## Primary Sources

**The Univalent Foundations Program.** (2013). *Homotopy Type Theory: Univalent Foundations of Mathematics*. Chapters 2.10 (paths between types), 4 (equivalences), and 2.10 (univalence axiom). The definitive treatment.

**Voevodsky, V.** (2014). "The origins and motivations of univalent foundations." *The IAS Letter*, Spring 2014. Voevodsky's own account of how and why he developed the Univalence Axiom.

**Voevodsky, V.** (2015). "An experimental library of formalized mathematics based on the univalent foundations." *Mathematical Structures in Computer Science* 25(5): 1278–1294. The Voevodsky Mathematical Library, implementing HoTT in Coq.

## On Equivalences

**Lumsdaine, P. L.** (2010). "Weak omega-categories from intensional type theory." *Logical Methods in Computer Science* 6(3). Early work on the connection between identity types and weak omega-groupoids.

**Joyal, A.** (2014). "Remarks on homotopical logic." In *Epistemology versus Ontology*, Springer. Philosophical and categorical treatment of equivalences in HoTT.

## On the Simplicial Set Model

**Kapulkin, C. and Lumsdaine, P. L.** (2012). "The simplicial model of univalent foundations." arxiv.org/abs/1211.2851. The rigorous proof that Univalence holds in the simplicial set model.

**Shulman, M.** (2015). "Univalence for inverse diagrams and homotopy canonicity." *Mathematical Structures in Computer Science* 25(5): 1203–1277. Extends the simplicial model to show that a wide class of models satisfies Univalence.

## On Cubical Type Theory

**Cohen, C., Coquand, T., Huber, S., and Mortberg, A.** (2016). "Cubical Type Theory: A constructive interpretation of the univalence axiom." In *21st International Conference on Types for Proofs and Programs (TYPES 2015)*. The original paper on cubical type theory where Univalence is proved, not axiomatized.

**Angiuli, C., Brunerie, G., Coquand, T., Favonia, K., Harper, R., and Licata, D.** (2019). "Cartesian cubical computational type theory: Constructive reasoning with paths and equalities." In *Computer Science Logic 2019*. Advanced treatment of computational content of univalence.

## Philosophical and Mathematical Background

**Awodey, S.** (2014). "Structuralism, invariance, and univalence." *Philosophia Mathematica* 22(1): 1–11. The philosophical case that Univalence captures the mathematical principle of structural invariance.

**Makkai, M.** (1995). "First Order Logic with Dependent Sorts." Available at math.mcgill.ca/makkai. Makkai's "FOLDS" system, a precursor to univalent foundations that tries to make mathematics invariant under isomorphism.
