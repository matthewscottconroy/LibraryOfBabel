# References: Higher Inductive Types

## Primary Sources

**The Univalent Foundations Program.** (2013). *Homotopy Type Theory: Univalent Foundations of Mathematics*. Chapter 6 (Higher Inductive Types) is the canonical reference, covering the interval, circle, spheres, suspensions, pushouts, and truncations with full detail and proofs.

**Lumsdaine, P. L. and Shulman, M.** (2017). "Semantics of Higher Inductive Types." *Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society*. Available at arxiv.org/abs/1705.07088. The rigorous semantic foundation for HITs.

## On Specific HITs

**Licata, D. R. and Shulman, M.** (2013). "Calculating the Fundamental Group of the Circle in Homotopy Type Theory." *Proceedings of LICS 2013*. The original formal proof of π₁(S^1) = Z in HoTT, using the circle HIT.

**Brunerie, G.** (2016). *On the Homotopy Groups of Spheres in Homotopy Type Theory.* PhD thesis. The computation of π₄(S³) = Z/2Z, the most advanced application of HITs to date.

**Licata, D. R. and Finster, E.** (2014). "Eilenberg-MacLane Spaces in Homotopy Type Theory." *Proceedings of LICS 2014*. Construction of K(G,n) as HITs and their use in cohomology.

## On Pushouts and van Kampen

**Favonia (Kuen-Bang Hou) and Shulman, M.** (2016). "The Seifert-van Kampen Theorem in Homotopy Type Theory." *Proceedings of CSL 2016*. Proof of van Kampen using pushouts and their universal property.

## On Suspensions and Freudenthal

**Lumsdaine, P. L. and Shulman, M.** (2017). "Proof of the Freudenthal Suspension Theorem." In preparation/preprint. The synthetic HoTT proof of the Freudenthal theorem.

**Anel, M., Biedermann, G., Finster, E., and Joyal, A.** (2017). "Goodwillie's Calculus of Functors and Higher Topos Theory." Available at arxiv.org/abs/1703.09632. The Blakers-Massey theorem in HoTT.

## On Cubical Computation for HITs

**Cohen, C., Coquand, T., Huber, S., and Mortberg, A.** (2016). "Cubical Type Theory: A constructive interpretation of the univalence axiom." Contains the first computational treatment of HITs in cubical type theory.

**Vezzosi, A., Mortberg, A., and Abel, A.** (2019). "Cubical Agda: A Dependently Typed Programming Language with Univalence and Higher Inductive Types." *Proceedings of ICFP 2019*. Implementation of HITs in Cubical Agda.

## For Further Reading

**Buchholtz, U. and Rijke, E.** (2017). "The real projective spaces in homotopy type theory." *Proceedings of LICS 2017*. Construction and analysis of RP^n as a HIT.

**Cavallo, E. and Harper, R.** (2019). "Higher inductive types in cubical computational type theory." *Proceedings of POPL 2019*. Computational treatment of HITs with definitional computation rules.
