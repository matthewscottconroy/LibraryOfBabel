# References — Chapter 24: Simplicial Type Theory

## Primary Sources

**Riehl, E., & Shulman, M. (2017).** A Type Theory for Synthetic ∞-Categories. *Higher Structures*, 1(1), 147–224. — The foundational paper. Introduces STT, extension types, the directed interval, Segal and Rezk types, and proves the synthetic Yoneda lemma. Required reading.

**Bardomiano Martínez, C. (2022).** Limits and colimits in a category formed in a pasting diagram. arXiv:2202.12386. — Limits in synthetic ∞-category theory.

**Riehl, E., & Verity, D. (2022).** *Elements of ∞-Category Theory*. Cambridge University Press. — The comprehensive reference for classical ∞-category theory (in the model of ∞-cosmoi), complementary to the synthetic approach.

## The Rezk and Segal Conditions

**Rezk, C. (2001).** A Model for the Homotopy Theory of Homotopy Theory. *Transactions of the AMS*, 353(3), 973–1007. — Introduces complete Segal spaces (the classical version of Rezk types) and proves their model structure.

**Segal, G. (1974).** Categories and Cohomology Theories. *Topology*, 13(3), 293–312. — The original paper introducing the Segal condition for simplicial spaces.

**Bergner, J. E. (2007).** A Survey of (∞, 1)-Categories. — A comprehensive survey of the different models of ∞-categories (quasi-categories, complete Segal spaces, etc.) and their relationships.

## ∞-Category Theory (Classical)

**Lurie, J. (2009).** *Higher Topos Theory*. Princeton University Press. (Also available at math.harvard.edu/~lurie/) — The comprehensive classical reference. Essential for understanding what the synthetic approach is trying to capture.

**Joyal, A. (2002).** Quasi-categories and Kan complexes. *Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra*, 175(1-3), 207–222. — Joyal's original development of quasi-categories.

**Lurie, J. (2017).** *Higher Algebra*. Available at math.harvard.edu/~lurie/ — The second volume, covering stable ∞-categories, ∞-operads, and derived algebra.

## Two-Level Type Theory

**Altenkirch, T., Capriotti, P., Kraus, N., & Sattler, C. (2016).** Extending Homotopy Type Theory with Strict Equality. *CSL 2016.* — Introduces 2LTT, the framework underlying the outer/inner level distinction in STT.

**Annenkov, D., Capriotti, P., Kraus, N., & Sattler, C. (2023).** Two-Level Type Theory and Applications. *Mathematical Structures in Computer Science.* — A comprehensive treatment of 2LTT with applications.

**Capriotti, P., & Kraus, N. (2017).** Univalent Higher Categories via Complete Semi-Segal Types. *POPL 2018.* — Complete Segal types in 2LTT.

## Extension Types and Fibrations

**Shulman, M. (2015).** Univalence for Inverse Diagrams and Homotopy Canonicity. *Mathematical Structures in Computer Science*, 25(5), 1203–1277. — Extension types in the context of inverse diagrams.

**Licata, D. R., & Finster, E. (2014).** Eilenberg-MacLane Spaces in Homotopy Type Theory. *LICS 2014.* — Uses fibrations in HoTT, relevant to the covariant fibration notion in STT.

## Implementation: Rzk

**Kuhn, N., et al. The Rzk Proof Assistant** — `github.com/rzk-lang/rzk` — The primary implementation of simplicial type theory.

**Riehl, E. (2022).** ∞-Category Theory for Undergraduates. *Proceedings of the ICM 2022*, Vol. 1, 1041–1056. — An accessible introduction to the synthetic program by the co-founder.

**Buchholtz, U., & Weinberger, J. (2023).** Synthetic Fibered (∞, 1)-Category Theory. *Higher Structures*, 7(1). — Fibration theory (covariant and contravariant fibrations) in STT.

**Weinberger, J. (2022).** A Synthetic Perspective on (∞, 1)-Category Theory: Fibrational and Semantic Aspects. PhD Thesis, TU Darmstadt. — Comprehensive treatment of fibrations and semantics in STT.

## The Yoneda Lemma

**MacLane, S. (1998).** *Categories for the Working Mathematician* (2nd ed.). Springer. — The classical source for the Yoneda lemma, for comparison with the synthetic version.

**Loregian, F. (2021).** *(Co)end Calculus*. Cambridge University Press. — Advanced treatment of (co)ends and the Yoneda lemma, useful for understanding the synthetic version.

## Historical and Philosophical Context

**Grothendieck, A. (1983).** *Pursuing Stacks*. — The visionary document in which Grothendieck proposed that homotopy theory and category theory should be unified. The simplicial type theory program is one realization of this vision.

**Boardman, J. M., & Vogt, R. M. (1973).** *Homotopy Invariant Algebraic Structures on Topological Spaces*. Springer. — The original source of quasi-categories (called "weak Kan complexes" there).

**Street, R. (1987).** The algebra of oriented simplexes. *Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra*, 49(3), 283–335. — An early investigation of directed higher-dimensional structures, a precursor to ∞-category theory.
