# References and Primary Sources: What Is Ontology?

## Foundational Texts

Aristotle. *Metaphysics*. Trans. W.D. Ross. Oxford: Clarendon Press. The locus classicus for the study of "being qua being"; Books IV and VII are essential for understanding what ontology investigates.

Quine, W.V.O. "On What There Is." *Review of Metaphysics* 2 (1948): 21-38. Reframed the basic question of ontology in terms of existential quantification; one of the most cited papers in twentieth-century philosophy.

Heidegger, Martin. *Being and Time*. Trans. Macquarrie and Robinson. Harper & Row, 1962. The most influential phenomenological attempt to reopen the question of Being; raises the ontological difference between Being and beings.

## Contemporary Works

van Inwagen, Peter and Sullivan, Meghan. "Metaphysics." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2023. A careful survey of what metaphysics and ontology are, their methods, and main questions.

Lowe, E.J. *The Possibility of Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 1998. Argues that ontology is a legitimate, substantive discipline concerned with the general structure of reality.

Sider, Theodore. *Writing the Book of the World*. Oxford University Press, 2011. Defends a neo-Quinean vision of ontology oriented around the concept of structure in nature.
