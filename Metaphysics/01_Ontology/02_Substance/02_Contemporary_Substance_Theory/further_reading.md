# Further Reading and Primary Sources — Contemporary Substance Theory

*Essential texts for contemporary debates about substance, persistence, and identity.*

---

## Primary Sources and Key Articles

Hume, David. *A Treatise of Human Nature*, Book I, Part 4, Sections 3, 5, 6. — On substance, personal identity, and the bundle view.

Williams, D. C. "The Elements of Being." *Review of Metaphysics* 7 (1953): 3–18, 171–192. — The founding document of contemporary trope theory.

Armstrong, D. M. *A World of States of Affairs*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1997.

Fine, Kit. "Essence and Modality." *Philosophical Perspectives* 8 (1994): 1–16. — Seminal critique of modal analyses of essence.

Parfit, Derek. *Reasons and Persons*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1984. — Part III on personal identity. Essential.

Sider, Theodore. *Four-Dimensionalism: An Ontology of Persistence and Time*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2001. — The most thorough defense of perdurantism.

## Monographs

Campbell, Keith. *Abstract Particulars*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1990.

Lowe, E. J. *The Four-Category Ontology*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2006.

Olson, Eric T. *The Human Animal: Personal Identity Without Psychology*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1997. — Animalist theory of personal identity.

## Collections

Haslanger, Sally, and Roxanne Marie Kurtz, eds. *Persistence: Contemporary Readings*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2006. — Collects the key papers on endurantism and perdurantism.

Sider, Theodore, John Hawthorne, and Dean Zimmerman, eds. *Contemporary Debates in Metaphysics*. Oxford: Blackwell, 2008. — Chapters on substance, persistence, and identity.
