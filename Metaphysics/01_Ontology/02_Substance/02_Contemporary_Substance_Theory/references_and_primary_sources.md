# References and Primary Sources: Contemporary Substance Theory

## Foundational Texts

Armstrong, D.M. *A World of States of Affairs*. Cambridge University Press, 1997. Develops a neo-Aristotelian ontology in which thick particulars (substance + properties) are the basic constituents, embedded in states of affairs.

Lowe, E.J. *The Possibility of Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 1998. Argues for a four-category ontology that makes substances and their kinds ontologically fundamental.

## Contemporary Works

Simons, Peter. "Particulars in Particular Clothing: Three Trope Theories of Substance." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 54 (1994): 553-575. A systematic analysis of substance as a bundle of tropes, distinguishing nuclear-dependent and free-floating trope theories.

Hawthorne, John and Sider, Theodore. "Locations." *Philosophical Topics* 30 (2002): 53-76. Discusses the relation between substances and their spatial locations.

Koslicki, Kathlin. *The Structure of Objects*. Oxford University Press, 2008. A neo-Aristotelian account of material substances as hylomorphic compounds with both material and formal constituents.

Tahko, Tuomas (ed.). *Contemporary Aristotelian Metaphysics*. Cambridge University Press, 2012. A collection of essays on neo-Aristotelian approaches to substance, properties, and essence.
