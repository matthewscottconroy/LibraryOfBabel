# Further Reading and Primary Sources — Special Substances

*Essential texts on persons, artifacts, natural kinds, and social kinds.*

---

## Personal Identity

Locke, John. *An Essay Concerning Human Understanding*, Book II, Ch. 27. — The founding text of psychological continuity theories.

Parfit, Derek. *Reasons and Persons*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1984. — Part III. Essential.

Olson, Eric T. *What Are We? A Study in Personal Ontology*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.

Schechtman, Marya. *The Constitution of Selves*. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1996. — Narrative identity theory.

## Natural Kinds and Essentialism

Kripke, Saul. *Naming and Necessity*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1980. — Lectures II and III on natural kind terms.

Putnam, Hilary. "The Meaning of 'Meaning.'" In *Mind, Language and Reality*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1975.

Dupré, John. *The Disorder of Things: Metaphysical Foundations of the Disunity of Science*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1993. — Anti-essentialist.

## Social Ontology

Searle, John. *The Construction of Social Reality*. New York: Free Press, 1995.

Haslanger, Sally. *Resisting Reality: Social Construction and Social Critique*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2012.

## Artifacts

Thomasson, Amie. *Ordinary Objects*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.

van Inwagen, Peter. *Material Beings*. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1990. — Part III for the near-eliminativist view of artifacts.
