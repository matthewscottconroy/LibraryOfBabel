# Further Reading: The Nature of Properties

Armstrong, D.M. *Universals and Scientific Realism*, 2 vols. Cambridge University Press, 1978. The canonical contemporary defense of universals.

Armstrong, D.M. *A Theory of Universals*. Cambridge University Press, 1978. Companion volume analyzing predication and instantiation.

Mellor, D.H. and Oliver, Alex (eds.). *Properties*. Oxford University Press, 1997. A well-curated anthology spanning the debate.

Shoemaker, Sydney. "Causality and Properties." In Peter van Inwagen (ed.), *Time and Cause*. Reidel, 1980. The original causal-role identity argument.

Ellis, Brian. *Scientific Essentialism*. Cambridge University Press, 2001. Full presentation of the essentialist program for properties.

Molnar, George. *Powers: A Study in Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2003. Posthumously published defense of irreducible dispositional properties.
