# References and Primary Sources: The Nature of Properties

## Foundational Texts

Armstrong, D.M. *Universals and Scientific Realism*, 2 vols. Cambridge University Press, 1978. The canonical contemporary defense of universals as the ontological basis for properties.

Williams, D.C. "On the Elements of Being." *Review of Metaphysics* 7 (1953): 3-18. The founding paper of modern trope theory; argues that abstract particulars are the fundamental elements of reality.

## Contemporary Works

Mellor, D.H. and Oliver, Alex (eds.). *Properties*. Oxford University Press, 1997. An essential anthology covering universals, tropes, nominalism, and the role of properties in laws and causation.

Ellis, Brian. *Scientific Essentialism*. Cambridge University Press, 2001. Argues that natural properties have their causal roles essentially, making scientific laws metaphysically necessary.

Molnar, George. *Powers: A Study in Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2003. Posthumously published defense of irreducibly dispositional properties as the fundamental constituents of reality.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343-377. Defends natural properties as a primitive, theoretically indispensable notion.
