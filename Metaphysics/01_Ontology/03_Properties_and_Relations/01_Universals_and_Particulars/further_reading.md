# Further Reading and Primary Sources — Universals and Particulars

*Essential texts on the problem of universals.*

---

## Primary Sources

Plato. *Phaedo*, *Republic* (Books V–VII), *Parmenides*. — Foundational texts for Platonic realism about forms.

Aristotle. *Metaphysics*, Books I, VII, XIII. — Aristotle's immanent realism and critique of Platonic forms.

Russell, Bertrand. *Problems of Philosophy*, Ch. 9–10. — A clear and accessible defense of universals.

Armstrong, D. M. *Universals and Scientific Realism*, 2 vols. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1978.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343–377. — Lewis's influential use of *naturalness* to bypass the universals debate.

Rodriguez-Pereyra, Gonzalo. *Resemblance Nominalism: A Solution to the Problem of Universals*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2002.

## Secondary Literature

Loux, Michael J. *Metaphysics: A Contemporary Introduction*, 3rd ed. New York: Routledge, 2006. — Chapters on universals and nominalism.

MacBride, Fraser. "Universals." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2024 edition). — Comprehensive survey.

Oliver, Alex. "The Metaphysics of Properties." *Mind* 105 (1996): 1–80. — Dense but rewarding survey of the options.
