# Exercises and Thought Experiments — Properties

*Problems for engaging with the metaphysics of properties, intrinsicality, and dispositions.*

---

**Exercise 1 — Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic**
Classify the following as intrinsic or extrinsic and explain your reasoning: (a) having a mass of 5 kg; (b) being the heaviest object in the room; (c) being spherical; (d) being admired by philosophers; (e) having a negative charge; (f) being a sibling. Are there any cases where you are uncertain, and if so, why?

**Exercise 2 — Relations and Bradley's Regress**
Consider the relation *is taller than* holding between Alice and Bob. Bradley argues that any relation requires a further relating relation, generating a regress. Outline the regress argument carefully. Then present and evaluate the most convincing response. Does the response dissolve the problem or merely relocate it?

**Exercise 3 — Essential Properties**
Fine argues that being a member of {Socrates} is a necessary property of Socrates (necessarily, if Socrates exists, {Socrates} exists) but not an essential property (it does not reveal Socrates's nature). Do you find this distinction compelling? Construct two more examples: one where a necessary property seems non-essential, and one where an essential property seems to be contingent (or at least where the essentiality is not obvious from necessity).

**Exercise 4 — Finkish Dispositions**
Describe Martin's finkish disposition counterexample to the conditional analysis of dispositions. Then try to repair the conditional analysis to handle finkish cases. (Hint: consider whether adding more clauses to the conditional helps.) Does any purely conditional analysis work, or is irreducible power-talk unavoidable?

**Thought Experiment — Powered Universe**
Imagine a universe that contains only one kind of fundamental property: charge. Charge is essentially the power to attract opposite charges and repel like charges. If all properties are pure powers defined by their relations to other powers, does such a universe have enough categorical anchoring to be determinate? What would it mean for charge to have its actual causal role in such a universe rather than some different role?
