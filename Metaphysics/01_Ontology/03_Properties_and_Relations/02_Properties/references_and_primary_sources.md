# References and Primary Sources: Properties

## Foundational Texts

Armstrong, D.M. *A Theory of Universals*. Cambridge University Press, 1978. Vol. 2 of *Universals and Scientific Realism*; covers predication, universals as constituents of states of affairs, and the problem of higher-order properties.

Shoemaker, Sydney. "Causality and Properties." In van Inwagen (ed.), *Time and Cause*. Reidel, 1980. The original causal-role identity argument: properties are identical iff they confer identical causal powers.

## Contemporary Works

Oliver, Alex. "The Metaphysics of Properties." *Mind* 105 (1996): 1-80. A comprehensive survey of property theories — universals, tropes, and nominalism — assessing the merits of each.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343-377. Introduces the distinction between natural (sparse) and abundant properties and argues for the theoretical indispensability of natural properties.

Hawthorne, John. "Causal Structuralism." *Philosophical Perspectives* 15 (2001): 361-378. Develops a causal structuralist account of properties and assesses its consequences for modality and laws.

Bealer, George. *Quality and Concept*. Oxford University Press, 1982. Defends an intensional theory of properties as primitive abstract entities required by logic and semantics.
