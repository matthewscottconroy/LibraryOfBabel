# Exercises: Relations

1. Explain Bradley's regress. Does it prove that relations are unreal, or does it prove something else?

2. Distinguish internal from external relations. Give one example of each and explain why the distinction matters for metaphysics.

3. Russell argued that mathematics requires external relations. What is his argument, and does it succeed?

4. Ontic structural realism holds that objects are derivative on relations. Is this view coherent? What would it mean for an object to be "derivative"?

5. Some relations are non-symmetric (like "being taller than"). How do we represent the directionality of such relations in a formal metaphysics?

6. Is the relation of parthood — x is a part of y — intrinsic to the relata, or does it depend on further facts? What difference does your answer make to mereology?
