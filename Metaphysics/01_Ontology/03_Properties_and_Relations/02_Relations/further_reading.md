# Further Reading: Relations

Russell, Bertrand. "The Nature of Truth." In *Philosophical Essays*. Longmans, Green, 1910. Early argument for irreducible external relations.

Bradley, F.H. *Appearance and Reality*. Swan Sonnenschein, 1893. The idealist regress argument against relations.

Fine, Kit. "Neutral Relations." *Philosophical Review* 109 (2000): 1-33. Careful analysis of the metaphysics of relational structure.

Ladyman, James and Ross, Don. *Every Thing Must Go*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Defends ontic structural realism.

MacBride, Fraser. "Relations." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2016. Comprehensive overview of all major positions.
