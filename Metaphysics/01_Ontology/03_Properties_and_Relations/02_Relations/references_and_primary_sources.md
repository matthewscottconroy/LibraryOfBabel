# References and Primary Sources: Relations

## Foundational Texts

Russell, Bertrand. *The Principles of Mathematics*. Norton, 1903. Chapter 26 argues for the irreducibility of relations against the idealist view that all relations are internal.

Bradley, F.H. *Appearance and Reality*. Swan Sonnenschein, 1893. Chapter 2 develops the regress argument against the reality of relations.

## Contemporary Works

Fine, Kit. "Neutral Relations." *Philosophical Review* 109 (2000): 1-33. A rigorous analysis of relational facts, distinguishing positionalist from anti-positionalist accounts of asymmetric relations.

MacBride, Fraser. "Relations." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2023. The definitive contemporary survey of the metaphysics of relations.

Dorr, Cian. "There Are No Abstract Objects." In Sider, Hawthorne, and Zimmerman (eds.), *Contemporary Debates in Metaphysics*. Blackwell, 2008. Defends a sparse view of what relations exist.

Ladyman, James and Ross, Don. *Every Thing Must Go*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Argues for ontic structural realism: the view that relations rather than objects are the fundamental ontological category.
