# Exercises and Thought Experiments — Relations

*Problems for engaging with the metaphysics of relations, structure, and grounding.*

---

**Exercise 1 — Internal vs. External**
Classify the following relations as internal or external and defend your classification: (a) being more massive than; (b) being to the left of; (c) resembling; (d) causing; (e) being a sibling of; (f) being entangled with (quantum). Are there any cases where the internal/external distinction breaks down or becomes unclear?

**Exercise 2 — Bradley's Regress**
State Bradley's regress argument. Then consider two responses: (a) the regress is vicious and shows that relations are not real; (b) the regress is benign — it generates an infinity of truths about how A and B are related, but each is grounded in the original relation, so there is no explanatory circularity. Which response do you find more convincing, and why?

**Exercise 3 — Structural Realism**
Ontic structural realism says there are no individuals with intrinsic natures — only structure. An objector says: "Structures are abstract; they need to be *instantiated* by something concrete. Structure without relata is just mathematics, not physics." Assess this objection. Does ontic structural realism have a satisfying response?

**Exercise 4 — Grounding vs. Causation**
Explain the difference between grounding and causation using a specific example. (Hint: consider the relation between a mental state and its neural basis.) Can grounding be circular — can A ground B and B ground A? Can causation be circular in a non-question-begging sense? Does grounding require a well-founded terminus, or could there be infinite chains of grounding?

**Thought Experiment — A World of Pure Relations**
Imagine a world in which there are no individual objects, only a network of relations. Two "things" exist only insofar as they are nodes in the relational network; they have no intrinsic properties of their own. Is this world coherent? Could such a world be the actual world? What would experience of such a world be like, if anything?
