# Further Reading and Primary Sources — Relations

*Essential texts on the metaphysics of relations, structural realism, and grounding.*

---

Bradley, F. H. *Appearance and Reality*. London: Swan Sonnenschein, 1893. — Chapter 3 on relations.

Russell, Bertrand. *Problems of Philosophy*. London: Williams and Norgate, 1912. — Chapter 9.

Worrall, John. "Structural Realism: The Best of Both Worlds?" *Dialectica* 43 (1989): 99–124.

Ladyman, James, and Don Ross. *Every Thing Must Go: Metaphysics Naturalized*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.

Fine, Kit. "The Question of Realism." *Philosophers' Imprint* 1 (2001): 1–30.

Schaffer, Jonathan. "On What Grounds What." In Chalmers, Manley, and Wasserman (eds.), *Metametaphysics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2009.

Correia, Fabrice, and Benjamin Schnieder, eds. *Metaphysical Grounding: Understanding the Structure of Reality*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2012. — Essential collection.

Lowe, E. J. *The Possibility of Metaphysics: Substance, Identity, and Time*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1998. — Part III on dependence.
