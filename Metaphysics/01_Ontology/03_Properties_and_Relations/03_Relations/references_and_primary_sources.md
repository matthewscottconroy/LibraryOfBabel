# References and Primary Sources: Relations (II)

## Foundational Texts

Russell, Bertrand. *Our Knowledge of the External World*. Open Court, 1914. Develops the logical analysis of relations and their role in the structure of knowledge and reality.

McTaggart, J.M.E. *The Nature of Existence*, vol. 1. Cambridge University Press, 1921. Discusses the categories of existence and the role of internal relations in idealist ontology.

## Contemporary Works

Dorr, Cian and Rosen, Gideon. "Composition as a Fiction." In Gale (ed.), *Blackwell Guide to Metaphysics*. Blackwell, 2002. Examines how relations ground composite structures.

Sider, Theodore. *Four-Dimensionalism*. Oxford University Press, 2001. Employs a sophisticated account of temporal relations to analyze persistence and identity through time.

Hawley, Katherine. *How Things Persist*. Oxford University Press, 2001. Discusses the relations between objects across time.

Mumford, Stephen and Anjum, Rani Lill. *Getting Causes from Powers*. Oxford University Press, 2011. Analyzes causal relations in terms of the manifestation of dispositional properties.
