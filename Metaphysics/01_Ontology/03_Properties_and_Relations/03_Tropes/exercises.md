# Exercises: Tropes

1. Explain the core motivation for trope theory. What problems does positing particular properties (rather than universal ones) solve?

2. Trope theory faces the resemblance regress: to explain why two red tropes resemble, we posit a resemblance relation, which itself seems like a universal. How do trope theorists respond?

3. How does the trope bundle theory account for individual substances? What is the compresence relation that holds tropes together?

4. Compare substratum theory to the trope bundle theory as accounts of a single red ball. What does each view posit? Which faces harder objections?

5. D.C. Williams called tropes "abstract particulars." Is this terminology apt? In what sense are they abstract? In what sense are they particular?

6. Can trope theory account for structural or relational properties? Are there tropes for relations? What problems might arise?
