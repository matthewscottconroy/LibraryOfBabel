# Further Reading: Tropes

Williams, D.C. "On the Elements of Being." *Review of Metaphysics* 7 (1953): 3-18. The founding document of modern trope theory.

Campbell, Keith. *Abstract Particulars*. Blackwell, 1990. Full systematic development of trope ontology.

Simons, Peter. "Particulars in Particular Clothing: Three Trope Theories of Substance." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 54 (1994): 553-575.

Rodriguez-Pereyra, Gonzalo. *Resemblance Nominalism: A Solution to the Problem of Universals*. Oxford University Press, 2002.

Maurin, Anna-Sofia. *If Tropes*. Kluwer, 2002. Defense and analysis of trope ontology.

Heil, John. *The Universe as We Find It*. Oxford University Press, 2012. A trope-friendly ontology of qualities and powers.
