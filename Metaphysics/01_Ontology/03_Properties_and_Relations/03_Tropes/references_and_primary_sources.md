# References and Primary Sources: Tropes

## Foundational Texts

Williams, D.C. "On the Elements of Being." *Review of Metaphysics* 7 (1953): 3-18. The founding paper of modern trope theory; introduces tropes as the fundamental elements of being.

Campbell, Keith. *Abstract Particulars*. Blackwell, 1990. The most systematic book-length defense of trope ontology.

## Contemporary Works

Simons, Peter. "Particulars in Particular Clothing." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 54 (1994): 553-575. Three trope theories of substance, distinguished by how they bundle tropes into individuals.

Maurin, Anna-Sofia. *If Tropes*. Kluwer, 2002. A careful foundational study of the logic and ontology of trope theory.

Rodriguez-Pereyra, Gonzalo. *Resemblance Nominalism*. Oxford University Press, 2002. Develops a trope-based resemblance nominalism.

Heil, John. *The Universe as We Find It*. Oxford University Press, 2012. Defends a powerful qualities ontology using trope-like entities; argues that every property is both a quality and a power.
