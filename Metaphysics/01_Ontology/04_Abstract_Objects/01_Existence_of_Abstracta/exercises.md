# Exercises: Existence of Abstracta

1. What criteria distinguish abstract from concrete objects? Is the distinction exhaustive and mutually exclusive?

2. Frege argued that numbers are neither mental nor physical. What is his argument, and do you find it convincing?

3. Explain the indispensability argument for mathematical Platonism. What are its premises, and which is most vulnerable to attack?

4. Benacerraf's epistemological challenge: if mathematical objects are causally inert, how can we know anything about them? Evaluate two responses to this challenge.

5. Hartry Field claims that mathematics is useful but not true. What does he mean? Does this position require a revisionary account of mathematical practice?

6. Can a nominalist account for the applicability of mathematics to science without positing abstract objects? What strategies are available?
