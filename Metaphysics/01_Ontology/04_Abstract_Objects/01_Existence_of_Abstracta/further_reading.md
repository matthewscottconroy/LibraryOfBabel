# Further Reading: Existence of Abstracta

Frege, Gottlob. *The Foundations of Arithmetic*. Blackwell, 1884/1950. The classic argument that numbers are objective, non-mental abstract objects.

Benacerraf, Paul. "What Numbers Could Not Be." *Philosophical Review* 74 (1965): 47-73. Argues that numbers cannot be identified with any particular sets.

Benacerraf, Paul. "Mathematical Truth." *Journal of Philosophy* 70 (1973): 661-679. The epistemological challenge for Platonism.

Field, Hartry. *Science Without Numbers*. Princeton University Press, 1980. The most influential nominalist program.

Balaguer, Mark. *Platonism and Anti-Platonism in Mathematics*. Oxford University Press, 1998. Comprehensive survey of both sides.

Zalta, Edward. *Abstract Objects: An Introduction to Axiomatic Metaphysics*. Reidel, 1983. A rigorous formal theory of abstract objects.
