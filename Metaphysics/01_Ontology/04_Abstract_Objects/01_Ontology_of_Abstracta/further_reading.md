# Further Reading and Primary Sources — Ontology of Abstracta

*Essential texts on abstract objects, mathematical ontology, and the abstract/concrete distinction.*

---

## Primary Sources

Frege, Gottlob. *The Foundations of Arithmetic* (1884). Translated by J. L. Austin. Oxford: Blackwell, 1959.

Benacerraf, Paul. "What Numbers Could Not Be." *Philosophical Review* 74 (1965): 47–73.

Benacerraf, Paul. "Mathematical Truth." *Journal of Philosophy* 70 (1973): 661–679.

Quine, W. V. O. "On What There Is." In *From a Logical Point of View*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1953.

Field, Hartry. *Science Without Numbers: A Defence of Nominalism*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1980.

Shapiro, Stewart. *Philosophy of Mathematics: Structure and Ontology*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1997.

## Collections and Surveys

Balaguer, Mark. *Platonism and Anti-Platonism in Mathematics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1998.

Burgess, John, and Gideon Rosen. *A Subject With No Object: Strategies for Nominalistic Interpretation of Mathematics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1997.

Leng, Mary. *Mathematics and Reality*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2010. — Fictionalism defended.

Rosen, Gideon. "Abstract Objects." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2024 edition). — Authoritative survey.
