# References and Primary Sources: Ontology of Abstracta

## Foundational Texts

Frege, Gottlob. "The Thought: A Logical Inquiry." *Beitrage zur Philosophie des Deutschen Idealismus* 2 (1918): 58-77. Argues that thoughts (propositions) are abstract objects — objective, mind-independent, non-spatial.

Quine, W.V.O. "Ontological Relativity." In *Ontological Relativity and Other Essays*. Columbia University Press, 1969. Examines the conditions for ontological commitment to abstract entities.

## Contemporary Works

Zalta, Edward. *Abstract Objects: An Introduction to Axiomatic Metaphysics*. Reidel, 1983. Develops a formal axiomatic theory distinguishing encoding from exemplifying properties for abstract objects.

Lowe, E.J. "The Metaphysics of Abstract Objects." *Journal of Philosophy* 92 (1995): 509-524. Examines the ontological categories to which abstract objects belong.

Thomasson, Amie. *Fiction and Metaphysics*. Cambridge University Press, 1999. A detailed account of fictional characters as abstract artifacts — culturally created abstract objects.

Lewis, David. "Mathematics Is Megethology." *Philosophia Mathematica* 1 (1993): 3-23. A structuralist reconstruction of set theory in mereological terms; illuminates the ontology of mathematical abstracta.
