# Further Reading and Primary Sources — Fictionalism and Nominalism

*Essential texts on nominalist and fictionalist approaches to abstract objects.*

---

Goodman, Nelson, and W. V. Quine. "Steps Toward a Constructive Nominalism." *Journal of Symbolic Logic* 12 (1947): 105–122. — The original nominalist program for mathematics.

Field, Hartry. *Science Without Numbers: A Defence of Nominalism*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1980. Second edition: Oxford University Press, 2016.

Field, Hartry. *Realism, Mathematics and Modality*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1989. — Especially the title essay on fictionalism.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343–377. — Introduces naturalness as a primitive.

Rodriguez-Pereyra, Gonzalo. *Resemblance Nominalism: A Solution to the Problem of Universals*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2002.

Balaguer, Mark. *Platonism and Anti-Platonism in Mathematics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1998.

Thomasson, Amie. *Ontology Made Easy*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2015.

Rosen, Gideon, and John Burgess. "Nominalism Reconsidered." In Shapiro (ed.), *Oxford Handbook of Philosophy of Mathematics and Logic*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2005.
