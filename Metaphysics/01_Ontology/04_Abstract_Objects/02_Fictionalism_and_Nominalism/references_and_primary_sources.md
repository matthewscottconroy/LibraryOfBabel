# References and Primary Sources: Fictionalism and Nominalism

## Foundational Texts

Goodman, Nelson and Quine, W.V.O. "Steps Toward a Constructive Nominalism." *Journal of Symbolic Logic* 12 (1947): 105-122. An early attempt to systematically eliminate abstract objects from mathematics; later abandoned by Quine.

Field, Hartry. *Science Without Numbers*. Princeton University Press, 1980. The benchmark for contemporary nominalism; shows how Newtonian physics can be reformulated without quantification over numbers.

## Contemporary Works

Field, Hartry. *Realism, Mathematics, and Modality*. Blackwell, 1989. Essays developing and defending mathematical fictionalism and nominalism about abstract objects.

Balaguer, Mark. "A Fictionalist Account of the Indispensable Applications of Mathematics." *Philosophical Studies* 83 (1996): 291-314. Argues that fictionalism can account for the applicability of mathematics without admitting abstract objects.

Leng, Mary. *Mathematics and Reality*. Oxford University Press, 2010. A careful contemporary defense of mathematical fictionalism.

Sider, Theodore. "Ontological Realism." In Chalmers, Manley, and Wasserman (eds.), *Metametaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2009. Discusses whether disputes about abstract objects are genuinely substantive.
