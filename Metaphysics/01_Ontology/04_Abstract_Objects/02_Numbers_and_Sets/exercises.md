# Exercises: Numbers and Sets

1. Benacerraf argues that numbers cannot be identified with any particular sets. Reconstruct the argument and assess its force.

2. Explain the iterative conception of set. Why is it more philosophically satisfying than the naive comprehension principle?

3. Can all mathematical objects be reduced to sets? What philosophical problems arise from the attempt?

4. Structuralism holds that mathematics is about structures rather than particular objects. What is a structure? Are structures themselves abstract objects?

5. Modal structuralism translates mathematical claims into modal claims. Give an example of such a translation and assess whether it succeeds.

6. What is the relationship between the foundations of mathematics (set theory, category theory) and the ontology of mathematical objects?
