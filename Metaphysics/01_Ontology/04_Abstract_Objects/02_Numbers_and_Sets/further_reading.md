# Further Reading: Numbers and Sets

Benacerraf, Paul. "What Numbers Could Not Be." *Philosophical Review* 74 (1965): 47-73. The argument that numbers are structural positions, not sets.

Shapiro, Stewart. *Philosophy of Mathematics: Structure and Ontology*. Oxford University Press, 1997. The canonical statement of ante rem structuralism.

Hellman, Geoffrey. *Mathematics Without Numbers*. Oxford University Press, 1989. Modal structuralism as a nominalist alternative.

Maddy, Penelope. *Realism in Mathematics*. Oxford University Press, 1990. A naturalist Platonism grounded in cognitive access to sets.

Boolos, George. "The Iterative Conception of Set." *Journal of Philosophy* 68 (1971): 215-231. Philosophical foundations of the iterative hierarchy.

Potter, Michael. *Set Theory and Its Philosophy*. Oxford University Press, 2004. A philosophically sophisticated introduction to set theory.
