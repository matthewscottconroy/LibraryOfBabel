# Important Figures: Numbers and Sets

**Georg Cantor** (1845-1918) developed set theory and the theory of transfinite cardinals, establishing the mathematical framework within which most subsequent philosophy of mathematics operates.

**Giuseppe Peano** (1858-1932) formulated the axioms for arithmetic (Peano axioms) that provide the standard formalization of the natural number system.

**Ernst Zermelo** (1871-1953) and **Abraham Fraenkel** (1891-1965) developed ZF set theory, the standard axiomatic foundation for mathematics in which all mathematical objects are construed as sets.

**Paul Benacerraf** (b. 1931) argued that numbers cannot be identified with any particular sets, because any omega-sequence of sets would serve as well — a result that motivates structuralism.

**Stewart Shapiro** (b. 1951) developed a sophisticated ante rem structuralism: mathematics is about abstract structures that exist independently, and numbers are positions in those structures rather than particular objects.
