# Further Reading: Propositions and Properties

Frege, Gottlob. "The Thought: A Logical Inquiry." *Beitrage zur Philosophie des Deutschen Idealismus* 2 (1918): 58-77. Propositions as abstract thoughts.

Russell, Bertrand. *The Problems of Philosophy*. Williams and Norgate, 1912. Accessible discussion of universals and propositions.

Bealer, George. *Quality and Concept*. Oxford University Press, 1982. A systematic intensional theory of properties and propositions.

Stalnaker, Robert. "Propositions." In *Issues in the Philosophy of Language*, ed. MacKay and Merrill. Yale University Press, 1976.

Armstrong, D.M. *A World of States of Affairs*. Cambridge University Press, 1997. The canonical statement of the states-of-affairs ontology.

Soames, Scott. *What is Meaning?* Princeton University Press, 2010. Arguments against possible-worlds propositions and for structured propositions.
