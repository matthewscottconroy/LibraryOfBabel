# Further Reading and Primary Sources — Historical Background

*Essential texts for the history of the problem of universals.*

---

## Primary Sources

Plato. *Parmenides* — Plato's self-critical dialogue exploring problems for the Forms, including the Third Man regress.

Aristotle. *Categories*, Ch. 2–5. *Metaphysics*, Books I, VII, XIII.

Porphyry. *Isagoge*. Translated in *Five Texts on the Medieval Problem of Universals*, ed. Spade. Hackett, 1994.

Abelard, Peter. *Logica 'Ingredientibus'*. Selections in Spade (ed.) above.

Ockham, William of. *Summa of Logic*, Part I (on terms and universals). Translated by Loux. Notre Dame, 1974.

Russell, Bertrand. *Problems of Philosophy*, Ch. 9–10. Oxford, 1912.

Armstrong, D. M. *Universals and Scientific Realism*, Vol. 1: *Nominalism and Realism*. Cambridge, 1978.

## Secondary Literature

Spade, Paul Vincent, ed. *Five Texts on the Medieval Problem of Universals*. Indianapolis: Hackett, 1994. — Essential anthology with Porphyry, Boethius, Abelard, Duns Scotus, Ockham.

Loux, Michael J., ed. *Universals and Particulars: Readings in Ontology*. Notre Dame: University of Notre Dame Press, 1976.

Marenbon, John. *The Philosophy of Peter Abelard*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1997.

MacBride, Fraser. "Universals." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2024.
