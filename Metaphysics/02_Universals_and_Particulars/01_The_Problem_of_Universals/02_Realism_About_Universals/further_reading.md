# Further Reading — Realism About Universals

*Essential texts for realist positions on universals.*

---

Armstrong, D. M. *Universals and Scientific Realism*, 2 vols. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1978.

Armstrong, D. M. *What Is a Law of Nature?* Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1983.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343–377.

Lewis, David. *On the Plurality of Worlds*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1986. — Chapter 1 for the theory of properties as sets and naturalness.

Oliver, Alex. "The Metaphysics of Properties." *Mind* 105 (1996): 1–80. — Survey of realist and nominalist accounts.

Sider, Theodore. *Writing the Book of the World*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2011. — Develops a Lewisian account of structure/naturalness.

Loux, Michael J. *Metaphysics: A Contemporary Introduction*, 3rd ed. New York: Routledge, 2006. — Chapters on universals.
