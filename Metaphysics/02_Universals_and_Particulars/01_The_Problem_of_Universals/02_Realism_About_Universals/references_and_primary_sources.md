# References and Primary Sources: Realism About Universals

## Foundational Texts

Armstrong, D.M. *Universals and Scientific Realism*, 2 vols. Cambridge University Press, 1978. The definitive contemporary defense of immanent realism: universals are real, mind-independent, and instantiated in the physical world.

Russell, Bertrand. *The Problems of Philosophy*. Williams & Norgate, 1912. Ch. 9-10 present a clear and accessible case for universals as genuinely existing entities required for both knowledge and predication.

## Contemporary Works

Bealer, George. *Quality and Concept*. Oxford University Press, 1982. Defends an intensional realism about properties and propositional attitudes.

Moreland, J.P. *Universals*. McGill-Queen's University Press, 2001. A comprehensive treatment covering both immanent and transcendent versions of realism.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343-377. Argues that natural properties (a species of universals) are indispensable for analyses of laws, similarity, supervenience, and content.

Sider, Theodore. *Writing the Book of the World*. Oxford University Press, 2011. Develops a sophisticated realism about structure in nature that is in the spirit of, though distinct from, realism about universals.
