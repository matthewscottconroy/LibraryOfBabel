# Further Reading — Nominalism

*Essential texts for nominalist accounts of universals.*

---

Goodman, Nelson. *The Structure of Appearance*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1951.

Goodman, Nelson, and W. V. Quine. "Steps Toward a Constructive Nominalism." *Journal of Symbolic Logic* 12 (1947): 105–122.

Quine, W. V. O. "On What There Is." In *From a Logical Point of View*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1953.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343–377. — Introduces naturalness as a primitive; the key Lewis paper for the universals debate.

Rodriguez-Pereyra, Gonzalo. *Resemblance Nominalism: A Solution to the Problem of Universals*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2002.

Price, H. H. *Thinking and Experience*. London: Hutchinson, 1953. — Chapters 1–2 on resemblance.

Loux, Michael J. *Metaphysics: A Contemporary Introduction*, 3rd ed. New York: Routledge, 2006. — Survey of nominalist options.
