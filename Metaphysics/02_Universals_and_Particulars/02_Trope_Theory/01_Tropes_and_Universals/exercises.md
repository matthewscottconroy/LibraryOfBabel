# Exercises — Tropes and Universals

*Problems for engaging with trope theory.*

---

**Exercise 1 — Tropes vs. Universals**
State the "multi-location problem" for universals. Does trope theory avoid it? Now state the "individuation problem" for tropes (two exactly similar tropes: what makes them distinct?). Does universals realism have the analogous problem? Compare the two problems and assess which is more serious.

**Exercise 2 — The Bundle Theory**
On the trope-bundle theory, a concrete particular is just a bundle of compresent tropes. What individuation problem does this create for two qualitatively identical particulars (two perfect spheres with identical properties)? Can the bundle theorist distinguish them? Compare the trope-bundle solution with the universals-bundle solution and with the bare-particular solution.

**Exercise 3 — Resemblance Among Tropes**
The trope theorist says that universals are replaced by resemblance classes of tropes. Construct the following objection: "Resemblance among tropes requires a resemblance relation; this relation is a universal; so the trope theorist has not eliminated universals after all." Now construct the best trope-theoretic response.

**Exercise 4 — Compresence**
Explain the compresence problem. Evaluate three proposed solutions: (a) compresence is a primitive relation; (b) the bundle is a set of tropes; (c) tropes are mutually dependent. For each, identify the ontological commitment it introduces and assess whether it is less problematic than positing bare particulars or universals.

**Thought Experiment — Trope Duplication**
God creates a perfect duplicate of the actual world alongside it. The duplicate has all the same tropes, numerically distinct but exactly resembling. On the trope-bundle view, what makes the actual table numerically distinct from its duplicate? Does your answer help or hurt the trope theory?
