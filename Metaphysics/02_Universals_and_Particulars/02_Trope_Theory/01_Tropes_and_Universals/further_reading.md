# Further Reading — Tropes and Universals

*Essential texts for trope theory.*

---

Williams, D. C. "The Elements of Being." *Review of Metaphysics* 7 (1953): 3–18, 171–192.

Campbell, Keith. *Abstract Particulars*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1990.

Maurin, Anna-Sofia. *If Tropes*. Dordrecht: Kluwer, 2002.

Bacon, John. *Universals and Property Instances: The Alphabet of Being*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1995.

Molnar, George. *Powers: A Study in Metaphysics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2003. — Chapter 2 on tropes and powers.

Daly, Chris. "Tropes." *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society* 94 (1994): 253–261. — A critical assessment.

Armstrong, D. M. *Universals: An Opinionated Introduction*. Boulder, CO: Westview Press, 1989. — Chapter on tropes from the universals realist perspective.
