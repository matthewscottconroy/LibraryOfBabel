# Important Figures — Tropes and Universals

*Key philosophers in the development of trope theory.*

---

**D. C. Williams (1899–1983)** — Introduced "abstract particulars" (tropes) into analytic philosophy in "The Elements of Being" (1953). His programme of building all ontology from tropes + resemblance remains foundational.

**G. F. Stout (1860–1944)** — Earlier British philosopher who anticipated trope theory with his doctrine of "particular properties" or "individual accidents." Williams developed his view largely independently.

**Keith Campbell (1938–)** — *Abstract Particulars* (1990) is the most systematic and technically developed monograph on trope theory in analytic philosophy.

**Anna-Sofia Maurin (1970–)** — Contemporary defender of tropes who has clarified the metaphysical structure of trope theory, especially regarding individuation and compresence. *If Tropes* (2002).

**John Bacon (1935–)** — Developed the formal logic of tropes and their relations to universals and particulars.

**Chris Daly** — Critical assessments of trope theory, helping sharpen the debate about whether tropes genuinely improve on universals-based accounts.
