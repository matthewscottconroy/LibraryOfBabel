# References and Primary Sources: Tropes and Universals

## Foundational Texts

Williams, D.C. "On the Elements of Being." *Review of Metaphysics* 7 (1953): 3-18. Introduces modern trope theory as a middle way between universals realism and simple nominalism.

Stout, G.F. "The Nature of Universals and Propositions." *Proceedings of the British Academy* 10 (1921): 157-172. An early statement of the view that properties are particular, not universal — a precursor to trope theory.

## Contemporary Works

Campbell, Keith. *Abstract Particulars*. Blackwell, 1990. The book-length development of trope theory as a complete metaphysical system.

Lowe, E.J. "Statues and Lumps: A Matter of Coincidence?" *Synthese* 148 (2006): 489-501. Discusses how tropes and universals relate in a four-category ontology.

Daly, Chris. "Tropes." *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society* 94 (1994): 253-261. A careful critical examination of trope theory and its alternatives.

Denkel, Arda. *Object and Property*. Cambridge University Press, 1996. Discusses tropes in relation to substance, property, and identity.
