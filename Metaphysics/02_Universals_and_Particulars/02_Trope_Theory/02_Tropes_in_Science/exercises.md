# Exercises — Tropes in Science

*Problems for engaging with trope theory's applications in philosophy of science.*

---

**Exercise 1 — Mass as a Trope**
Every electron has the same mass (9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kg). On the universals view, all electrons instantiate one universal ELECTRON-MASS. On the trope view, each electron has its own distinct mass-trope, and all these tropes exactly resemble. What is the difference, if any, between these two views regarding the *explanation* of why all electrons have the same mass? Is either view's explanation more illuminating?

**Exercise 2 — Tropes and the Best Systems Account**
Lewis's Best Systems Account says laws are regularities that appear in the simplest and strongest systematic description of the world. Explain how this account works for a world described entirely in trope terms (no universals). Does the Best Systems Account work differently in a trope ontology versus a universals ontology?

**Exercise 3 — Powerful Qualities**
C. B. Martin's "powerful qualities" are simultaneously qualitative (they have a specific intrinsic character) and dispositional (they are directed toward a manifestation). Explain the difference between this view and (a) pure categoricalism and (b) pure dispositionalism. Does the powerful qualities view solve the "finkish disposition" problem that defeats conditional analyses?

**Exercise 4 — Tropes and Emergence**
Wetness is often cited as an emergent property of water. On the trope view, how would you characterize the wetness-trope of a body of water? Is it grounded in the molecular tropes of the water's H₂O molecules? If yes, explain how; if no, what else is needed? Does your answer commit you to a specific account of emergent causation?

**Thought Experiment — The Last Trope**
Suppose God eliminates all tropes in the universe one by one, leaving only one: the charge-trope of the last remaining electron. Does the law of electromagnetism still exist? Does the fact that charge disposes toward certain interactions still obtain? What does your answer imply about whether laws are grounded in individual tropes or in the pattern of many tropes?
