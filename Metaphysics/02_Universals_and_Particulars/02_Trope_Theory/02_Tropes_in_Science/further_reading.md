# Further Reading — Tropes in Science

*Essential texts on tropes, dispositions, and their application to physics and laws.*

---

Campbell, Keith. *Abstract Particulars*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1990. — The foundational monograph.

Martin, C. B. *The Mind in Nature*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2008.

Molnar, George. *Powers: A Study in Metaphysics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2003.

Bird, Alexander. *Nature's Metaphysics: Laws and Properties*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.

Mumford, Stephen. *Dispositions*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1998.

Mumford, Stephen, and Rani Lill Anjum. *Getting Causes from Powers*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2011.

Lewis, David. "Humean Supervenience Debugged." *Mind* 103 (1994): 473–490. — Lewis's defense of Humeanism, relevant to the trope-Humean combination.

Armstrong, D. M. "Tropes." In *Papers in Metaphysics and Epistemology*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1999.
