# Important Figures — Tropes in Science

*Key philosophers connecting trope theory to philosophy of science and metaphysics of nature.*

---

**D. C. Williams (1899–1983)** — Foundational work on tropes including their application to physical properties.

**Keith Campbell (1938–)** — *Abstract Particulars* (1990) includes discussion of how trope theory applies to physical science.

**C. B. Martin (1924–2008)** — Developed the view of properties as "powerful qualities" — both qualitative and dispositional — which connects trope theory to dispositionalism. Key papers collected in *The Mind in Nature* (2008).

**George Molnar (1934–1999)** — *Powers: A Study in Metaphysics* (2003), posthumously published, develops the account of physical properties as directed powers, compatible with trope theory.

**Alexander Bird (1964–)** — *Nature's Metaphysics* (2007) argues for essential dispositional natures of properties, relevant to trope-disposition combinations.

**Anna-Sofia Maurin (1970–)** — *If Tropes* (2002) includes analysis of trope theory's commitments in metaphysics of science.

**Stephen Mumford (1968–)** — Extensive work on dispositions and powers, including their relationship to trope ontology. *Dispositions* (1998).
