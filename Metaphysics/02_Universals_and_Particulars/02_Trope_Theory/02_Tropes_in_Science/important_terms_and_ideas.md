# Important Terms and Ideas: Tropes in Science

**Scientific ontology**: The ontological commitments entailed by accepting our best scientific theories. Trope theorists argue that physics posits particular property-instances (specific values of charge, mass, spin at specific locations) rather than universals.

**Causal tropes**: The idea that the causal relata in singular causation are specific tropes — this particular heat-trope causing this particular expansion — rather than generic event-types or universals. Grounds singular causal explanations.

**Powers and tropes**: Many trope theorists identify the relevant tropes with powers or dispositions: the mass of this particle is a particular power directed at producing gravitational attraction. The identity of the trope is constituted by its directedness.

**Quantum fields and tropes**: An argument that quantum field theory supports trope ontology: what the theory describes are particular values of field quantities at points or regions, which resemble particular property-instances (tropes) more than they resemble universals.

**Causal efficacy of tropes**: The question of whether it is the trope itself or the whole object (or the state of affairs) that is causally efficacious. Trope-based causal theories typically assign efficacy directly to particular property-instances.

**Type-token identity**: In philosophy of mind, the view that each particular mental event (token) is identical to a particular physical event. Token identity is compatible with type-type distinctness — two mental tokens of the same type may be physically different.

**Natural kind membership and tropes**: Whether membership in a natural kind (gold, water, electron) is determined by qualitative resemblance among tropes, or by some deeper essence. Essentialism about kinds holds that the essence grounds the trope-resemblances.
