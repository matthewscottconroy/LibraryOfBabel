# References and Primary Sources: Identity of Indiscernibles

## Foundational Texts

Leibniz, G.W. "Discourse on Metaphysics" and "Correspondence with Clarke." In *Philosophical Texts*, ed. Francks and Woolhouse. Oxford University Press, 1998. The original statement and defense of the Principle of the Identity of Indiscernibles.

Black, Max. "The Identity of Indiscernibles." *Mind* 61 (1952): 153-164. The classic counterexample: a universe containing only two qualitatively identical spheres, arguing that PII is false.

## Contemporary Works

Adams, Robert Merrihew. "Primitive Thisness and Primitive Identity." *Journal of Philosophy* 76 (1979): 5-26. Argues for primitive haecceities (thisnesses) as the basis of individual identity, against the PII.

O'Leary-Hawthorne, John. "The Bundle Theory of Substance and the Identity of Indiscernibles." *Analysis* 55 (1995): 191-196. Examines the consequences of PII for bundle theories.

Strawson, P.F. *Individuals*. Methuen, 1959. Discusses the conditions for identifying and re-identifying material objects and persons.

Hawthorne, John. "Identity." In Sider, Hawthorne, and Zimmerman (eds.), *Contemporary Debates in Metaphysics*. Blackwell, 2008. A survey of issues in the metaphysics of identity.
