# References and Primary Sources: Qualitativism

## Foundational Texts

Leibniz, G.W. *New Essays on Human Understanding*. Trans. Remnant and Bennett. Cambridge University Press, 1996. Leibniz's defense of the Principle of the Identity of Indiscernibles against Locke's view that matter provides a principle of individuation.

Quine, W.V.O. "Identity, Ostension, and Hypostasis." In *From a Logical Point of View*. Harvard University Press, 1953. Discusses qualitative criteria for identity.

## Contemporary Works

Adams, Robert Merrihew. "Primitive Thisness and Primitive Identity." *Journal of Philosophy* 76 (1979): 5-26. The classic defense of non-qualitative (haecceitistic) individuation against qualitativism.

Hawthorne, John and Sider, Theodore. "Locations." *Philosophical Topics* 30 (2002): 53-76. Discusses how qualitative properties and spatiotemporal location interact in individuation.

Zimmerman, Dean. "Distinct Indiscernibles and the Bundle Theory." *Mind* 106 (1997): 305-309. Defends bundle theory against the Identity of Indiscernibles objection.

Lewis, David. *On the Plurality of Worlds*. Blackwell, 1986. §4.4 discusses individuation by qualitative properties and haecceities.
