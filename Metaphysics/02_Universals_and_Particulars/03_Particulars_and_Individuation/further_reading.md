# Further Reading — Particulars and Individuation

*Essential texts on substrata, haecceities, identity, and individuation.*

---

Adams, Robert Merrihew. "Primitive Thisness and Primitive Identity." *Journal of Philosophy* 76 (1979): 5–26.

Black, Max. "The Identity of Indiscernibles." *Mind* 61 (1952): 153–164.

Lewis, David. *On the Plurality of Worlds*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1986. — Chapter 4 on counterpart theory and haecceitism.

Bergmann, Gustav. *Realism: A Critique of Brentano and Meinong*. Madison: University of Wisconsin Press, 1967.

Swinburne, Richard. "Thisness." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 73 (1995): 389–400.

French, Steven, and Décio Krause. *Identity in Physics: A Historical, Philosophical, and Formal Analysis*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2006. — On quantum mechanics and individuation.

Lowe, E. J. *A Survey of Metaphysics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2002. — Chapters 5–6 on individuation.

Hawthorne, John, and Ted Sider. "Locations." *Philosophical Topics* 30 (2002): 53–76. — On spatiotemporal individuation.
