# Further Reading — Metaontological Issues

*Essential texts on the methodology of ontology.*

---

Carnap, Rudolf. "Empiricism, Semantics, and Ontology." *Revue Internationale de Philosophie* 4 (1950): 20–40.

Quine, W. V. O. "On What There Is." In *From a Logical Point of View*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1953.

van Inwagen, Peter. "Meta-ontology." *Erkenntnis* 48 (1998): 233–250.

Chalmers, David, David Manley, and Ryan Wasserman, eds. *Metametaphysics: New Essays on the Foundations of Ontology*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2009. — The essential collection on metaontology.

Sider, Theodore. *Writing the Book of the World*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2011.

Hirsch, Eli. *Quantifier Variance and Realism*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2011.

Thomasson, Amie. *Ontology Made Easy*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2015.

Fine, Kit. "The Question of Realism." *Philosophers' Imprint* 1 (2001): 1–30.
