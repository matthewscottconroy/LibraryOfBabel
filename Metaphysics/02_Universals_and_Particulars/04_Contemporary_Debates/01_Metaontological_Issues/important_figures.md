# Important Figures — Metaontological Issues

*Key thinkers on the methodology and foundations of ontological inquiry.*

---

**Rudolf Carnap (1891–1970)** — Distinguished internal from external ontological questions, deflating traditional ontology. "Empiricism, Semantics, and Ontology" (1950).

**W. V. O. Quine (1908–2000)** — Criterion of ontological commitment via existential quantification. "On What There Is" (1953). Challenged Carnap's internal/external distinction.

**Peter van Inwagen (1942–)** — Neo-Quinean realist about ontology. *Material Beings* (1990) and *Ontology, Identity and Modality* (2001).

**Theodore Sider (1967–)** — Developed quantifier variance and structural metaphysics. *Writing the Book of the World* (2011).

**Eli Hirsch (1944–)** — Defended quantifier variance against Sider's metaphysical realism. *Dividing Reality* (1993) and *Quantifier Variance and Realism* (2011).

**Amie Thomasson (1968–)** — Easy ontology and deflationary approach to ontological questions. *Ontology Made Easy* (2015).

**Kit Fine (1946–)** — Distinguished ontological reality from being (existence); developed notionist approach. "The Question of Realism" (2001).

**Jonathan Schaffer (1970–)** — Priority monism and the view that the fundamental question of ontology is not existence but grounding. "On What Grounds What" (2009).
