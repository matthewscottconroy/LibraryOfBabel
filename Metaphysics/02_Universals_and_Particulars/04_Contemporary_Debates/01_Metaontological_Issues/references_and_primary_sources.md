# References and Primary Sources: Metaontological Issues (Universals)

## Foundational Texts

Quine, W.V.O. "On What There Is." *Review of Metaphysics* 2 (1948): 21-38. The canonical statement of the Quinean approach to ontological questions; provides the criterion of ontological commitment for universals debates.

Carnap, Rudolf. "Empiricism, Semantics, and Ontology." *Revue Internationale de Philosophie* 4 (1950): 20-40. Argues that ontological questions about abstract entities are "external" and should be answered pragmatically, not theoretically.

## Contemporary Works

Chalmers, David, Manley, David, and Wasserman, Ryan (eds.). *Metametaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2009. The central anthology on the question of whether metaphysical debates are substantive.

Sider, Theodore. *Writing the Book of the World*. Oxford University Press, 2011. A defense of substantive metaphysical realism: some ontological claims track the real joints in nature.

Thomasson, Amie. *Ontology Made Easy*. Oxford University Press, 2015. Argues that many traditional ontological debates are deflationary — answerable by trivial inference from ordinary truths.

Hirsch, Eli. *Quantifier Variance and Realism*. Oxford University Press, 2011. Argues that ontological disputes often reflect different conventions for using quantifiers, not substantive disagreements.
