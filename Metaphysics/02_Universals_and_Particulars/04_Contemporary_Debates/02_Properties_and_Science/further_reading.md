# Further Reading — Properties and Science

*Essential texts on naturalness, determinables, and properties in the context of science.*

---

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343–377.

Sider, Theodore. *Writing the Book of the World*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2011. — On naturalness and joint-carving.

Armstrong, D. M. *What Is a Law of Nature?* Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1983.

Ellis, Brian. *Scientific Essentialism*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2001.

Wilson, Jessica M. "Determinables and Determinates." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2023.

Kim, Jaegwon. "Multiple Realization and the Metaphysics of Reduction." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 52 (1992): 1–26. — On determinable mental properties.

Yablo, Stephen. "Mental Causation." *Philosophical Review* 101 (1992): 245–280. — On higher-order properties and causal relevance.
