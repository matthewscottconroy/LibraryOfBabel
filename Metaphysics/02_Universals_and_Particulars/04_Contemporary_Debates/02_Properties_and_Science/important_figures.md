# Important Figures — Properties and Science

*Key thinkers at the intersection of property theory and philosophy of science.*

---

**W. V. O. Quine (1908–2000)** — Connected ontological commitment to scientific theorizing; argued that properties (as universals) might be required by the best total theory.

**David Armstrong (1926–2014)** — His naturalistic account of universals explicitly connects genuine properties to those figuring in natural laws. His work on laws of nature is directly relevant.

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** — The concept of naturalness as a feature of properties, connected to the goals of scientific theorizing. "New Work for a Theory of Universals" (1983).

**Brian Ellis (1929–)** — *Scientific Essentialism* (2001) argues that natural kinds have real essences and that the properties of natural kinds are necessarily dispositional.

**Alexander Bird (1964–)** — *Nature's Metaphysics* (2007) on dispositional properties and necessary laws in the context of scientific realism.

**Jaegwon Kim (1934–2019)** — Work on supervenience and determinables relevant to higher-order properties and mental causation. *Supervenience and Mind* (1993).

**Peter Unger (1942–)** — Raised challenges about property individuation and natural kinds in the context of philosophy of language and science.
