# Further Reading — Applications

*Essential texts on the application of property theory to color, mind, and morality.*

---

Moore, G. E. *Principia Ethica*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1903. — Non-naturalist moral realism.

Mackie, J. L. *Ethics: Inventing Right and Wrong*. Harmondsworth: Penguin, 1977. — Error theory.

Boyd, Richard. "How to Be a Moral Realist." In Sayre-McCord (ed.), *Essays on Moral Realism*. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1988.

Kim, Jaegwon. "Multiple Realization and the Metaphysics of Reduction." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 52 (1992): 1–26.

Shoemaker, Sydney. "Causality and Properties." In Van Inwagen (ed.), *Time and Cause*. Dordrecht: Reidel, 1980.

Byrne, Alex, and David Hilbert, eds. *Readings on Color*, 2 vols. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1997. — Comprehensive anthology on color metaphysics.

Thomasson, Amie. *Fiction and Metaphysics*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1999.
