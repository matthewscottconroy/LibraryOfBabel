# References and Primary Sources: Applications (Universals and Particulars)

## Foundational Texts

Lewis, David. *Philosophical Papers*, vol. 2. Oxford University Press, 1986. Contains Lewis's papers on causation, laws, and properties — showing how universals and natural properties function in applied metaphysics.

Armstrong, D.M. *A World of States of Affairs*. Cambridge University Press, 1997. Shows how universals and states of affairs provide the fundamental ontology for causation, laws, and truthmaking.

## Contemporary Works

Hawthorne, John. *Metaphysical Essays*. Oxford University Press, 2006. A collection covering the applications of universals and properties in personal identity, materialism, and probability.

Sider, Theodore. *Four-Dimensionalism*. Oxford University Press, 2001. Uses a sophisticated account of properties (especially temporal properties) to analyze persistence through time.

Horgan, Terence and Potrč, Matjaž. *Austere Realism*. MIT Press, 2008. An application of a radical ontology with minimal universals to problems in metaphysics, semantics, and epistemology.

Kim, Jaegwon. *Supervenience and Mind*. Cambridge University Press, 1993. Explores the role of properties in the mind-body problem, focusing on how properties and their supervenience relations structure the debate.
