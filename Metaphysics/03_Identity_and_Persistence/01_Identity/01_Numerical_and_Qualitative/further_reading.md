# Further Reading — Numerical and Qualitative Identity

*Essential texts on the logic and metaphysics of identity.*

---

Frege, Gottlob. "On Sense and Reference." *Zeitschrift für Philosophie und philosophische Kritik* 100 (1892): 25–50. Translated in *Translations from the Philosophical Writings of Gottlob Frege*, ed. Geach and Black. Oxford: Blackwell, 1952.

Geach, Peter T. *Reference and Generality*. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1962. — Relative identity.

Wiggins, David. *Sameness and Substance Renewed*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2001.

Kripke, Saul. *Naming and Necessity*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1980. — Lecture III on the necessity of identity.

Noonan, Harold. *Personal Identity*, 2nd ed. London: Routledge, 2003.

Perry, John, ed. *Personal Identity*. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1975. — Classic anthology.

Hawley, Katherine. "Identity and Indiscernibility." *Mind* 118 (2009): 101–119.
