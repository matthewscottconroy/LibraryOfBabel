# References and Primary Sources: Numerical and Qualitative Identity

## Foundational Texts

Leibniz, G.W. "Discourse on Metaphysics." In *Philosophical Texts*, ed. Francks and Woolhouse. Oxford University Press, 1998. Source of the Indiscernibility of Identicals and the Principle of the Identity of Indiscernibles.

Frege, Gottlob. *The Foundations of Arithmetic*, §§62-67. Trans. Austin. Blackwell, 1950. The "Julius Caesar problem" and Frege's criterion of identity: the number of Fs = the number of Gs iff the Fs and Gs can be placed in one-one correspondence.

## Contemporary Works

Wiggins, David. *Sameness and Substance Renewed*. Cambridge University Press, 2001. A thorough treatment of identity as an absolute relation and the role of sortals in fixing identity conditions.

Noonan, Harold. *Personal Identity*. 2nd ed. Routledge, 2003. Covers the main theories and thought experiments about personal identity.

Sider, Theodore. "All the World's a Stage." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 74 (1996): 433-453. Defends a stage view of personal identity and objects.

Hawthorne, John. *Metaphysical Essays*. Oxford University Press, 2006. Contains essays on identity, composition, and persistence.
