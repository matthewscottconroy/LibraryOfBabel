# Further Reading — Personal Identity

*Essential texts on personal identity.*

---

Locke, John. *An Essay Concerning Human Understanding*, Book II, Ch. 27. — The founding text.

Parfit, Derek. *Reasons and Persons*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1984. — Part III.

Olson, Eric. *The Human Animal*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1997.

Shoemaker, Sydney, and Richard Swinburne. *Personal Identity*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1984.

Perry, John, ed. *Personal Identity*. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1975. — Essential anthology including Locke, Butler, Reid, Hume, and key modern papers.

Martin, Raymond, and John Barresi, eds. *Personal Identity*. Oxford: Blackwell, 2003. — More comprehensive anthology.

Noonan, Harold. *Personal Identity*, 2nd ed. London: Routledge, 2003. — Survey.
