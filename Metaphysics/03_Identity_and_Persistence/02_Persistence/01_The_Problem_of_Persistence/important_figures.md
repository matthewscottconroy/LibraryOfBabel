# Important Figures — The Problem of Persistence

*Key thinkers on the metaphysics of persistence through time.*

---

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** — Developed and defended four-dimensionalism and the temporal parts solution to temporary intrinsics. *On the Plurality of Worlds* (1986) and *Philosophical Papers*, Vol. II (1986).

**Mark Johnston (1954–)** — Defended endurantism against Lewis's temporary intrinsics argument. "Is There a Problem About Persistence?" (1987).

**Sally Haslanger (1955–)** — Developed a sophisticated endurantist response to the temporary intrinsics argument. "Endurance and Temporary Intrinsics" (1989).

**Theodore Sider (1967–)** — *Four-Dimensionalism: An Ontology of Persistence and Time* (2001) is the most thorough contemporary defense of perdurantism and stage theory.

**Katherine Hawley (1971–)** — *How Things Persist* (2001) surveys endurantism, worm theory, and stage theory systematically.

**Peter van Inwagen (1942–)** — Defended endurantism and raised challenges for temporal-parts ontology. *Material Beings* (1990).

**Ernest Sosa (1940–)** — Defended endurantism with time-indexed properties. "Subjects Among Other Things" (1987).
