# References and Primary Sources: The Problem of Persistence

## Foundational Texts

Lewis, David. "Survival and Identity." In *Philosophical Papers*, vol. 1. Oxford University Press, 1983. Introduces the distinction between perdurance (four-dimensionalism) and endurance (three-dimensionalism).

van Inwagen, Peter. "Four-Dimensional Objects." *Noûs* 24 (1990): 245-255. A clear statement of the debate between three-dimensional and four-dimensional views.

## Contemporary Works

Sider, Theodore. *Four-Dimensionalism*. Oxford University Press, 2001. The definitive contemporary defense of four-dimensionalism, with responses to objections.

Hawley, Katherine. *How Things Persist*. Oxford University Press, 2001. A thorough treatment of the problem of persistence, covering both three-dimensionalist and four-dimensionalist options.

Haslanger, Sally. "Endurance and Temporary Intrinsics." *Analysis* 49 (1989): 119-125. An influential discussion of how three-dimensionalists should handle temporary intrinsic properties.

Sider, Theodore. "All the World's a Stage." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 74 (1996): 433-453. Defends stage theory as an alternative to both endurantism and perdurance worm theory.
