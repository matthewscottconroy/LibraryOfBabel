# References and Primary Sources: Coincident Objects

## Foundational Texts

Thomson, Judith Jarvis. "The Statue and the Clay." *Noûs* 32 (1998): 149-173. A careful treatment of the statue-clay puzzle; defends the existence of two numerically distinct co-located objects.

Lewis, David. *Parts of Classes*. Blackwell, 1991. Though primarily about set theory, the discussion of mereology and composition is essential for understanding the coincidence debate.

## Contemporary Works

Sider, Theodore. *Four-Dimensionalism*. Oxford University Press, 2001. Ch. 5 analyzes coincidence puzzles; argues they motivate four-dimensionalism.

Lowe, E.J. "The Metaphysics of Abstract Objects." *Journal of Philosophy* 92 (1995): 509-524; and *The Possibility of Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 1998. Lowe's sortalist approach to coincidence.

Zimmerman, Dean. "Theories of Masses and Problems of Constitution." *Philosophical Review* 104 (1995): 53-110. A comprehensive treatment of problems of constitution.

Baker, Lynne Rudder. *The Metaphysics of Everyday Life*. Cambridge University Press, 2007. Applies constitution theory to a range of coincident-object puzzles.
