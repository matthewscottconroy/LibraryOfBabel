# References and Primary Sources: Vagueness and Identity

## Foundational Texts

Evans, Gareth. "Can There Be Vague Objects?" *Analysis* 38 (1978): 208. The one-paragraph proof that indeterminate identity leads to contradiction.

Williamson, Timothy. *Vagueness*. Routledge, 1994. The definitive treatment of epistemic vagueness and a survey of other approaches.

## Contemporary Works

Keefe, Rosanna. *Theories of Vagueness*. Cambridge University Press, 2000. A comprehensive survey including supervaluationism, epistemicism, and many-valued approaches.

Lewis, David. "Many, But Almost One." In *Papers in Metaphysics and Epistemology*. Cambridge University Press, 1999. On vagueness in material constitution and the problem of the many.

Sider, Theodore. "Four-Dimensionalism and the Indeterminacy of Composition." *Philosophical Studies* 101 (2000): 135-157. Discusses vagueness in the context of composition and persistence.

Barnes, Elizabeth. *The Metaphysics of Vagueness*. Oxford University Press (forthcoming); and Barnes, E. and Williams, J.R.G. "A Theory of Metaphysical Indeterminacy." *Oxford Studies in Metaphysics* 6 (2011). On ontological indeterminacy.
