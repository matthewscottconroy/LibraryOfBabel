# Further Reading — Constitution and Coincidence

*Essential texts on material constitution, coincident objects, and vagueness.*

---

Baker, Lynne Rudder. *Persons and Bodies: A Constitution View*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2000.

Thomson, Judith Jarvis. "The Statue and the Clay." *Noûs* 32 (1998): 149–173.

Unger, Peter. "The Problem of the Many." *Midwest Studies in Philosophy* 5 (1980): 411–467.

Evans, Gareth. "Can There Be Vague Objects?" *Analysis* 38 (1978): 208.

Williamson, Timothy. *Vagueness*. London: Routledge, 1994.

Fine, Kit. "Vagueness, Truth and Logic." *Synthese* 30 (1975): 265–300.

Burke, Michael. "Preserving the Principle of One Object to a Place." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 54 (1994): 591–624.

Sider, Theodore. *Four-Dimensionalism*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2001. — Chapters 5–6 on constitution.
