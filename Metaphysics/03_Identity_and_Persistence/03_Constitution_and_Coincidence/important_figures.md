# Important Figures — Constitution and Coincidence

*Key thinkers on constitution, coincident objects, and vagueness.*

---

**Lynne Rudder Baker (1944–2017)** — Developed the constitution view: constitution is not identity. *Persons and Bodies* (2000).

**Michael Rea (1968–)** — *World Without Design* (2002) and papers on the metaphysics of constitution and coincident objects.

**Judith Jarvis Thomson (1929–2024)** — "The Statue and the Clay" (1998) — careful analysis of the coincident objects case.

**Peter Unger (1942–)** — Problem of Many. "The Problem of the Many" (1980).

**Gareth Evans (1946–1980)** — "Can There Be Vague Objects?" (1978) — the argument against ontic vagueness.

**Willard V. O. Quine (1908–2000)** — Developed the approach to vagueness as semantic rather than ontic.

**Kit Fine (1946–)** — "Vagueness, Truth and Logic" (1975) — supervaluationism for vagueness.

**Timothy Williamson (1955–)** — *Vagueness* (1994) — defended epistemicism: all vague terms have precise extensions we are ignorant of.

**Peter Simons (1950–)** — *Parts: A Study in Ontology* (1987) — mereology and coincident objects.
