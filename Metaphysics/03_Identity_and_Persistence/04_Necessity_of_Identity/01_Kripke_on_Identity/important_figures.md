# Important Figures — Necessity of Identity

*Key thinkers on necessary identity, transworld identity, and sortal dependence.*

---

**Saul Kripke (1940–2022)** — *Naming and Necessity* (1980) established the necessity of identity and the essential properties of individuals.

**Ruth Barcan Marcus (1921–2012)** — Proved the necessity of identity in quantified modal logic; anticipated many of Kripke's results. "The Identity of Individuals in a Strict Functional Calculus of Second Order" (1947).

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** — Counterpart theory as an alternative to transworld identity. *On the Plurality of Worlds* (1986).

**David Wiggins (1933–)** — Sortal-relative identity and the role of substance sortals in personal identity. *Sameness and Substance Renewed* (2001).

**Peter Geach (1916–2013)** — Relative identity thesis: "a is the same F as b" is not reducible to absolute identity. *Reference and Generality* (1962).

**Alvin Plantinga (1932–)** — Defended transworld identity and haecceitism against Lewis's counterpart theory. *The Nature of Necessity* (1974).
