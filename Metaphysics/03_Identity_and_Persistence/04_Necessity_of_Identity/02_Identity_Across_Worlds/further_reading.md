# Further Reading — Necessity of Identity

*Essential texts on Kripke's arguments and debates about transworld identity.*

---

Kripke, Saul. *Naming and Necessity*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1980.

Lewis, David. "Counterpart Theory and Quantified Modal Logic." *Journal of Philosophy* 65 (1968): 113–126.

Lewis, David. *On the Plurality of Worlds*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1986. — Chapter 4 on counterpart theory.

Plantinga, Alvin. *The Nature of Necessity*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1974. — Defense of transworld identity against Lewis.

Wiggins, David. *Sameness and Substance Renewed*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2001.

Fine, Kit. "Essence and Modality." *Philosophical Perspectives* 8 (1994): 1–16.

Marcus, Ruth Barcan. "Essentialism in Modal Logic." *Noûs* 1 (1967): 90–96.
