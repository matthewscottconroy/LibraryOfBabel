# References and Primary Sources: Identity Across Worlds

## Foundational Texts

Lewis, David. *On the Plurality of Worlds*. Blackwell, 1986. The definitive statement of modal realism and counterpart theory: individuals exist only in one world; transworld identity is replaced by counterpart relations.

Kripke, Saul. *Naming and Necessity*. Harvard University Press, 1980. The preface and lectures 1-2 argue for transworld identity via rigid designation and against counterpart theory.

## Contemporary Works

Plantinga, Alvin. *The Nature of Necessity*. Oxford University Press, 1974. Defends transworld identity and individual essences (haecceities) against Lewis's counterpart theory.

Sider, Theodore. "Reductive Theories of Modality." In Loux and Zimmerman (eds.), *Oxford Handbook of Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2003. A survey of approaches to modality including counterpart theory.

Forbes, Graeme. *The Metaphysics of Modality*. Oxford University Press, 1985. A book-length treatment of de re modality and transworld identity.

Williamson, Timothy. *Modal Logic as Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2013. Defends necessitism — the view that everything that exists, exists necessarily — with implications for transworld identity.
