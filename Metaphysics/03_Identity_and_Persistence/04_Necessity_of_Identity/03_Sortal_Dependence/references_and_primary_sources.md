# References and Primary Sources: Sortal Dependence

## Foundational Texts

Wiggins, David. *Sameness and Substance Renewed*. Cambridge University Press, 2001. Defends D(x,y): x and y are identical iff there is a sortal F such that x and y are the same F.

Geach, Peter T. *Reference and Generality*. Cornell University Press, 1962. The original statement of the relative identity thesis.

## Contemporary Works

Lowe, E.J. *The Possibility of Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 1998. Ch. 3 develops a sortalist account of identity within a four-category ontology.

Noonan, Harold. "Relative Identity." In Craig (ed.), *Routledge Encyclopedia of Philosophy*. Routledge, 1998. A survey of relative identity and its implications.

Gallois, Andre. *Occasions of Identity*. Oxford University Press, 1998. Defends a view on which identity can hold contingently.

Hawthorne, John. "Identity." In *Metaphysical Essays*. Oxford University Press, 2006. Reviews the main debates over relative identity and sortal dependence.
