**Argument for perdurantism (Lewis — temporary intrinsics):**
- P1: If endurantism is true, I am wholly present at t1 (when straight) and at t2 (when bent).
- P2: The property *being straight* is incompatible with *being bent*.
- P3: An object wholly present at two times must have the same properties at both times.
- C: Contradiction — I cannot be both straight and bent. Perdurantism avoids this by attributing straightness to my t1-stage and bentness to my t2-stage.
