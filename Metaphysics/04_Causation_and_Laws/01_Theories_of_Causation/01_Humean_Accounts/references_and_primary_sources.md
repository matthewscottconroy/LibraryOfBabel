# References and Primary Sources: Humean Accounts of Causation

## Foundational Texts

Hume, David. *A Treatise of Human Nature*, Book I, Part III. Ed. Selby-Bigge. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1978. The original regularity analysis of causation and the discovery that no impression corresponds to necessary connection.

Mackie, J.L. *The Cement of the Universe*. Oxford University Press, 1974. The INUS condition analysis: causes as parts of sufficient conditions.

## Contemporary Works

Lewis, David. "Causation." *Journal of Philosophy* 70 (1973): 556-567. Reprinted in *Philosophical Papers*, vol. 2. The counterfactual analysis: c causes e iff if c had not occurred, e would not have occurred.

Lewis, David. "Causation as Influence." *Journal of Philosophy* 97 (2000): 182-197. A revised counterfactual analysis that handles pre-emption and over-determination.

Menzies, Peter. "Causation: Influencing and Producing." In Collins, Hall, and Paul (eds.), *Causation and Counterfactuals*. MIT Press, 2004. A survey of Humean and counterfactual approaches.

Psillos, Stathis. *Causation and Explanation*. McGill-Queen's University Press, 2002. A comprehensive treatment of the regularity, INUS, and counterfactual traditions.
