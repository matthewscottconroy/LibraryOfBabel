# Further Reading: Humean and Regularity Theories

## Primary Sources

- Hume, David. *A Treatise of Human Nature*, Book I, Part III. (1739)
- Hume, David. *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding*, Section VII. (1748)
- Mill, John Stuart. *A System of Logic*, Book III. (1843)
- Mackie, J.L. "Causes and Conditions." *American Philosophical Quarterly* 2 (1965): 245–264.
- Mackie, J.L. *The Cement of the Universe*. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1974.
- Russell, Bertrand. "On the Notion of Cause." *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society* 13 (1912): 1–26.

## Secondary Sources and Commentaries

- Beauchamp, Tom L., and Alexander Rosenberg. *Hume and the Problem of Causation*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1981.
- Garrett, Don. *Cognition and Commitment in Hume's Philosophy*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1997.
- Stroud, Barry. *Hume*. London: Routledge, 1977.
- Sosa, Ernest, and Michael Tooley (eds.). *Causation*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1993. [Includes Mackie's "Causes and Conditions" and other key papers]

## Contemporary Assessments

- Beebee, Helen. *Hume on Causation*. London: Routledge, 2006.
- Psillos, Stathis. *Causation and Explanation*. Chesham: Acumen, 2002.
- Woodward, James. *Making Things Happen*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2003. [Critical of regularity theories, but excellent overview]
