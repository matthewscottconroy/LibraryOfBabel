# References and Primary Sources: Humean and Regularity Theories

## Foundational Texts

Hume, David. *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding*, §VII. Ed. Beauchamp. Oxford University Press, 1999. The accessible second statement of Hume's analysis of causation.

Mill, John Stuart. *A System of Logic*, Book III, chs. 8-10. Collected Works, vol. 7. University of Toronto Press, 1974. The methods of inductive inference for causal discovery.

## Contemporary Works

Mackie, J.L. *The Cement of the Universe*. Oxford University Press, 1974. The comprehensive treatment of regularity theory via INUS conditions.

Armstrong, D.M. *What Is a Law of Nature?* Cambridge University Press, 1983. Defends a non-Humean account of laws while engaging systematically with regularity theories.

Lewis, David. "Humean Supervenience Debugged." *Mind* 103 (1994): 473-490. A defense of Humeanism about laws and causation.

Sober, Elliott. *Evidence and Evolution*. Cambridge University Press, 2008. Contains a clear treatment of how regularity and probabilistic notions of causation apply in science.
