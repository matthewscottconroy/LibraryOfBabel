# Further Reading: Counterfactual Theories

## Primary Sources

- Lewis, David. "Causation." *Journal of Philosophy* 70 (1973): 556–567.
- Lewis, David. "Causation as Influence." *Journal of Philosophy* 97 (2000): 182–197.
- Lewis, David. *Counterfactuals*. Oxford: Blackwell, 1973.
- Woodward, James. *Making Things Happen: A Theory of Causal Explanation*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2003.
- Pearl, Judea. *Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference*. 2nd ed. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2009.

## Key Articles

- Hitchcock, Christopher. "The Intransitivity of Causation Revealed in Equations and Graphs." *Journal of Philosophy* 98 (2001): 273–299.
- Collins, John, Ned Hall, and L.A. Paul (eds.). *Causation and Counterfactuals*. Cambridge: MIT Press, 2004. [Anthology with key papers on preemption, overdetermination, and Lewis's framework]

## Secondary Literature

- Menzies, Peter. "Counterfactual Theories of Causation." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2014.
- Beebee, Helen, Christopher Hitchcock, and Peter Menzies (eds.). *The Oxford Handbook of Causation*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2009.
- Psillos, Stathis. *Causation and Explanation*. Chesham: Acumen, 2002.
