# References and Primary Sources: Powers and Mechanisms

## Foundational Texts

Harré, Rom and Madden, E.H. *Causal Powers*. Blackwell, 1975. An early systematic defense of a powers-based account of causation as distinct from regularity.

Salmon, Wesley. *Scientific Explanation and the Causal Structure of the World*. Princeton University Press, 1984. Develops the process theory of causation: causes are connected to effects by continuous causal processes.

## Contemporary Works

Martin, C.B. *The Mind in Nature*. Oxford University Press, 2008. Powerful qualities and mutual manifestation partners in causation.

Mumford, Stephen and Anjum, Rani Lill. *Getting Causes from Powers*. Oxford University Press, 2011. Causation as mutual manifestation of powers: a direct alternative to Humean and counterfactual accounts.

Bunge, Mario. *Causality and Modern Science*. 3rd ed. Dover, 1979. A comprehensive treatment of causal ontology from a scientific realist perspective.

Machamer, Peter, Darden, Lindley, and Craver, Carl. "Thinking about Mechanisms." *Philosophy of Science* 67 (2000): 1-25. The canonical statement of the mechanistic account of scientific explanation.
