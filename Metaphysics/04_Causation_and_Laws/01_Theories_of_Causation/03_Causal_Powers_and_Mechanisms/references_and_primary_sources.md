# References and Primary Sources: Causal Powers and Mechanisms (II)

## Foundational Texts

Molnar, George. *Powers: A Study in Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2003. The posthumous systematic treatment of irreducible dispositional properties and their role in causation.

Ellis, Brian. *Scientific Essentialism*. Cambridge University Press, 2001. Argues that natural properties are essentially dispositional and that laws flow from their natures.

## Contemporary Works

Bird, Alexander. *Nature's Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Pandispositionalism: all fundamental natural properties are dispositional; categorical properties are not required.

Chakravartty, Anjan. *A Metaphysics for Scientific Realism*. Cambridge University Press, 2007. Develops a semirealist view grounding causation in powers of posited scientific entities.

Jacobs, Jonathan D. "A Powers Theory of Modality." *Philosophical Studies* 151 (2010): 227-248. Uses powers ontology to ground modal claims about causation and natural necessity.

Cartwright, Nancy. *Nature's Capacities and Their Measurement*. Oxford University Press, 1989. Defends a capacities view of causal powers in scientific contexts.
