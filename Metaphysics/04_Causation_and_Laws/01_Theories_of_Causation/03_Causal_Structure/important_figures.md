# Important Figures: Causal Structure

## Judea Pearl (born 1936)
Computer scientist and philosopher who developed the modern mathematical framework for causal reasoning: directed acyclic graphs (DAGs), do-calculus, and the structural causal model. Pearl's work provides a rigorous framework for identifying causal structure from statistical data.

## Wesley Salmon (1925–2001)
Developed the process theory of causation: causation involves spatiotemporally continuous processes that transmit a conserved quantity (energy-momentum). Causal processes (as opposed to pseudo-processes) can transmit marks; causal structure consists of interlocking causal processes and interactions.

## Christopher Hitchcock (born 1964)
Has developed sophisticated accounts of the relationship between causal structure and causal explanation, including contrastive causation (causation is always relative to a contrast class) and token vs. type causation.

## James Woodward (born 1946)
Developed the interventionist account of causation and causal structure: X is a cause of Y if an intervention on X would change Y. Causal structure is revealed by the pattern of what would happen under various interventions.
