# References and Primary Sources: Causal Structure

## Foundational Texts

Pearl, Judea. *Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference*. Cambridge University Press, 2000. The foundational text on the structural equation / directed acyclic graph (DAG) approach to causation.

Spirtes, Peter, Glymour, Clark, and Scheines, Richard. *Causation, Prediction, and Search*. 2nd ed. MIT Press, 2000. Develops algorithms for inferring causal structure from statistical data.

## Contemporary Works

Woodward, James. *Making Things Happen*. Oxford University Press, 2003. The interventionist analysis using structural equations to model causation.

Hitchcock, Christopher. "Prevention, Preemption, and the Principle of Sufficient Reason." *Philosophical Review* 116 (2007): 495-532. Analyzes causal structure in cases of prevention and preemption.

Schaffer, Jonathan. "Causes Need Not Be Physically Connected to Their Effects." In Hitchcock (ed.), *Contemporary Debates in Philosophy of Science*. Blackwell, 2004. Discusses causation by absences within a structural framework.

Hall, Ned. "Two Concepts of Causation." In Collins, Hall, and Paul (eds.), *Causation and Counterfactuals*. MIT Press, 2004. Distinguishes dependence-causation from production-causation.
