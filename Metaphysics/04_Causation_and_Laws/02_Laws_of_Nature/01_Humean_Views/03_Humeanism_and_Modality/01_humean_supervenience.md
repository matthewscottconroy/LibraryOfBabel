# Humean Supervenience

There is a powerful and economical picture of what the world contains: particular things at particular locations in spacetime, each with their locally determined intrinsic properties, and nothing else. No necessities connecting one thing to another across space or time. No causal powers residing in objects over and above their patterns of behavior. No laws governing things, over and above the regularities those things exhibit. Everything else — causation, laws, chance, possibility — supervenes on this pattern of local, intrinsic, qualitative facts. This is Humean supervenience, and it is the foundational commitment behind Lewis's entire metaphysical program.

## The Thesis Stated

Lewis introduced the thesis in *Philosophical Papers*, Volume II (1986) as the framework unifying his accounts of laws, causation, chance, and modality.

**Humean Supervenience (HS)**: For any two possible worlds W₁ and W₂ that are duplicates with respect to the distribution of local, intrinsic, qualitative properties across their spacetime points, W₁ and W₂ are duplicates with respect to all their properties — including their laws, causal relations, counterfactuals, and objective chances.

The key terms need unpacking. A property is **local** iff it is instantiated at a spacetime point or region, not spread across distant regions. A property is **intrinsic** iff it belongs to a thing independently of what other things exist or how things are related — it is not a relational property. A property is **qualitative** iff it does not involve particular individuals: "being Napoleon" is not qualitative, "being a general who conquered Europe" is. The Humean mosaic is the complete distribution of properties satisfying all three conditions across all spacetime points.

Two features of the mosaic are decisive. First, no necessary connections: any combination of local properties at different points is possible. There is no metaphysical constraint preventing point p from having property F while point q has property G, regardless of what other points have. Second, completeness: all the facts about the world are fixed by the mosaic. Fix the mosaic and you fix the laws, the causal relations, the counterfactuals, and the chances — everything supervenes.

## The Research Program

Lewis was explicit that HS is not a proven thesis but a research program: show that all apparently non-Humean facts can be analyzed as patterns in the mosaic. His own analyses — laws as Best System theorems, causation as counterfactual dependence, chance as what appears in the best system for an indeterministic world, modality as quantification over possible worlds — were designed to vindicate HS by reducing each of these to features of the mosaic. The success of the program requires an adequate account of natural properties to specify the qualitative character of the mosaic, successful analyses of each target concept, and resistance to challenges from physics and metaphysics.

The deepest commitment is Hume's own: no necessary connection between distinct existences. Any correlation between distinct points in the mosaic is contingent — it holds in the actual world but could have been otherwise. Laws are patterns in the mosaic, not necessities imposed on it. Causation is a relation among events, analyzed in terms of counterfactual dependence between those events, not in terms of any productive power flowing from one to the other. What is possible or necessary is determined by the total mosaic and the space of possible worlds, not by primitive de re modality over and above the actual facts.

## The Challenges

Quantum mechanics presents the most pressing empirical challenge. Quantum states are not local: an entangled two-particle state cannot be decomposed into a product of individual particle states. The state of an entangled pair is a holistic, non-local property of the pair, not determined by the local properties of each particle separately. This violates Lewis's foundational assumption that all fundamental properties are local and intrinsic. Lewis acknowledged this with characteristic candor: "If I am wrong about [local properties being qualitative and intrinsic], again so much the worse for Humean supervenience: it would merely be contingently actual, not necessary." Recent Humeans — Esfeld, Ney, Albert — have explored whether quantum mechanics can be recast in locally supervenient form, for example by treating the wavefunction on configuration space as the fundamental ontology.

The causal powers objection attacks from within. If properties are essentially dispositional — if having charge is constitutively the power to attract and repel — then properties carry modal character in their very natures, not merely as patterns in the mosaic. This need not be directly incompatible with HS, but it suggests that the local properties of the mosaic already contain the modal character that HS wanted to explain away. The picture of a world of "bare" local properties from which everything modal supervenes becomes difficult to maintain if the local properties themselves are shot through with dispositionality.

Maudlin presses the governing objection: laws are fundamental primitives that govern the mosaic, not patterns in it. They make the mosaic be what it is. If Maudlin is right, the Humean gets the direction of explanation backwards — not the mosaic determining the laws, but the laws determining the mosaic's character. This is directly incompatible with HS and cannot be accommodated by any refinement of it.

Whether HS is defensible is one of the genuinely contested questions in contemporary metaphysics. It represents the most systematic articulation of empiricist, reductionist metaphysics — the attempt to do without primitive necessities, causal powers, and governing laws. Whether such a world is possible, and whether the actual world is like this, are questions that physics and metaphysics are jointly addressing and have not yet settled.
