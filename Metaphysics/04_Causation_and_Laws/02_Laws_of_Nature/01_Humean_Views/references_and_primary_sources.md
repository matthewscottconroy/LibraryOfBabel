# References and Primary Sources: Humean Views of Laws

## Foundational Texts

Hume, David. *A Treatise of Human Nature*, Book I, Part III, §XIV. Ed. Selby-Bigge. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1978. The critique of necessary connection and the regularity account.

Lewis, David. "New Work for a Theory of Universals." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* 61 (1983): 343-377. Introduces and defends the Best System Analysis of laws.

## Contemporary Works

Lewis, David. "Humean Supervenience Debugged." *Mind* 103 (1994): 473-490. Defends Humean supervenience against objections from chance and laws.

Loewer, Barry. "Humean Supervenience." *Philosophical Topics* 24 (1996): 101-127. A defense and development of Humeanism about laws.

Beebee, Helen. "The Non-Governing Conception of Laws of Nature." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 61 (2000): 571-594. An analysis of the Humean view on which laws describe but do not govern.

Cohen, Jonathan and Callender, Craig. "A Better Best System Account of Laws." *Philosophical Studies* 145 (2009): 1-34. A refinement of the BSA that allows multiple systems.
