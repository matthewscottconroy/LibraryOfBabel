# References and Primary Sources: Regularity and Necessity in Laws

## Foundational Texts

Armstrong, D.M. *What Is a Law of Nature?* Cambridge University Press, 1983. The classic challenge to Humean regularity theories; defends necessity relations between universals as the ontological basis of laws.

Dretske, Fred. "Laws of Nature." *Philosophy of Science* 44 (1977): 248-268. An early defense of the view that laws relate universals and are not mere regularities.

## Contemporary Works

Carroll, John. *Laws of Nature*. Cambridge University Press, 1994. Defends primitivism about laws: laws are fundamental and cannot be reduced to Humean regularities.

Tooley, Michael. "The Nature of Laws." *Canadian Journal of Philosophy* 7 (1977): 667-698. Defends the view that laws are higher-order relations between universals.

Bird, Alexander. *Nature's Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Connects necessity in laws to the essentialist theory of properties.

Maudlin, Tim. *The Metaphysics within Physics*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Argues that laws are primitive and fundamental, not reducible to either Humean supervenience or universals.
