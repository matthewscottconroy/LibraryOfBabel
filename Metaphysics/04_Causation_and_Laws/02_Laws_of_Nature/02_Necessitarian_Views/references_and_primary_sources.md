# References and Primary Sources: Necessitarian Views of Laws

## Foundational Texts

Armstrong, D.M. *What Is a Law of Nature?* Cambridge University Press, 1983. The necessitation theory.

Ellis, Brian. *Scientific Essentialism*. Cambridge University Press, 2001. Essential natures of properties ground lawlike necessities.

## Contemporary Works

Bird, Alexander. *Nature's Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Pandispositionalism and natural necessity.

Shoemaker, Sydney. "Causality and Properties." In van Inwagen (ed.), *Time and Cause*. Reidel, 1980. Property identity via causal roles — a precursor to bird's necessitarianism.

Koons, Robert. *Realism Regained*. Oxford University Press, 2000. A metaphysical realism that includes necessary connections in nature.

Chakravartty, Anjan. *A Metaphysics for Scientific Realism*. Cambridge University Press, 2007. Grounds lawlike necessity in the powers of theoretical entities.
