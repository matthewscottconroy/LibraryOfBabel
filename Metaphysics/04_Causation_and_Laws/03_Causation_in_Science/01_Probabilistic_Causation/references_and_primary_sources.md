# References and Primary Sources: Probabilistic Causation

## Foundational Texts

Suppes, Patrick. *A Probabilistic Theory of Causality*. North-Holland, 1970. The first systematic probabilistic theory of causation.

Salmon, Wesley. *Statistical Explanation and Statistical Relevance*. University of Pittsburgh Press, 1971. Develops probabilistic relevance as a criterion for causal explanation.

## Contemporary Works

Mellor, D.H. *The Facts of Causation*. Routledge, 1995. A systematic treatment of causation using probability and facts.

Hitchcock, Christopher. "The Intransitivity of Causation Revealed in Equations and Graphs." *Journal of Philosophy* 98 (2001): 273-299. Analyzes probabilistic causation using structural equations.

Cartwright, Nancy. *How the Laws of Physics Lie*. Oxford University Press, 1983. Argues for a capacity view of causal laws that handles probabilistic cases.

Williamson, Jon. *Bayesian Nets and Causality*. Oxford University Press, 2005. A Bayesian and objective epistemic approach to probabilistic causation.
