# References and Primary Sources: Causal Explanation

## Foundational Texts

Hempel, Carl and Oppenheim, Paul. "Studies in the Logic of Explanation." *Philosophy of Science* 15 (1948): 135-175. The D-N (deductive-nomological) model: explanation is deduction of the explanandum from laws and initial conditions.

Salmon, Wesley. *Scientific Explanation and the Causal Structure of the World*. Princeton University Press, 1984. Argues against the D-N model; explanation requires identifying the causal mechanisms behind phenomena.

## Contemporary Works

Woodward, James. *Making Things Happen*. Oxford University Press, 2003. The interventionist theory of causal explanation: to explain is to identify factors that would make a difference if manipulated.

Strevens, Michael. *Depth: An Account of Scientific Explanation*. Harvard University Press, 2008. Defends the kairetic account: causal explanation abstracts from causally irrelevant differences.

Kitcher, Philip. "Explanatory Unification and the Causal Structure of the World." In Kitcher and Salmon (eds.), *Scientific Explanation*. Minnesota Studies in the Philosophy of Science, vol. 13. 1989. The unificationist account: explanation increases the unity of science.

Machamer, Peter, Darden, Lindley, and Craver, Carl. "Thinking about Mechanisms." *Philosophy of Science* 67 (2000): 1-25. The mechanistic account of biological explanation.
