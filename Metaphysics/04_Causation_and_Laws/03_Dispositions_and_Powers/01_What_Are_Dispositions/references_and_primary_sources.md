# References and Primary Sources: What Are Dispositions?

## Foundational Texts

Ryle, Gilbert. *The Concept of Mind*. Hutchinson, 1949. The philosophical introduction of dispositions as the correct analysis of mental concepts; dispositions are analyzed via "if...then" conditionals.

Goodman, Nelson. *Fact, Fiction, and Forecast*. Harvard University Press, 1955. The projection problem and the grue paradox — shows the difficulty of grounding counterfactuals and dispositions.

## Contemporary Works

Martin, C.B. "Dispositions and Conditionals." *Philosophical Quarterly* 44 (1994): 1-8. Introduces finkish dispositions as counterexamples to the conditional analysis.

Lewis, David. "Finkish Dispositions." *Philosophical Quarterly* 47 (1997): 143-158. Lewis's response to Martin using the notion of intrinsic versus extrinsic bases.

Mumford, Stephen. *Dispositions*. Oxford University Press, 1998. A comprehensive study of the metaphysics of dispositions.

Bird, Alexander. "Dispositional Supervenience." In Marmodoro (ed.), *The Metaphysics of Powers*. Routledge, 2010. Defends a supervenience account of dispositions.
