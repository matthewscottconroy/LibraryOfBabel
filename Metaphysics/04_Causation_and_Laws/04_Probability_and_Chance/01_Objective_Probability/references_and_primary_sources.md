# References and Primary Sources: Objective Probability

## Foundational Texts

von Mises, Richard. *Probability, Statistics, and Truth*. 2nd English ed. Dover, 1981. The systematic frequentist account of probability as limiting relative frequency.

Lewis, David. "A Subjectivist's Guide to Objective Chance." In *Philosophical Papers*, vol. 2. Oxford University Press, 1986. Introduces the Principal Principle connecting objective chance to rational credence.

## Contemporary Works

Loewer, Barry. "Determinism and Chance." *Studies in History and Philosophy of Modern Physics* 32 (2001): 609-620. Discusses how objective chances can exist in a deterministic world.

Eagle, Antony. "Chance versus Randomness." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2021. A careful analysis of objectivity in probability.

Gillies, Donald. *Philosophical Theories of Probability*. Routledge, 2000. An accessible overview of frequentism, propensity, subjectivism, and logical probability.

Humphreys, Paul. *The Chances of Explanation*. Princeton University Press, 1989. Analyzes the propensity interpretation and its application in scientific contexts.
