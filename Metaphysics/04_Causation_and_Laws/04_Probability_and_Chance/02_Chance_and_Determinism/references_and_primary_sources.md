# References and Primary Sources: Chance and Determinism

## Foundational Texts

Laplace, Pierre-Simon. *A Philosophical Essay on Probabilities*. Trans. Truscott and Emory. Dover, 1996. The classic statement of determinism as a scientific ideal and the role of probability as a measure of ignorance.

Lewis, David. "Humean Supervenience Debugged." *Mind* 103 (1994): 473-490. Addresses the problem of fitting objective chances into a deterministic Humean framework.

## Contemporary Works

Loewer, Barry. "Determinism and Chance." *Studies in History and Philosophy of Modern Physics* 32 (2001): 609-620. Develops Lewis's approach to chance under determinism.

Albert, David Z. *Time and Chance*. Harvard University Press, 2000. Analyzes the relationship between the asymmetry of time, determinism, and the probabilities of statistical mechanics.

Earman, John. *A Primer on Determinism*. Reidel, 1986. The authoritative philosophical treatment of determinism in classical and quantum physics.

Eagle, Antony. *Philosophy of Probability and Statistical Modelling*. Cambridge University Press, 2021. A comprehensive treatment of probability in deterministic and indeterministic settings.
