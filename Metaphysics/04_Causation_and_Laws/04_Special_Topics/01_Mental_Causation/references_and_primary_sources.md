# References and Primary Sources: Mental Causation

## Foundational Texts

Davidson, Donald. "Mental Events." In *Essays on Actions and Events*. Oxford University Press, 1980. Anomalous monism: mental events are physical events, but mental-physical type identities are denied.

Kim, Jaegwon. *Mind in a Physical World*. MIT Press, 1998. The exclusion argument and functionalism — the challenge for non-reductive physicalism.

## Contemporary Works

Yablo, Stephen. "Mental Causation." *Philosophical Review* 101 (1992): 245-280. The proportionality solution to the exclusion problem.

Fodor, Jerry. "Making Mind Matter More." *Philosophical Topics* 17 (1989): 59-79. An argument that mental properties must be causally efficacious to be psychologically real.

Crane, Tim. "Mental Causation." In *The Contents of Experience*, ed. Crane. Cambridge University Press, 1992. A survey of the issues.

Heil, John and Mele, Alfred (eds.). *Mental Causation*. Oxford University Press, 1993. An essential anthology covering exclusion, anomalous monism, and non-reductive physicalism.
