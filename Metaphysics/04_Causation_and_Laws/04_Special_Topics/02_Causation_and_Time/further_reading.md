# Further Reading: Special Topics in Causation

## Mental Causation

- Kim, Jaegwon. *Supervenience and Mind*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1993.
- Kim, Jaegwon. *Mind in a Physical World*. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1998.
- Yablo, Stephen. "Mental Causation." *Philosophical Review* 101 (1992): 245–280.
- Fodor, Jerry. "Making Mind Matter More." *Philosophical Topics* 17 (1989): 59–79.
- Burge, Tyler. "Mind-Body Causation and Explanatory Practice." In *Mental Causation*, edited by Heil and Mele. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1993.

## Causation and Time

- Dummett, Michael. "Bringing About the Past." *Philosophical Review* 73 (1964): 338–359.
- Price, Huw. *Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1996.
- Lewis, David. "Counterfactual Dependence and Time's Arrow." *Noûs* 13 (1979): 455–476.
- Maudlin, Tim. *Quantum Non-Locality and Relativity*. 3rd ed. Oxford: Wiley-Blackwell, 2011.

## General

- Beebee, Helen, Christopher Hitchcock, and Peter Menzies (eds.). *The Oxford Handbook of Causation*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2009.
