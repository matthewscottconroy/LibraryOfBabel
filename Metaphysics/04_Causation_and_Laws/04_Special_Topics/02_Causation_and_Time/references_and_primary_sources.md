# References and Primary Sources: Causation and Time

## Foundational Texts

Reichenbach, Hans. *The Direction of Time*. University of California Press, 1956. Argues that the asymmetry of causation explains (or is explained by) the arrow of time; introduces the "common cause principle."

Price, Huw. *Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point*. Oxford University Press, 1996. A sophisticated analysis of the temporal asymmetry of causation from a two-perspectival standpoint.

## Contemporary Works

Maudlin, Tim. *The Metaphysics within Physics*. Oxford University Press, 2007. Contains a discussion of how laws of nature relate to the direction of time and causation.

Albert, David Z. *Time and Chance*. Harvard University Press, 2000. Analyzes the thermodynamic arrow of time and its relationship to causal asymmetry.

Kutach, Douglas. *Causation and Its Basis in Fundamental Physics*. Oxford University Press, 2013. Defends a fundamental physics-based account of causation and its temporal direction.

Tooley, Michael. *Time, Tense, and Causation*. Oxford University Press, 1997. A comprehensive treatment of the metaphysics of time, tense, and causation.
