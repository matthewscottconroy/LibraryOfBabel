## Map 2: The Preemption Problem and Its Solutions

```
PREEMPTION SCENARIO:
Billy throws (B) → B-rock arrives first → window breaks (W)
Suzy also throws (S) → S-rock would have broken W anyway

Question: Does W counterfactually depend on B?
Answer: NO (had B not thrown, Suzy would have broken W)
BUT: B caused W (by commonsense judgment)
         │
LEWIS'S SOLUTIONS:
         │
  ┌──────┴──────────────────────────────┐
  │                                     │
QUASI-DEPENDENCE (1973):          INFLUENCE (2000):
Find a chain of counterfactual    W depends on
dependence from cause to          the manner/time of B;
effect via intermediates          small differences in B
                                  would produce differences in W
         │                                     │
OBJECTION:                        OBJECTION (Schaffer):
Fails for "late preemption"       "Trumping preemption" — two
where the preemptor's effect      identical spells cast simultaneously;
and the backup coincide           first caster's spell prevails;
                                  no "influence" difference exists
         │
SCHAFFER'S RESPONSE:
Contrastive causation — B caused W (rather than nothing)
in contrast with S's throw causing W (rather than nothing)
```
