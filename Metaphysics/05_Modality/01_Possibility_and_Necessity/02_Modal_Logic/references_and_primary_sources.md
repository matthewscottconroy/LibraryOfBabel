# References and Primary Sources: Modal Logic

## Foundational Texts

- Lewis, C.I. and Langford, C.H., *Symbolic Logic* (1932). The classic presentation of the S1–S5 modal systems, motivated by the inadequacies of material implication and introducing strict implication as the proper logical connective for entailment.

- Kripke, Saul, "Semantical Considerations on Modal Logic," *Acta Philosophica Fennica* 16 (1963). Presents the relational semantics for modal logic, defining Kripke frames and establishing the completeness of standard modal systems.

- Barcan Marcus, Ruth, "A Functional Calculus of First Order Based on Strict Implication," *Journal of Symbolic Logic* 11 (1946). Introduces quantified modal logic and derives the Barcan formula, opening enduring debates about existence and modality.

## Contemporary Works

- Blackburn, Patrick, de Rijke, Maarten, and Venema, Yde, *Modal Logic* (2001). The standard advanced textbook covering relational semantics, completeness, decidability, and complexity for a wide range of modal systems.

- Chellas, Brian, *Modal Logic: An Introduction* (1980). An accessible and rigorous introduction to propositional modal logic covering the main systems, their axiomatizations, and their Kripke semantics.
