# References and Primary Sources: Lewis's Modal Realism

## Foundational Texts

- Lewis, David, *On the Plurality of Worlds* (1986). The definitive statement of modal realism; argues for the literal existence of infinitely many concrete possible worlds, defends the account against objections, and shows how it grounds modal, counterfactual, and propositional notions.

- Lewis, David, *Counterfactuals* (1973). Introduces the possible-worlds analysis of counterfactual conditionals that motivates much of Lewis's metaphysics; a counterfactual is true if the consequent holds in the closest worlds where the antecedent holds.

- Lewis, David, "Counterpart Theory and Quantified Modal Logic," *Journal of Philosophy* 65 (1968). Argues that de re modal claims about individuals are analyzed in terms of counterparts in other worlds rather than transworld identity.

## Contemporary Works

- Divers, John, *Possible Worlds* (2002). A comprehensive critical survey of theories of possible worlds, with especially careful treatment of Lewis's modal realism and the alternatives.

- Nolan, Daniel, *David Lewis* (2005). A clear and thorough guide to Lewis's philosophy, including his metaphysics of modality, causation, and mind.
