# Important Figures: Actualism

## Alvin Plantinga (1932–present)
The leading defender of actualist modal metaphysics; argues that possible worlds are maximal possible states of affairs—abstract entities that exist actually but are only partially instantiated. His account preserves robust modal realism while rejecting Lewis's concrete worlds.

## Robert Adams (born 1937)
Developed actualism in terms of world-stories or maximally consistent sets of propositions, arguing that possibilities are grounded in the actual world's abstract structure rather than in concrete alternative universes.

## Kit Fine (born 1946)
Made major contributions to actualist metaphysics through his work on essence, which grounds modal facts in the natures of actually existing things rather than in possible-worlds structure. His approach offers an alternative grounding for necessity without Lewis's ontology.

## Peter van Inwagen (born 1942)
A vigorous critic of possibilism and defender of actualism; his work on existence and ontology argues that quantification always ranges over what actually exists and that modal claims can be understood without commitment to non-actual entities.
