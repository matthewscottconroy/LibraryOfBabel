# Important Figures: Modal Knowledge in Practice

## Stephen Yablo (born 1957)
Developed an influential account of modal knowledge through imaginative simulation—conceiving of possible situations by imagining a world and verifying that P obtains within it. His work provides a naturalistic account of how modal knowledge is actually exercised in philosophical practice.

## Tamar Szabó Gendler (born 1966)
Examined the role of intuition pumps and thought experiments in generating modal knowledge, distinguishing cases where imaginative exercises genuinely track modal facts from cases where they mislead. Her work connects modal epistemology to the psychology of imagination.

## Peter Strawson (1919–2006)
Though not primarily a modal epistemologist, Strawson's method of conceptual analysis exemplifies the appeal to intuitions about cases that philosophers rely on to establish modal claims. His work on descriptive metaphysics illustrates how ordinary conceptual competence can ground modal knowledge.

## Frank Jackson (born 1943)
Defended the method of conceptual analysis as a way of accessing modal truths about ordinary concepts. On his account, a priori reflection on concepts reveals what is possible or necessary by the lights of those concepts, which in turn reflects modal reality.
