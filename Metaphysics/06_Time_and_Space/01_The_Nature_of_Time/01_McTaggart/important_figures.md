# Important Figures: McTaggart and the Nature of Time

**J.M.E. McTaggart (1866–1925)** introduced the A-series/B-series distinction and the argument for the unreality of time in "The Unreality of Time" (1908). His *The Nature of Existence* (1921) provides the full metaphysical context.

**D.C. Williams (1899–1983)** defended the B-theory in "The Myth of Passage" (1951), arguing that temporal passage is an illusion arising from our perspective in the four-dimensional block universe.

**Michael Dummett (1925–2011)** engaged deeply with the A-theory, developing the concept of "backward causation" and contributing to the philosophy of time in relation to anti-realism.

**Arthur Prior (1914–1969)** developed tense logic as a formal framework for A-theory metaphysics. His *Time and Tense* (1968) and *Papers on Time and Tense* (1968) are foundational texts.

**Ned Markosian (b. 1963)** is a prominent contemporary defender of presentism, developing arguments for the view in "A Defense of Presentism" (2004) and elsewhere.

**Ted Sider (b. 1967)** has defended four-dimensionalism (eternalism combined with temporal parts) in *Four-Dimensionalism* (2001), connecting the philosophy of time to the metaphysics of persistence.

**Craig Callender (b. 1968)** has written extensively on the philosophy of time, temporal passage, and the connections between physics and the metaphysics of time.
