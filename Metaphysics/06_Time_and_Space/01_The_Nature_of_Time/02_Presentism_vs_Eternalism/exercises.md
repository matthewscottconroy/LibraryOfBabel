# Exercises and Thought Experiments: Presentism Versus Eternalism

## Comprehension Questions

1. State the difference between presentism, eternalism, and the growing block theory. What does each view say about the ontological status of past and future events?

2. What is the special relativity objection to presentism? How do presentists respond?

3. Explain the "epistemic problem" for the growing block theory. Why is this problem distinctive to growing block and not to eternalism?

## Discussion Questions

4. The eternalist says the future "already exists." Does this threaten free will? Can we freely choose among futures that already exist in the block universe?

5. The presentist says only the present exists. But what is the present? Is it a dimensionless point (an instant) or a finite interval? And if it is a point, how can anything happen in the present?

6. Is there a principled way to choose between presentism and eternalism based on physics, or is the choice ultimately metaphysical?

## Thought Experiments

7. **The Persisting Table**: A table exists now but not in the distant future (it decays). If eternalism is true, the table exists at some spacetime region but not others. Is this a coherent picture of the table's existence?

8. **Yesterday's Dinosaurs**: If presentism is true, dinosaurs do not exist. What makes it true that dinosaurs once existed? What are the "presentist truthmakers" for past-tense sentences?

9. **The Growing Block and Consciousness**: If you are at the "leading edge" of the growing block, you experience the present moment as special. But entities in the middle of the block also experience their moment as the present. Is there any way to tell, from the inside, whether you are really at the edge?
