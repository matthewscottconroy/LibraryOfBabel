# Important Figures: Presentism Versus Eternalism

**C.D. Broad (1887–1971)** introduced the growing block theory in *Scientific Thought* (1923), arguing that the past and present exist but the future does not.

**Willard Van Orman Quine (1908–2000)** was an early defender of four-dimensionalism and the block universe in "Identity, Ostension, and Hypostasis" (1950) and elsewhere.

**Ned Markosian (b. 1963)** is a leading contemporary presentist. His papers "A Defense of Presentism" (2004) and "How Fast Does Time Pass?" are key references.

**Dean Zimmerman** has written important work defending presentism and the A-theory, including responses to the relativistic challenges.

**Michael Rea** has developed the compatibilist program, arguing that presentism can be reconciled with special relativity.

**Huw Price (b. 1953)** has argued for the block universe from a naturalistic perspective, connecting the metaphysics of time to the physics of the thermodynamic arrow. *Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point* (1996) is essential.

**David Z. Albert (b. 1954)** has defended a neo-Newtonian approach to time in relation to quantum mechanics, including his "Past Hypothesis" about the initial low-entropy state of the universe. *Time and Chance* (2000) is the key text.
