# References and Primary Sources: Presentism vs. Eternalism

## Foundational Texts

- Sider, Theodore, *Four-Dimensionalism: An Ontology of Persistence and Time* (2001). The canonical defense of eternalism and four-dimensionalism; argues that temporal parts are as real as spatial parts and that the four-dimensional framework resolves major puzzles about persistence and change.

- Zimmerman, Dean, "Presentism and the Space-Time Manifold," in *The Oxford Handbook of Philosophy of Time* (2011). A careful defense of presentism against the objection from special relativity, arguing that presentism can be reconciled with relativistic physics.

- Crisp, Thomas, "Presentism," in *The Oxford Handbook of Metaphysics* (2003). A comprehensive survey of presentist positions, their motivations, and the objections they face from physics and from cross-temporal relations.

## Contemporary Works

- Bourne, Craig, *A Future for Presentism* (2006). Defends a sophisticated version of presentism that accommodates special relativity by adopting a preferred frame without abandoning the ontological priority of the present.

- Callender, Craig (ed.), *Time, Reality and Experience* (2002). Collects important papers on the metaphysics of time with attention to the relationship between philosophical accounts and physical theory.
