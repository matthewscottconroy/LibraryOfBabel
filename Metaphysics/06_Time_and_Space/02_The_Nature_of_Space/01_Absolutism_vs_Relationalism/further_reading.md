# Further Reading: The Nature of Space

## Primary Sources

- Newton, Isaac. *Mathematical Principles of Natural Philosophy* (Principia). 1687. (See the Scholium on absolute space and time)
- Leibniz and Clarke. *The Leibniz-Clarke Correspondence*. Edited by H.G. Alexander. Manchester: Manchester University Press, 1956.
- Mach, Ernst. *The Science of Mechanics*. 1883. Trans. T.J. McCormack. La Salle: Open Court, 1960.

## Modern Treatments

- Earman, John. *World Enough and Space-Time: Absolute versus Relational Theories of Space and Time*. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1989.
- Maudlin, Tim. *The Metaphysics Within Physics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.
- Sklar, Lawrence. *Space, Time, and Spacetime*. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1974.

## Key Articles

- Huggett, Nick. "Absolute and Relational Theories of Space." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2012.
- Norton, John. "The Hole Argument." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2015.
