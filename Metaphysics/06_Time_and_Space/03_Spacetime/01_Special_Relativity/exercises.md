# Exercises and Thought Experiments: Spacetime

## Comprehension Questions

1. What is Minkowski spacetime? How does the spacetime interval differ from spatial distance and temporal duration, and what makes it invariant?

2. Explain the difference between substantivalism and relationalism about spacetime. How does the hole argument challenge manifold substantivalism?

3. What philosophical problems does the relativity of simultaneity create for presentism?

## Discussion Questions

4. Is spacetime a "thing" or merely a way of organizing facts about events? Does this question have a determinate answer, or is it conventional?

5. General relativity makes the metric of spacetime dynamic — it is shaped by matter-energy. Does this support or undermine the view that spacetime is a substantive entity?

6. The "hole argument" shows that the metric field can be "dragged" across the manifold without changing any observable. What does this imply about the identity conditions for spacetime points?

## Thought Experiments

7. **The Twin Paradox**: One twin stays home while the other travels at near-light speed to a distant star and returns. The traveler ages less. Whose "present" is privileged? What does special relativity say about simultaneity?

8. **The Curved Universe**: In general relativity, spacetime can be curved so that traveling in a "straight line" in one direction eventually brings you back to where you started (like the surface of a sphere). If the universe has this topology, what does this imply about the nature of space?

9. **The Big Bang**: Was there space and time before the Big Bang? If the Big Bang is the beginning of spacetime, what does "before" mean in this context?
