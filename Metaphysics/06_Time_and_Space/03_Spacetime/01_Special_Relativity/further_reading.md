# Further Reading: Spacetime

## Primary Sources

- Einstein, Albert. "On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies." (1905) In *The Collected Papers of Albert Einstein*, vol. 2. Princeton: Princeton University Press.
- Minkowski, Hermann. "Space and Time." In *The Principle of Relativity*, trans. W. Perrett and G.B. Jeffery. London: Methuen, 1923.

## Philosophy of Spacetime

- Earman, John. *World Enough and Space-Time: Absolute versus Relational Theories of Space and Time*. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1989.
- Maudlin, Tim. *The Metaphysics Within Physics*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007.
- Sklar, Lawrence. *Space, Time, and Spacetime*. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1974.

## Accessible Introductions

- Huggett, Nick. *Space from Zeno to Einstein*. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1999.
- Callender, Craig. *What Makes Time Special?* Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2017.
