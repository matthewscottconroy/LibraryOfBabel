# Important Figures: Spacetime and Relativity

**Albert Einstein (1879–1955)** developed both special relativity (1905) and general relativity (1915), transforming our understanding of space, time, and gravity. His papers "On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies" (1905) and "The Foundation of the General Theory of Relativity" (1916) are foundational.

**Hermann Minkowski (1864–1909)** reformulated special relativity geometrically in "Space and Time" (1908), introducing Minkowski spacetime and the spacetime interval.

**Henri Poincaré (1854–1912)** made crucial contributions to the mathematics and philosophy of relativity, anticipating some of Einstein's results in "On the Dynamics of the Electron" (1905).

**John Earman (b. 1942)** and **John Norton (b. 1960)** developed the hole argument's implications for spacetime ontology. Earman's *World Enough and Space-Time* (1989) is the standard reference.

**Tim Maudlin (b. 1958)** has written on the metaphysics of spacetime in *The Metaphysics Within Physics* (2007) and on quantum non-locality in *Quantum Non-Locality and Relativity* (1994).

**Harvey Brown** has defended a dynamical (rather than geometric) interpretation of special relativity in *Physical Relativity* (2005).
