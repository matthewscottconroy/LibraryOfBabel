# References and Primary Sources: Special Relativity and Philosophy

## Foundational Texts

- Einstein, Albert, "On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies," *Annalen der Physik* 17 (1905). The original paper presenting special relativity; establishes the relativity of simultaneity, time dilation, and length contraction, eliminating absolute space and time.

- Minkowski, Hermann, "Space and Time" (1908 lecture, published 1909). Presents the four-dimensional spacetime geometry that unifies space and time, showing that special relativity is naturally understood as geometry in a four-dimensional manifold with a Lorentzian metric.

- Sklar, Lawrence, *Space, Time, and Spacetime* (1974). A philosophical classic examining the metaphysical implications of special and general relativity for debates about space, time, and simultaneity.

## Contemporary Works

- Maudlin, Tim, *Quantum Non-Locality and Relativity* (1994). Examines the tension between special relativity and quantum entanglement, with careful attention to what special relativity actually requires metaphysically.

- Norton, John, "The Philosophy of Space and Time," in *Introduction to the Philosophy of Science* edited by Salmon et al. (1992). An accessible survey of philosophical issues raised by relativistic physics.
