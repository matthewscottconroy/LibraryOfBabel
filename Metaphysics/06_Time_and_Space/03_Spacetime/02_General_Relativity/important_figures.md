# Important Figures: General Relativity and Philosophy

## Albert Einstein (1879–1955)
Developed general relativity (1915), showing that gravity is the curvature of spacetime caused by mass-energy. GR transformed the philosophy of space and time by making spacetime a dynamical entity that interacts with its matter content.

## John Earman (born 1942)
Philosopher of physics who provided the most rigorous philosophical analysis of GR's implications for substantivalism and relationalism, developing the "hole argument" as a challenge to substantivalism about spacetime points.

## Tim Maudlin (born 1958)
Developed influential positions on the metaphysics of spacetime structure, arguing for a form of substantivalism that takes the metric field as a genuine physical entity distinct from mere relations between material objects.

## Howard Stein (1929–2022)
Made foundational contributions to the philosophy of spacetime, examining the neo-Newtonian (Galilean) spacetime structures intermediate between absolute Newtonian space and the full spacetime of GR, and clarifying what physical significance accrues to different spatiotemporal structures.
