# References and Primary Sources: General Relativity and Philosophy

## Foundational Texts

- Einstein, Albert, "The Foundation of the General Theory of Relativity," *Annalen der Physik* 49 (1916). The foundational paper presenting GR as the theory of gravity as spacetime curvature; introduces the field equations and the equivalence principle.

- Earman, John, *World Enough and Space-Time* (1989). The definitive philosophical treatment of absolute vs. relational space in the context of both Newtonian mechanics and relativistic spacetime; introduces the hole argument as a problem for substantivalism.

- Friedman, Michael, *Foundations of Space-Time Theories* (1983). A systematic philosophical examination of the foundations of special and general relativity, covering conventionalism, the status of geometry, and the metaphysics of spacetime structure.

## Contemporary Works

- Maudlin, Tim, *Philosophy of Physics: Space and Time* (2012). An accessible yet rigorous introduction to the philosophical issues raised by special and general relativity, defending a realist interpretation of spacetime structure.

- Norton, John, "General Covariance and the Foundations of General Relativity: Eight Decades of Dispute," *Reports on Progress in Physics* 56 (1993). Historical and philosophical examination of the significance of general covariance and the hole argument.
