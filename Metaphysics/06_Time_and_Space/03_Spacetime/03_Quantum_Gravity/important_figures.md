# Important Figures: Quantum Gravity and Philosophy

## Carlo Rovelli (born 1956)
Physicist and philosopher who developed loop quantum gravity and has written extensively on the philosophical implications of quantum gravity, particularly regarding the disappearance of time as a fundamental concept and the relational character of spacetime.

## Julian Barbour (born 1937)
Developed a timeless formulation of dynamics and argued that time is not fundamental but emergent from the configuration space of the universe. His work in *The End of Time* (1999) explores the profound implications of background-independent physics for our understanding of time.

## Lee Smolin (born 1955)
A founder of loop quantum gravity who has written philosophically about the nature of space, time, and laws, arguing in *Time Reborn* (2013) that time is real and fundamental against the timeless block-universe interpretation.

## David Deutsch (born 1953)
Developed the many-worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics and has engaged with quantum gravity's implications, arguing that physics supports a strongly realist picture and that reconciling quantum mechanics with spacetime is among the deepest unsolved problems.
