# Exercises and Thought Experiments: Time Travel

## Comprehension Questions

1. Explain Lewis's distinction between personal time and external time. How does this distinction help dissolve certain apparent paradoxes of time travel?

2. What is the Grandfather Paradox? State Lewis's consistency response. Does this response show that backward time travel is logically possible?

3. What is the Bootstrap Paradox? How does it differ from the Grandfather Paradox, and what philosophical problem does it raise?

## Discussion Questions

4. Lewis argues that time travelers cannot change the past but can act in it. Does this mean time travel is not really worth having — you cannot prevent historical disasters, only be present at them?

5. Is the consistency constraint (only consistent histories are possible) a satisfying solution to the Grandfather Paradox, or does it seem to make time travel paradoxically constrained?

6. General relativity allows for closed timelike curves in some spacetimes (like Gödel's rotating universe). Does this show that backward time travel is physically possible? What additional conditions would need to be met?

## Thought Experiments

7. **The Self-Defeating Book**: A manuscript of a great novel travels back in time and is given to an author who copies it and publishes it as their own. It becomes famous, and a time traveler takes it back in time. Where does the content of the novel come from? Is this coherent?

8. **The Predestination Paradox**: Tim travels back in time intending to stop a crime. He discovers that his presence is what caused the crime. Can he now refrain from acting in a way that causes the crime? What does this reveal about free will in a time travel scenario?

9. **Multiple Time Travelers**: Ten time travelers from different future decades all travel to the same day in the past. Can they all exist on that day simultaneously? What consistency constraints apply?
