# Further Reading: Time Travel and Paradoxes

- Lewis, David. "The Paradoxes of Time Travel." *American Philosophical Quarterly* 13 (1976): 145–152.
- Gödel, Kurt. "An Example of a New Type of Cosmological Solutions of Einstein's Field Equations." *Reviews of Modern Physics* 21 (1949): 447–450.
- Dummett, Michael. "Bringing About the Past." *Philosophical Review* 73 (1964): 338–359.

## Modern Treatments

- Horwich, Paul. *Asymmetries in Time*. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1987.

- Arntzenius, Frank, and Tim Maudlin. "Time Travel and Modern Physics." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, 2013.
