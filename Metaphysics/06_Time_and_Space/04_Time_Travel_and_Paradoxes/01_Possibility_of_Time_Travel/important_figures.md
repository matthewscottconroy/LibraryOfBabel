# Important Figures: Time Travel

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** provided the definitive philosophical treatment of time travel in "The Paradoxes of Time Travel" (1976), introducing the distinction between personal and external time and the consistency constraint.

**Kurt Gödel (1906–1978)** discovered a solution to Einstein's field equations representing a rotating universe with closed timelike curves, the first physically motivated demonstration of the possibility of time travel in general relativity. "An Example of a New Type of Cosmological Solutions" (1949).

**Michael Dummett (1925–2011)** discussed backward causation and temporal paradoxes in "Bringing About the Past" (1964).

**Paul Horwich (b. 1947)** analyzed the logical structure of time travel and causal loops in *Asymmetries in Time* (1987).

**Nicholas J.J. Smith** has written extensively on time travel, including the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry and *Time Travel: The Science and the Philosophy* (2016).

**Frank Arntzenius and Tim Maudlin** have written on the physics of closed timelike curves in "Time Travel and Modern Physics" (2013), *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
