# Further Reading: Temporal Paradoxes and Direction of Time

## Primary Sources

- Reichenbach, Hans. *The Direction of Time*. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1956.
- Price, Huw. *Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1996.
- Albert, David Z. *Time and Chance*. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 2000.
- Lewis, David. "Counterfactual Dependence and Time's Arrow." *Noûs* 13 (1979): 455–476.

## Anthologies

- Savitt, Steven (ed.). *Time's Arrows Today*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1995.
- Callender, Craig, and Ralph Edney. *Introducing Time*. London: Icon Books, 2001. [Accessible overview]

## Advanced Topics

- Penrose, Roger. *The Road to Reality*. London: Jonathan Cape, 2004. [Chapters on entropy and the arrow of time]
- Sklar, Lawrence. *Physics and Chance: Philosophical Issues in the Foundations of Statistical Mechanics*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1993.
