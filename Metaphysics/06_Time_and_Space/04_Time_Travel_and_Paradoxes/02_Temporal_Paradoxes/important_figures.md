# Important Figures: Temporal Paradoxes and Direction of Time

**Ludwig Boltzmann (1844–1906)** developed statistical mechanics and the thermodynamic interpretation of entropy, providing the physics of the thermodynamic arrow of time.

**Hans Reichenbach (1891–1953)** analyzed the direction of time and the common cause principle in *The Direction of Time* (1956).

**Huw Price (b. 1953)** has argued that the asymmetry of causation and the thermodynamic arrow are perspectival rather than objective in *Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point* (1996).

**David Albert (b. 1954)** has defended a neo-Newtonian approach to the arrow of time and developed the "Past Hypothesis" in *Time and Chance* (2000).

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** analyzed the asymmetry of counterfactual dependence and its grounding in the thermodynamic arrow in "Counterfactual Dependence and Time's Arrow" (1979).

**Roger Penrose (b. 1931)** has argued that the arrow of time is connected to a quantum gravitational phenomenon (the "Weyl curvature hypothesis") rather than purely thermodynamic. *The Emperor's New Mind* (1989) and *The Road to Reality* (2004).
