# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Plato, *Phaedo* (arguments for immortality, soul-body dualism)
- Plato, *Republic*, Book IV (tripartite soul), Book X (myth of Er)
- Plato, *Phaedrus* (charioteer allegory)
- Aristotle, *De Anima* (On the Soul), esp. Books I–III
- Lucretius, *De Rerum Natura*, Book III (soul as atoms)
- Plotinus, *Enneads*, IV (On the Soul)

## Secondary Sources
- A.E. Taylor, *Plato: The Man and His Work* (1926)
- Richard Sorabji, *Aristotle on Memory* (1972)
- Jonathan Barnes (ed.), *The Cambridge Companion to Aristotle* (1995)
- Christopher Shields, *Aristotle* (2007)
- Lloyd Gerson, *Aristotle and Other Platonists* (2005)
- Michael Frede and Gisela Striker (eds.), *Rationality in Greek Thought* (1996)
