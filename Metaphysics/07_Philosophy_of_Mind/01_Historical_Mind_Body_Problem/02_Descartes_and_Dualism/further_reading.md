# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- René Descartes, *Meditations on First Philosophy* (1641), esp. Meditations I, II, VI
- Descartes, *Passions of the Soul* (1649)
- Descartes-Elisabeth Correspondence (1643–1649), in *Philosophical Letters* (Kenny, ed.)
- Malebranche, *The Search after Truth* (1674–75), esp. Books I–III
- Leibniz, *Monadology* (1714); *New Essays on Human Understanding* (1704, published 1765)

## Secondary Sources
- Bernard Williams, *Descartes: The Project of Pure Enquiry* (1978)
- Margaret Wilson, *Descartes* (1978)
- Marleen Rozemond, *Descartes's Dualism* (1998)
- Lisa Shapiro (ed.), *The Correspondence between Princess Elisabeth of Bohemia and René Descartes* (2007)
- C.P. Ragland and Sarah Baber (eds.), *What Is Philosophy?* (2008) — includes material on early modern mind-body problem
- Robert Pasnau, *Metaphysics of the Soul* (2011)
