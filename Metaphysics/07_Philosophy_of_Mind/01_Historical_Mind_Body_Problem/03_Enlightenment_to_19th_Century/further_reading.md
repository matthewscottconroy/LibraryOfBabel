# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Kant, *Critique of Pure Reason* (1781/1787), Paralogisms of Pure Reason; Transcendental Deduction
- Hegel, *Phenomenology of Spirit* (1807), Introduction and sense-certainty chapters
- Franz Brentano, *Psychology from an Empirical Standpoint* (1874)
- William James, *Principles of Psychology* (1890), esp. chapters on stream of thought, attention, self
- Edmund Husserl, *Ideas I: General Introduction to Pure Phenomenology* (1913)
- Heidegger, *Being and Time* (1927), Division I
- Merleau-Ponty, *Phenomenology of Perception* (1945)

## Secondary Sources
- P.F. Strawson, *The Bounds of Sense* (1966) — on Kant's theory of mind
- Beatrice Longuenesse, *Kant and the Capacity to Judge* (1998)
- Hubert Dreyfus, *Being-in-the-World* (1991) — commentary on Heidegger's Division I
- Sean Kelly, "Merleau-Ponty on the Body" (2000), *Ratio*
- Dan Zahavi, *Husserl's Phenomenology* (2003)
- Taylor Carman, *Merleau-Ponty* (2008)
