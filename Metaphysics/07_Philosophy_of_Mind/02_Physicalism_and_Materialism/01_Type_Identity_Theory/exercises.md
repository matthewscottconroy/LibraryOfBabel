# Exercises and Thought Experiments

1. **Identity and composition**: Place distinguishes "is" of definition ("A bachelor is an unmarried man") from "is" of composition ("Water is H₂O"). Apply this distinction to "Pain is C-fiber firing." Is this an identity of definition, composition, or something else? What are the implications?

2. **Kripke's modal argument**: Kripke argues that if pain = C-fiber firing, then necessarily pain = C-fiber firing. But it seems conceivable that C-fibers could fire without pain (zombie C-fibers). Does this show that pain ≠ C-fiber firing? How might an identity theorist respond?

3. **Multiple realizability thought experiment**: Imagine a silicon-based alien that exhibits all the same behavioral dispositions as a human in pain but has no neurons. Is the alien in pain? If yes, does this refute type identity theory? If no, what is your criterion for pain attribution?

4. **Eliminativism and self-refutation**: If eliminative materialism is true, there are no beliefs. But doesn't the eliminativist believe eliminative materialism is true? Is this a genuine self-refutation, or can eliminativists find a way out?

5. **Folk psychology test**: List five mental state attributions you made in the last hour (e.g., "she wants coffee," "he doesn't know the answer"). For each, consider whether the attribution could be replaced without loss by a purely behavioral description. Does this test support or undermine functionalism?
