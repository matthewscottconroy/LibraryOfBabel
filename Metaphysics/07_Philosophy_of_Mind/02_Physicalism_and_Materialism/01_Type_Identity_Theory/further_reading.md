# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- U.T. Place, "Is Consciousness a Brain Process?" *British Journal of Psychology* 47 (1956)
- J.J.C. Smart, "Sensations and Brain Processes," *Philosophical Review* 68 (1959)
- Herbert Feigl, "The 'Mental' and the 'Physical'," *Minnesota Studies in the Philosophy of Science* 2 (1958)
- Hilary Putnam, "Psychological Predicates," in *Art, Mind, and Religion* (1967)
- Paul Churchland, *Matter and Consciousness* (1984), ch. 2–3
- Saul Kripke, *Naming and Necessity* (1980), Lecture III

## Secondary Sources
- Ned Block (ed.), *Readings in Philosophy of Psychology* (1980) — classic anthology
- Jaegwon Kim, *Mind in a Physical World* (1998)
- John Polger and Lawrence Shapiro, *The Multiple Realization Book* (2016)
- Andy Clark, *Microcognition* (1989)
- Cynthia Macdonald, *Mind-Body Identity Theories* (1989)
