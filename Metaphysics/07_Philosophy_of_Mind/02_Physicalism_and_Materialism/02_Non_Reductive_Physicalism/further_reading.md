# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Donald Davidson, "Mental Events," in *Essays on Actions and Events* (1980)
- Jaegwon Kim, *Supervenience and Mind* (1993)
- David Papineau, *Philosophical Naturalism* (1993)
- Frank Jackson, "Epiphenomenal Qualia," *Philosophical Quarterly* 32 (1982)
- Brian Loar, "Phenomenal States," in *Philosophical Perspectives* 4 (1990)
- David Chalmers, *The Conscious Mind* (1996), ch. 1–3

## Secondary Sources
- Jaegwon Kim, *Mind in a Physical World* (1998) — best systematic treatment
- Carl Gillett and Barry Loewer (eds.), *Physicalism and Its Discontents* (2001)
- David Papineau, *Thinking about Consciousness* (2002)
- Barry Loewer, "Mental Causation, or Something Near Enough," in *Contemporary Debates in Philosophy of Mind* (2007)
- Mark Rowlands, *The Nature of Consciousness* (2001)
