# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Descartes, *Meditations on First Philosophy* (1641), Meditations II, VI
- Descartes-Elisabeth Correspondence
- Leibniz, *Monadology* (1714)
- Kripke, *Naming and Necessity* (1980), Lecture III
- Swinburne, *The Evolution of the Soul* (1986)

## Secondary Sources
- Marleen Rozemond, *Descartes's Dualism* (1998)
- Howard Robinson (ed.), *Objections to Physicalism* (1993)
- John Foster, *The Immaterial Self* (1991)
- E.J. Lowe, *Personal Agency: The Metaphysics of Mind and Action* (2008)
- Brian Farrell, "Experience" *Mind* 59 (1950) — classic early statement of the problem
