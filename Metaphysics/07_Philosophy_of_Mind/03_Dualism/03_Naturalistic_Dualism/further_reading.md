# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- David Chalmers, *The Conscious Mind* (1996)
- Chalmers, "Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 2 (1995)
- Frank Jackson, "What Mary Didn't Know," *Journal of Philosophy* 83 (1986)
- Brian Loar, "Phenomenal States," *Philosophical Perspectives* 4 (1990)

## Secondary Sources
- Jonathan Shear (ed.), *Explaining Consciousness: The Hard Problem* (1997)
- Ned Block and Robert Stalnaker, "Conceptual Analysis, Dualism, and the Explanatory Gap," *Philosophical Review* 108 (1999)
- Derk Pereboom, "Conceptual Isolation and its Relevance to Knowledge Arguments," *Consciousness and Cognition* (2011)
- Philip Goff, *Consciousness and Fundamental Reality* (2017)
- Daniel Stoljar, *Ignorance and Imagination* (2006) — the "ignorance hypothesis" alternative to dualism
