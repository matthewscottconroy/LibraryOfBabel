# Important Terms and Ideas: Criticisms of Functionalism

**Chinese Room Argument**
Searle's thought experiment: a person in a room follows rules to manipulate Chinese symbols, producing correct outputs, without understanding Chinese at all. This shows that syntax (symbol manipulation) is insufficient for semantics (meaning and understanding). Applied to AI: computers manipulate symbols but do not thereby understand.

**Inverted Qualia**
The hypothesis that two people could be functionally identical (same input-output relations, same behavioral dispositions) yet have phenomenally inverted experiences—what is qualitatively red for one is qualitatively green for the other. If coherent, this shows that qualia are not determined by functional organization.

**Absent Qualia**
Block's hypothesis: a system could realize the correct functional organization for mentality while having no phenomenal experience at all—the lights are out inside. The Chinese nation thought experiment (a billion people organized to simulate a brain) is meant to make absent qualia intuitively plausible.

**Semantic Content**
Searle's point: genuine mental states have intentional content—they are about things in the world. Mere symbol manipulation does not confer semantic content; symbols derive their meaning from the mental states of their users. A purely functional account that omits biological causal history cannot ground genuine intentionality.

**Systems Reply**
The standard functionalist reply to the Chinese Room: while the person in the room does not understand Chinese, the whole system (person + rules + symbols) does. Searle responds by internalizing the system (the person memorizes all the rules) and insisting understanding is still absent.

**Original vs. Derived Intentionality**
Searle's distinction: human mental states have original (intrinsic) intentionality—they are genuinely about things. Artifacts like books and computers have only derived intentionality, borrowed from the minds that created and use them. Functionalism, Searle argues, cannot account for original intentionality.
