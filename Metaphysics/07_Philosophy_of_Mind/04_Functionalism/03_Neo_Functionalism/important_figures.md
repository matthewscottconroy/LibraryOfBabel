# Important Figures

**Ruth Millikan (1933–present)**: *Language, Thought, and Other Biological Categories* (1984) founded teleological semantics, grounding intentional content in biological proper function.

**Fred Dretske (1932–2013)**: Developed informational semantics and teleological accounts of mental content in *Knowledge and the Flow of Information* (1981) and *Explaining Behavior* (1988).

**David Chalmers (1966–present)**: Developed two-dimensional semantics as a framework for analyzing the relationship between phenomenal and physical concepts. *The Conscious Mind* (1996) and "The Components of Content" (2002).

**Frank Jackson (1943–present)**: *From Metaphysics to Ethics* (1998) develops descriptivism about concepts using two-dimensional semantics, connecting conceptual analysis to neo-functionalist accounts of mental content.

**Denny Dieks and Karen Neander**: Developed and refined the proper function account of mental content in various papers on teleosemantics.
