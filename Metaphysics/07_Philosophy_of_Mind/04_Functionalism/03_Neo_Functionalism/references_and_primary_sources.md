# References and Primary Sources: Neo-Functionalism

## Foundational Texts

- Shoemaker, Sydney, "Functionalism and Qualia," *Philosophical Studies* 27 (1975). Defends functionalism about qualia, arguing that qualia themselves have functional essences—a qualia's identity is partly constituted by its causal role.

- Dretske, Fred, *Naturalizing the Mind* (1995). Develops a representationalist functionalism: phenomenal properties are representational properties; qualia are determined by what a state naturally represents in the organism's evolutionary and learning history.

- Tye, Michael, *Ten Problems of Consciousness* (1995). Presents PANIC theory: qualia are abstract, nonconceptual, poised representational content—a naturalistic functionalist account that attempts to solve the qualia problem.

## Contemporary Works

- Lycan, William, *Consciousness and Experience* (1996). Homuncular functionalism: mental states are defined by their functional roles within a hierarchical system of sub-personal functional components, providing a more refined functionalist account of consciousness.

- Carruthers, Peter, *The Architecture of the Mind* (2006). Defends a massively modular functionalist view of the mind with a higher-order thought theory of consciousness.
