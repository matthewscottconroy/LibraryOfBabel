# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Frank Jackson, "Epiphenomenal Qualia," *Philosophical Quarterly* 32 (1982)
- Jackson, "What Mary Didn't Know," *Journal of Philosophy* 83 (1986)
- Daniel Dennett, "Quining Qualia," in *Consciousness in Contemporary Science* (1988)
- Ned Block, "On a Confusion about a Function of Consciousness," *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 18 (1995)

## Secondary Sources
- Peter Ludlow, Yujin Nagasawa, and Daniel Stoljar (eds.), *There's Something about Mary* (2004)
- Martine Nida-Rümelin, "Qualia: The Knowledge Argument," *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*
- David Chalmers (ed.), *Philosophy of Mind: Classical and Contemporary Readings* (2002), Parts IV–V
- Sydney Shoemaker, *The First-Person Perspective and Other Essays* (1996)
