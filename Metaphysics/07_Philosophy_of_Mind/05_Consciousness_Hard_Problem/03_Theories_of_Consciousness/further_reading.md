- Bernard Baars, *A Cognitive Theory of Consciousness* (1988)
- Giulio Tononi, "Consciousness as Integrated Information," *Biological Bulletin* 215 (2008)
- Stanislas Dehaene, *Consciousness and the Brain* (2014)

- Uriah Kriegel, *The Sources of Intentionality* (2011)
