# Important Figures

**David Rosenthal (1939–present)**: *Consciousness and Mind* (2005) is the major defense of higher-order thought theory. Rosenthal argues that consciousness requires higher-order non-inferential thoughts about first-order states.

**Uriah Kriegel (1974–present)**: Defends self-representationalism — conscious states represent themselves — as an alternative to higher-order theories. *Subjective Consciousness* (2009).

**Bernard Baars (1946–present)**: Developed Global Workspace Theory in *A Cognitive Theory of Consciousness* (1988). The GWT is the most empirically supported theory of access consciousness.

**Stanislas Dehaene (1965–present)**: Gave Global Workspace Theory its neural implementation in *Consciousness and the Brain* (2014). His research on the "ignition" of global workspaces is central to current consciousness neuroscience.

**Giulio Tononi (1960–present)**: Developed Integrated Information Theory (IIT). *Phi: A Voyage from the Brain to the Soul* (2012) is the popular exposition; the technical papers develop the formal theory.

**Christof Koch (1956–present)**: Long-time collaborator with Francis Crick on neural correlates of consciousness. Recently converted to IIT and defends it in *The Feeling of Life Itself* (2019).
