# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Alfred North Whitehead, *Process and Reality* (1929/1978)
- William James, "Does Consciousness Exist?" *Journal of Philosophy* 1 (1904)
- Bertrand Russell, *The Analysis of Matter* (1927), ch. 37
- Galen Strawson, "Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 13 (2006)

## Secondary Sources
- Philip Goff, *Consciousness and Fundamental Reality* (2017) — best systematic treatment
- David Chalmers, "Panpsychism and Panprotopsychism," in *Consciousness in the Physical World* (2015)
- Thomas Nagel, *Mind and Cosmos* (2012)
- William Seager, *Theories of Consciousness* (1999)
- Sam Coleman (ed.), *The Knowledge Argument* (2019) — includes panpsychist responses
