# Important Figures

**Philip Goff (1980–present)**: The leading popularizer and defender of contemporary panpsychism. *Consciousness and Fundamental Reality* (2017) and *Galileo's Error* (2019).

**William Seager (1954–present)**: Philosopher and historian of consciousness theories. Defends Russellian panpsychism and has contributed to combining problem analysis.

**David Chalmers (1966–present)**: Has moved from naturalistic dualism toward panpsychism and idealism as alternatives to physicalism. "The Combination Problem for Panpsychism" (2016) surveys the central challenge.

**Galen Strawson (1952–present)**: "Realistic Monism" argues that any credible physicalism must be panpsychist, since micro-physical entities must have the intrinsic properties that ground macro-level consciousness.

**Sam Coleman**: Developed "micro-phenomenalism" — a form of panpsychism on which micro-properties are neither phenomenal nor merely structural but proto-phenomenal.
