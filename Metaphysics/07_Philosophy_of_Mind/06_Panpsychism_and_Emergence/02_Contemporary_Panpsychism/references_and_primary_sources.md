# References and Primary Sources: Contemporary Panpsychism

## Foundational Texts

- Goff, Philip, *Consciousness and Fundamental Reality* (2017). A rigorous defense of panpsychism as the best response to the hard problem; argues that standard physicalism cannot account for qualia, and panpsychism preserves the reality of consciousness without dualism.

- Strawson, Galen, "Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 13 (2006). Argues that taking the reality of phenomenal consciousness seriously and maintaining physicalism requires panpsychism; experience must be part of the fundamental physical nature of reality.

- Chalmers, David, "Panpsychism and Panprotopsychism," in *Consciousness in the Physical World* edited by Alter and Nagasawa (2015). Distinguishes panpsychism (micro-experiences at the fundamental level) from panprotopsychism (proto-experiential properties) and evaluates them as responses to the hard problem.

## Contemporary Works

- Goff, Philip, Seager, William, and Allen-Hermanson, Sean, "Panpsychism," *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2022). A comprehensive survey of contemporary panpsychist positions and the arguments for and against them.

- Bruntrup, Godehard and Jaskolla, Ludwig (eds.), *Panpsychism: Contemporary Perspectives* (2017). An anthology of current work on panpsychism from philosophers and scientists.
