# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Keith Frankish, "Illusionism as a Theory of Consciousness," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 23 (2016)
- Daniel Dennett, *Consciousness Explained* (1991), ch. 10–12
- David Chalmers, "The Meta-Problem of Consciousness," *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 25 (2018)

## Secondary Sources
- Keith Frankish (ed.), *Illusionism as a Theory of Consciousness* (2017) — responses and replies
- Eric Schwitzgebel, *Perplexities of Consciousness* (2011)
- Thomas Metzinger, *Being No One* (2003) — a sophisticated deflationary account
- Peter Frankish and William Ramsey (eds.), *The Cambridge Handbook of Cognitive Science* (2012)
