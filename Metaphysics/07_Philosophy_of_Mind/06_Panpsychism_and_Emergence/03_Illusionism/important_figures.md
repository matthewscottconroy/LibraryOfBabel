# Important Figures

**Daniel Dennett (1942–present)**: The original illusionist (though he resists the label): *Consciousness Explained* (1991) argues that qualia as traditionally conceived do not exist. Dennett's "heterophenomenology" replaces first-person reports with a third-person methodology.

**Keith Frankish (1960–present)**: Coined and defends "illusionism" as a theory of consciousness. "Illusionism as a Theory of Consciousness" (2016) in *Journal of Consciousness Studies* is the canonical paper.

**Peter Frankish**: Developed the illusion/misrepresentation distinction for understanding introspection.

**David Chalmers (1966–present)**: Introduced the meta-problem of consciousness as a bridge between deflationary and inflationary approaches. "The Meta-Problem of Consciousness" (2018).

**Eric Schwitzgebel (1968–present)**: "The Unreliability of Naive Introspection" and related work challenges the reliability of phenomenal self-reports, providing empirical support for illusionist positions.
