# Important Figures

**Jerry Fodor (1935–2017)**: Developed the "asymmetric dependence" theory of mental content — a causal theory designed to handle misrepresentation. *Psychosemantics* (1987), *The Elm and the Expert* (1994).

**Ruth Millikan (1933–present)**: Developed teleological semantics — content is fixed by proper function (evolutionary history). *Language, Thought, and Other Biological Categories* (1984), *White Queen Psychology* (1993).

**Fred Dretske (1932–2013)**: Developed informational and teleological accounts of content. *Knowledge and the Flow of Information* (1981), *Explaining Behavior* (1988).

**Robert Brandom (1950–present)**: Developed inferentialist semantics — content is constituted by inferential role. *Making It Explicit* (1994), *Articulating Reasons* (2000).

**Christopher Peacocke (1950–present)**: Developed an account of concept possession conditions that combines inferential and perceptual conditions. *A Study of Concepts* (1992).
