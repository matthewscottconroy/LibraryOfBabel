# Important Figures

**Jaegwon Kim (1934–2019)**: Developed the exclusion argument systematically in "The Myth of Non-Reductive Physicalism" (1989) and *Mind in a Physical World* (1998). Kim ultimately concluded that non-reductive physicalism faces a fatal dilemma.

**Donald Davidson (1917–2003)**: Anomalous monism faces a version of the exclusion problem — do mental properties do any causal work if only physical descriptions figure in strict laws? Davidson's responses to LePore and Loewer are key.

**Ernest LePore and Barry Loewer**: Pressed the "epiphenomenalism worry" for Davidson's anomalous monism in "Mind Matters" (1987).

**Helen Steward (1956–present)**: *The Ontology of Mind* (1997) defends a non-standard view of causation that accommodates mental causation without invoking reduction.

**David Papineau (1947–present)**: Argues that the exclusion argument supports physicalism — mental properties must be physical properties if they are causally efficacious.
