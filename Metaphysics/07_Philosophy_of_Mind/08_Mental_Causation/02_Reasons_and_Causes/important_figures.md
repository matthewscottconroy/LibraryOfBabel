# Important Figures

**Donald Davidson (1917–2003)**: "Actions, Reasons, and Causes" (1963) established that reasons are causes. His work on action explanation is central to both philosophy of action and philosophy of mind.

**Gilbert Ryle (1900–1976)**: *The Concept of Mind* (1949) defended the view that reasons are not causes but a different kind of explanation — placing action in a normative framework.

**Michael Bratman (1945–present)**: Extended the belief-desire model to a planning theory of agency. *Intention, Plans, and Practical Reason* (1987).

**Harry Frankfurt (1929–present)**: Developed a hierarchical theory of free action, distinguishing first-order desires from second-order volitions. "Freedom of the Will and the Concept of a Person" (1971).

**Alvin Goldman (1938–present)**: *A Theory of Human Action* (1970) developed a systematic theory of action causation and individuation.
