- Kim, "Making Sense of Emergence," *Philosophical Studies* 95 (1999)
- Timothy O'Connor and Hong Yu Wong, "Emergent Properties," *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*

- Mark Bedau and Paul Humphreys (eds.), *Emergence: Contemporary Readings in Philosophy and Science* (2008)
- Philip Clayton, *Mind and Emergence* (2004)
- David Chalmers, "Strong and Weak Emergence," in *The Re-Emergence of Emergence* (2006)
