# Important Figures

**Gareth Evans (1946–1980)**: Distinguished the non-conceptual content of perceptual experience from the conceptual content of belief. *The Varieties of Reference* (1982).

**John McDowell (1942–present)**: Argues in *Mind and World* (1994) that experience must be conceptual if it is to stand in rational relations to judgment. Influential defense of conceptual content.

**Fred Dretske (1932–2013)**: Distinguished non-epistemic and epistemic perception, and developed the natural information content of perceptual states. *Seeing and Knowing* (1969).

**Christopher Peacocke (1950–present)**: *A Study of Concepts* (1992) and *The Realm of Reason* (2004) develop the conceptual role in perception and the analysis of what it is to possess a concept.

**Ned Block (1942–present)**: Phenomenological arguments for nonconceptual content, particularly through cases of fine-grained perceptual discrimination.
