# Important Figures

**Francisco Varela (1946–2001)**, **Evan Thompson (1962–present)**, and **Eleanor Rosch (1938–present)**: *The Embodied Mind* (1991) is the founding text of enactivism in cognitive science, combining phenomenology with biology and Buddhist psychology.

**Alva Noe (1964–present)**: *Action in Perception* (2004) develops enactive perception — perception as sensorimotor skill. *Out of Our Heads* (2009) extends the enactivist challenge to neuroscience-based accounts.

**Shaun Gallagher (1948–present)**: *How the Body Shapes the Mind* (2005) develops the phenomenological and empirical dimensions of embodied cognition.

**Mark Johnson (1949–present)** and **George Lakoff (1941–present)**: *The Body in the Mind* (1987) and *Philosophy in the Flesh* (1999) develop the embodiment of abstract thought through conceptual metaphor.

**Andy Clark (1957–present)**: *Being There: Putting Brain, Body, and World Together Again* (1997) is a key embodied cognition text.
