# Important Figures

**Alan Turing (1912–1954)**: Developed the theory of computation and the Turing Machine. 'Computing Machinery and Intelligence' (1950) proposed the Turing Test.

**Jerry Fodor (1935–2017)**: Developed the language of thought hypothesis and the computational-representational theory of mind (CRTM). *The Language of Thought* (1975), *The Modularity of Mind* (1983).

**Zenon Pylyshyn (1937–2022)**: Developed the computational account of cognitive architecture, distinguishing algorithmic from implementational levels. *Computation and Cognition* (1984).

**Andy Clark (1957–present)**: *Being There* (1997) and *Supersizing the Mind* (2008) develop extended and embodied alternatives to classical computationalism.

**David Rumelhart and James McClelland**: Edited *Parallel Distributed Processing* (1986), the founding text of connectionist cognitive science, which challenged the classical symbolic approach.
