# Further Reading and Primary Sources

## Primary Sources
- Searle, 'Minds, Brains, and Programs,' *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 3 (1980)
- Dreyfus, *What Computers Can't Do* (1972/1992)
- Turing, 'Computing Machinery and Intelligence,' *Mind* 59 (1950)
- Dennett, *Brainstorms* (1978), 'Why You Can't Make a Computer That Feels Pain'

## Secondary Sources
- John Preston and Mark Bishop (eds.), *Views into the Chinese Room* (2002)
- Ned Block, 'The Mind as the Software of the Brain,' in *An Invitation to Cognitive Science* (1995)
- Margaret Boden, *The Philosophy of Artificial Intelligence* (1990)
- Luciano Floridi (ed.), *The Cambridge Handbook of Artificial Intelligence* (2023)
