# Important Figures

**David Hume (1711–1776)**: The bundle theory of the self — the self as a collection of perceptions rather than a persisting substance. *Treatise of Human Nature*, Book I, Part IV, section 6.

**Ludwig Wittgenstein (1889–1951)**: *Philosophical Investigations* dissolved the problem of other minds by showing that mental concepts are not private labels for inner items but are learned in public, behavioral contexts.

**Derek Parfit (1942–2017)**: *Reasons and Persons* (1984) argued that personal identity is not what matters in survival; what matters is psychological connectedness and continuity.

**Thomas Metzinger (1958–present)**: *Being No One* (2003) and *The Ego Tunnel* (2009) develop a deflationary, neuroscientific account of the self as a transparent self-model.

**Shaun Gallagher (1948–present)**: Developed a phenomenologically-informed account of the minimal self, distinguishing minimal bodily self-awareness from narrative self-identity.
