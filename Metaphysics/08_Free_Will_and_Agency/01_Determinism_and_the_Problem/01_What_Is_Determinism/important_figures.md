# Important Figures: What Is Determinism?

## Pierre-Simon Laplace (1749–1827)
Formulated the classic statement of determinism: given complete knowledge of the positions and velocities of all particles at one time, together with all the forces acting on them, an intellect (Laplace's demon) could in principle predict every future event and know every past event. This "Laplace's demon" is the canonical formulation of physical determinism.

## Baron d'Holbach (1723–1789)
The most thoroughgoing Enlightenment determinist and materialist; argued in *The System of Nature* (1770) that everything in the universe, including human thought and action, is determined by prior causes and the laws of nature. Denied free will and moral responsibility on determinist grounds.

## William James (1842–1910)
A vigorous opponent of determinism and defender of indeterminism; his essay "The Dilemma of Determinism" (1884) argues that determinism is inconsistent with our judgment that some things in the world are genuinely bad and could have been otherwise.

## Carl Hempel (1905–1997)
Developed the covering-law model of scientific explanation, which gives deterministic explanation a precise formal structure: an event is explained by showing that it follows from a law and prior conditions. This model shaped philosophical debates about the relationship between determinism and explanation.
