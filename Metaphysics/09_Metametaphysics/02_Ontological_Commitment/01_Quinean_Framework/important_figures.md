# Important Figures: The Quinean Framework of Ontological Commitment

## W.V.O. Quine (1908–2000)
The architect of the standard framework: ontological commitment is determined by the range of quantification in our best theories. "To be is to be the value of a variable." His criterion provides a clear methodological tool and shapes virtually all subsequent work in analytic ontology.

## Rudolf Carnap (1891–1970)
Quine's main interlocutor: Carnap's distinction between internal and external questions of existence prefigured the metaontological debate. Quine rejected this distinction, arguing there is no purely linguistic or framework-relative sense of ontological commitment.

## Peter van Inwagen (born 1942)
Applied Quinean methodology rigorously, defending a sparse ontology of only physical simples and composite living organisms. His work on material beings illustrates how the Quinean framework generates sharp ontological verdicts.

## David Lewis (1941–2001)
Applied Quinean methodology in the opposite direction, accepting an extremely abundant ontology of sets, properties, possible worlds, and their inhabitants. Lewis illustrates how the same methodology can support very different ontological conclusions depending on theoretical commitments.
