# References and Primary Sources: The Quinean Framework

## Foundational Texts

- Quine, W.V.O., "On What There Is," *Review of Metaphysics* 2 (1948). The foundational paper: the ontological question is "What is there?"; we answer it by examining what the variables of our best theories range over.

- Quine, W.V.O., "Ontological Relativity," *Journal of Philosophy* 65 (1968). Extends and refines the criterion: ontological commitment is relative to a background theory; there is no scheme-independent standpoint for ontology.

- Carnap, Rudolf, "Empiricism, Semantics, and Ontology," *Revue Internationale de Philosophie* 4 (1950). Distinguishes internal questions (within a framework) from external questions (about adopting a framework); argues that only internal questions are meaningful and that external questions are merely pragmatic.

## Contemporary Works

- van Inwagen, Peter, "Meta-Ontology," *Erkenntnis* 48 (1998). A defense of Quinean meta-ontology against deflationary alternatives, arguing that ontological questions are substantive and that the Quinean method provides the right approach.

- Sider, Theodore, *Writing the Book of the World* (2011). Extends Quinean ontology with the concept of "structure"—the world's own joints—arguing that the best metaphysical theories carve reality at its joints.
