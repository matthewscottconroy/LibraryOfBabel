# Important Figures: The Grounding Relation

## Kit Fine (born 1946)
The central figure in the revival of grounding in analytic metaphysics; his papers "The Question of Realism" (2001) and "Guide to Ground" (2012) provide the canonical account of grounding as a primitive, non-modal, explanatory relation of metaphysical dependence.

## Jonathan Schaffer (born 1974)
Developed priority monism—the view that the whole is ontologically prior to its parts—using grounding as the key relation. His work has been central to the grounding literature and he has investigated the logic and metaphysics of grounding extensively.

## Gideon Rosen (born 1962)
Contributed to the analysis of grounding and its relationship to essence; his work on "Metaphysical Dependence" (2010) provides an important account of what grounding is and how it interacts with modal and essentialist notions.

## Fabrice Correia (born 1973)
Has worked on the formal aspects of grounding, including its logic, and on the relationship between grounding and other notions of metaphysical dependence such as supervenience and necessitation.
