# References and Primary Sources: The Grounding Relation

## Foundational Texts

- Fine, Kit, "The Question of Realism," *Philosophers' Imprint* 1 (2001). Introduces a realist conception of metaphysics grounded in a non-modal notion of factual priority; the grounding relation is the core of this conception.

- Fine, Kit, "Guide to Ground," in *Metaphysical Grounding* edited by Correia and Schnieder (2012). The most detailed account of grounding as a primitive metaphysical relation; distinguishes strict from weak grounding, full from partial grounding, and examines the logic.

- Schaffer, Jonathan, "On What Grounds What," in *Metametaphysics* edited by Chalmers, Manley, and Wasserman (2009). Defends grounding-based ontology (neo-Aristotelianism) against Quinean ontology; argues the fundamental question is what grounds what, not what there is.

## Contemporary Works

- Rosen, Gideon, "Metaphysical Dependence: Grounding and Reduction," in *Modality: Metaphysics, Logic, and Epistemology* edited by Hale and Hoffmann (2010). A careful analysis of the concept of grounding and its relationship to reduction and necessitation.

- Correia, Fabrice and Schnieder, Benjamin (eds.), *Metaphysical Grounding: Understanding the Structure of Reality* (2012). The defining anthology on grounding, with contributions from Fine, Rosen, Schaffer, and others.
