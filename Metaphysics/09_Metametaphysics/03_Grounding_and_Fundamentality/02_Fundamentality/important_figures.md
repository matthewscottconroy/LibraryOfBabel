# Important Figures: Fundamentality

## Jonathan Schaffer (born 1974)
Developed priority monism—the cosmos as a whole is fundamental, with all particular things grounded in the whole—using grounding and fundamentality as central concepts. His work is the most sustained recent treatment of what fundamentality is and what is fundamental.

## Theodore Sider (born 1967)
Argues in *Writing the Book of the World* that "structure" or "naturalness" is a primitive metaphysical notion marking what is fundamental; the fundamental facts are those that carve reality at its joints—using its most natural, fundamental vocabulary.

## David Lewis (1941–2001)
Introduced the concept of "natural properties" as a way of distinguishing fundamental from gerrymandered properties; fundamental properties are perfectly natural—they "carve nature at its joints" and ground objective similarities between things.

## Frank Jackson (born 1943)
Developed "serious metaphysics"—the project of identifying the fundamental facts from which all other facts follow by necessitation—and argued that this project is continuous with conceptual analysis.
