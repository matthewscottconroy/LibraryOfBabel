# Important Figures: Applications of Grounding

## Kit Fine (born 1946)
Applied grounding to topics across metaphysics: the relationship between essence and modality, the metaphysics of ordinary objects, the structure of truthmaking, and the nature of vagueness. His application of grounding to multiple domains demonstrates its wide utility.

## Gideon Rosen (born 1962)
Applied grounding to normative and evaluative domains, arguing that normative facts are grounded in natural facts and developing a grounding-based account of moral responsibility.

## Shamik Dasgupta (born 1980)
Applied grounding to the debate about absolute vs. relational structure in physics, and has examined the epistemology and ontology of grounding itself, contributing to questions about when and why we should appeal to ground.

## Jessica Wilson (born 1970)
Applied grounding (critically) to debates about physicalism, arguing that specific metaphysical relations (realization, composition, identity) do the work typically assigned to a primitive grounding relation, and that appeals to grounding are explanatorily idle.
