# References and Primary Sources: Applications of Grounding

## Foundational Texts

- Fine, Kit, "Ontological Dependence," *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society* 95 (1995). Applies grounding to the metaphysics of ontological dependence; distinguishes existential, identity, and factual dependence.

- Rosen, Gideon, "Metaphysical Dependence: Grounding and Reduction," in *Modality: Metaphysics, Logic, and Epistemology* edited by Hale and Hoffmann (2010). Develops grounding as the basis for understanding normative supervenience and reduction.

- Dasgupta, Shamik, "Absolutism vs Comparativism about Quantity," *Oxford Studies in Metaphysics* 8 (2013). Applies grounding to the debate about whether absolute or comparative quantities are fundamental.

## Contemporary Works

- Audi, Paul, "Grounding: Toward a Theory of the In-Virtue-Of Relation," *Journal of Philosophy* 109 (2012). A systematic account of grounding with applications to multiple domains including properties, events, and normative facts.

- Trogdon, Kelly, "An Introduction to Grounding," in *Varieties of Dependence* edited by Hoeltje, Schnieder, and Steinberg (2013). A comprehensive survey of the applications of grounding across metaphysics.
