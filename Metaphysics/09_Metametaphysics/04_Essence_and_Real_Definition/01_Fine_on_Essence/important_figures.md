# Important Figures: Fine on Essence

## Kit Fine (born 1946)
The central figure: his papers "Essence and Modality" (1994) and "Senses of Essence" (1995) fundamentally reoriented discussions of essence in analytic metaphysics. Fine argued that essence is prior to and more fundamental than modality, reversing decades of received wisdom.

## Saul Kripke (1940–2022)
Fine's primary target: Kripke's influential modal account of essence (essential properties are those an object has in every world where it exists) is what Fine criticizes. Fine argues this account is both extensionally inadequate and gets the order of explanation backwards.

## Aristotle (384–322 BCE)
The ultimate source of Fine's project: Fine is explicitly reviving an Aristotelian conception of essence as explanatorily fundamental, prior to necessity. The "real definition" as giving the essence of a thing traces directly to Aristotle's account of the to ti ēn einai.

## Bob Hale (1945–2017)
Applied Fine's conception of essence to questions in philosophy of mathematics and logic; his work extends the essentialist framework to the foundations of mathematics, arguing that essences of mathematical objects ground mathematical necessity.
