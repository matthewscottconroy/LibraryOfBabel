# References and Primary Sources: Fine on Essence

## Foundational Texts

- Fine, Kit, "Essence and Modality," *Philosophical Perspectives* 8 (1994). The landmark paper: argues that the modal analysis of essential properties (properties had in every world where the object exists) is inadequate, and that essence must be understood as a primitive, non-modal notion.

- Fine, Kit, "Senses of Essence," in *Modality, Morality and Belief* edited by Sinnott-Armstrong et al. (1995). Distinguishes constitutive, consequential, and immediate essence; provides the most detailed account of the fine structure of essentialist claims.

- Fine, Kit, "The Logic of Essence," *Journal of Philosophical Logic* 24 (1995). Develops a formal logic for essence, using an essentialist operator "it is part of the essence of x that P" and examining its logical properties.

## Contemporary Works

- Correia, Fabrice, "Ontological Dependence," *Philosophy Compass* 3 (2008). A survey of Fine's concept of essence and its applications to ontological dependence and grounding.

- Koslicki, Kathrin, *Form, Matter, Substance* (2018). Develops a neo-Aristotelian account of substance drawing on Fine's essentialist framework and the Aristotelian tradition.
