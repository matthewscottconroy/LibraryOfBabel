# Essence in Metaphysics

With Fine's rehabilitation of essence as a primitive metaphysical concept — not reducible to modality — the concept has become central to contemporary analytic metaphysics in ways that would have seemed surprising to philosophers of an earlier generation who dismissed essentialism as scholastic. Essence now does explanatory work in the theory of properties, the metaphysics of material objects, the analysis of laws of nature, and the foundations of modality.

In the theory of properties, essence provides a basis for distinguishing categorical from dispositional properties. If it is essential to a property that it play a certain causal role — if being negatively charged essentially includes the disposition to repel other negative charges — then the property's nature determines its causal contribution, and laws of nature reflect the essences of properties rather than being contingent external additions. This "dispositional essentialism" (Ellis, Bird) grounds the necessity of laws in the essences of the properties they invoke.

In the metaphysics of material objects, essence determines persistence conditions. What is essential to a biological organism — what it is to be that organism — includes being continuous with a certain ancestral lineage, having a certain developmental trajectory, and being constituted by cells of the appropriate kind. When a sculptor creates a statue from a piece of clay, the statue and the clay have different essences: the statue's essence includes representing a certain subject in a certain form; the clay's essence includes being composed of clay of a certain kind. Different essences ground different persistence conditions, which is why the two can coincide without being identical.

In the foundations of modality, the essentialist approach (Fine, Lowe) holds that modal facts are grounded in essences: what is necessary is necessary because of the essences of the things involved. This makes the modal facts derivative from the essentially real, rather than treating possible worlds as primitive.

This section examines the role of essence in contemporary metaphysical theorizing across these and other domains.
