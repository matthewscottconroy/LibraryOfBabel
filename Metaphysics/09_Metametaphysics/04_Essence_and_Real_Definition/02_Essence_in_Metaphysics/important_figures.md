# Important Figures: Essence in Metaphysics

## Aristotle (384–322 BCE)
The originator of the framework: essence (to ti ēn einai—what-it-is-to-be) is the core of Aristotle's metaphysics, grounding natural kinds, teleology, and the structure of scientific explanation. The Aristotelian tradition makes essence central to understanding any domain of reality.

## Saul Kripke (1940–2022)
Revived essentialism in analytic philosophy through *Naming and Necessity* (1980): origin essentialism, natural kind essentialism, and the necessary a posteriori rehabilitated essence after decades of Quinean skepticism. Kripke's work is the starting point for most contemporary essentialist metaphysics.

## Kit Fine (born 1946)
Applied essence as a metaphysical tool across multiple domains: to ground grounding, to explain modality, to understand ontological dependence, and to characterize what real definitions look like. Fine's program makes essence the master concept of contemporary metaphysics.

## E.J. Lowe (1950–2014)
Developed a neo-Aristotelian four-category ontology (individual substances, universal kinds, attributes, modes) in which essence plays a foundational role; argued that essences of kinds ground natural laws and modal truths.
