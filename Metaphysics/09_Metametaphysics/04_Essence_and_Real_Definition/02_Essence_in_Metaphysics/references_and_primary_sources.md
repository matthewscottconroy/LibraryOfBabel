# References and Primary Sources: Essence in Metaphysics

## Foundational Texts

- Kripke, Saul, *Naming and Necessity* (1980). The rehabilitation of essentialism: origin essentialism, natural kind essentialism, and the necessary a posteriori establish essence as a genuine metaphysical notion.

- Fine, Kit, "Essence and Modality," *Philosophical Perspectives* 8 (1994). Essence is prior to modality; metaphysics should be reorganized around essentialist rather than modal concepts.

- Lowe, E.J., *The Four-Category Ontology* (2006). A systematic neo-Aristotelian metaphysics in which essence plays a foundational role in grounding natural laws, causal powers, and ontological structure.

## Contemporary Works

- Ellis, Brian, *Scientific Essentialism* (2001). Argues that natural kinds have real essences grounded in their causal powers, providing a scientific essentialism that grounds the necessity of natural laws.

- Koslicki, Kathrin, *Form, Matter, Substance* (2018). Develops a neo-Aristotelian account of individual substances using essentialist concepts from both Aristotle and Fine.
