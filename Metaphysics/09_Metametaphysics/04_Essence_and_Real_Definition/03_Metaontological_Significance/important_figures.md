# Important Figures: Metaontological Significance of Essence

## Kit Fine (born 1946)
The most important figure: Fine has argued that essence is not only a metaphysical notion but a methodological one—metaphysics is in the business of providing real definitions, and the correct method for metaphysics is essentialist analysis.

## Amie Thomasson (born 1968)
Argues that essence can be understood in terms of the application conditions of concepts; what is essential to a kind is what the concept requires for application. This makes essence tractable through conceptual analysis.

## David Chalmers (born 1966)
Applied essentialist considerations to the methodology of philosophy of mind, arguing that philosophical analysis aims at identifying the constitutive essence of consciousness, knowledge, and other mental notions.

## Stephen Yablo (born 1957)
Examined the relationship between essence and definition, including whether real definitions are possible for moral, mathematical, and metaphysical terms, and how essentialist claims relate to philosophical analysis.
