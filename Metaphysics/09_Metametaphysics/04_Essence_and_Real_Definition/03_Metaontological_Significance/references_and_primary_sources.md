# References and Primary Sources: Metaontological Significance of Essence

## Foundational Texts

- Fine, Kit, "The Question of Realism," *Philosophers' Imprint* 1 (2001). Argues that the central question of metaphysics is "What grounds what?"—a question that requires essentialist analysis to answer.

- Thomasson, Amie, *Ordinary Objects* (2007). Argues that essence can be read off from the application conditions of ordinary concepts; what is essential to tables is what the concept "table" requires.

- Jackson, Frank, *From Metaphysics to Ethics* (1998). Defends the methodology of conceptual analysis as a route to essential truths; philosophical analysis identifies what is conceptually and hence essentially required.

## Contemporary Works

- Dasgupta, Shamik, "Essentialism and the Exceptions to Grounding," *Oxford Studies in Metaphysics* 11 (2019). Examines the limits of essentialist grounding and cases where the standard account faces difficulties.

- Rosen, Gideon, "Real Definition," *Analytic Philosophy* 56 (2015). A careful examination of the concept of real definition, its relationship to essence, and its role in metaphysical methodology.
