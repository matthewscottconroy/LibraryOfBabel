# Important Figures: The Scientific Realism Debate

## Bas van Fraassen (born 1941)
The most influential contemporary anti-realist: his *The Scientific Image* (1980) defends constructive empiricism—the aim of science is empirical adequacy (saving the phenomena), not truth about unobservables. We should be agnostic about unobservable entities.

## Hilary Putnam (1926–2016)
Made the "no miracles argument" for scientific realism famous: the predictive success of science would be miraculous if our theories were not at least approximately true. Also developed internal realism and later forms of pragmatic realism.

## Larry Laudan (1941–2024)
Developed the "pessimistic meta-induction": past successful theories have been superseded and their unobservable entities abandoned (caloric, phlogiston, ether); by induction, current theories are probably false. This is the main anti-realist argument from history.

## Stathis Psillos (born 1965)
Contemporary defender of scientific realism who has developed sophisticated responses to the pessimistic meta-induction and articulated a refined structural realism and a "divide and conquer" strategy for maintaining realism about the successful parts of past theories.
