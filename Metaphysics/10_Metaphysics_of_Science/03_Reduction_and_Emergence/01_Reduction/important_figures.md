# Important Figures: Reduction

## Ernest Nagel (1901–1985)
Developed the classical model of intertheoretic reduction: a higher-level theory is reduced to a lower-level theory by deriving the former from the latter plus bridge principles connecting the vocabularies. Nagel's model was the standard account of reduction for decades.

## Paul Oppenheim (1885–1977) and Hilary Putnam (1926–2016)
Developed the unity of science program in "Unity of Science as a Working Hypothesis" (1958): the sciences form a hierarchy from physics through chemistry, biology, psychology, and the social sciences; reduction from each level to the next is possible in principle.

## Paul and Patricia Churchland
Patricia Churchland (born 1943) and Paul Churchland (born 1942): the most prominent advocates of eliminative materialism, arguing that folk psychological categories (beliefs, desires) will be replaced rather than reduced by a mature neuroscience.

## Jaegwon Kim (1934–2019)
Argued against non-reductive physicalism: genuine causal efficacy for mental properties requires reduction to physical properties. His "multiple realization" problem for functionalism and his supervenience argument together push toward either reduction or elimination.
