# Important Figures: Thermodynamics and Philosophy

## Ludwig Boltzmann (1844–1906)
Developed statistical mechanics, grounding thermodynamics in the mechanics of particles; introduced the H-theorem and the statistical interpretation of entropy (S = k log W). His work raised profound questions about the relationship between time-symmetric microphysics and the time-asymmetric second law.

## James Clerk Maxwell (1831–1879)
Introduced Maxwell's demon—a thought experiment involving a demon that sorts fast and slow molecules—challenging whether the second law is absolute. The demon was eventually resolved by Szilard and Landauer's work on the thermodynamics of information.

## David Albert (born 1954)
Developed the "statistical mechanics of the Past Hypothesis"—the account that the thermodynamic arrow of time traces to a cosmological boundary condition (the low entropy of the early universe) combined with statistical mechanics.

## Huw Price (born 1953)
Argued for a time-symmetric perspective on thermodynamics and physics: our intuitive asymmetries (including the thermodynamic arrow) result from our time-biased perspective; fundamental physics should be formulated time-symmetrically.
