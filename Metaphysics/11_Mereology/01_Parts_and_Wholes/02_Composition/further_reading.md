# Further Reading: Composition

**On the Special Composition Question:**
- Van Inwagen, P. (1990). *Material Beings*. Cornell University Press. [The essential starting point.]
- Markosian, N. (1998). "Brutal Composition." *Philosophical Studies*, 92(3), 211–249. [Argues that composition has no principled necessary and sufficient conditions.]

**On universalism:**
- Lewis, D. K. (1991). *Parts of Classes*. Blackwell.
- Sider, T. (2001). *Four-Dimensionalism*, ch. 4. Oxford University Press.

**On nihilism:**
- Dorr, C. & Rosen, G. (2002). "Composition as a Fiction." In R. Gale (ed.), *The Blackwell Guide to Metaphysics*. Blackwell.
- Unger, P. (1979). "I Do Not Exist." In G. F. Macdonald (ed.), *Perception and Identity*. Macmillan.

**On restricted composition:**
- Markosian, N. (1998). "Brutal Composition." *Philosophical Studies*.
- Hudson, H. (2001). *A Materialist Metaphysics of the Human Person*. Cornell University Press.

**On verbal disputes:**
- Hirsch, E. (2002). "Quantifier Variance and Realism." *Philosophical Issues*, 12, 51–73.
- Thomasson, A. (2007). *Ordinary Objects*. Oxford University Press.
