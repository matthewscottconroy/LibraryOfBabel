# Further Reading: The Constitution Relation

**Primary texts:**
- Baker, L. R. (2000). *Persons and Bodies: A Constitution View*. Cambridge University Press.
- Baker, L. R. (2007). *The Metaphysics of Everyday Life*. Cambridge University Press.
- Thomson, J. J. (1998). "The Statue and the Clay." *Noûs*, 32(2), 149–173.
- Fine, K. (2003). "The Non-Identity of a Material Thing and Its Matter." *Mind*, 112(446), 195–234.

**Overviews and debates:**
- Rea, M. C. (ed.) (1997). *Material Constitution: A Reader*. Rowman & Littlefield. [Excellent anthology.]
- Zimmerman, D. (1995). "Theories of Masses and Problems of Constitution." *Philosophical Review*, 104(1), 53–110.
- Johnston, M. (1992). "Constitution Is Not Identity." *Mind*, 101(401), 89–105.

**Related topics:**
- Wiggins, D. (2001). *Sameness and Substance Renewed*. Cambridge University Press.
- Lowe, E. J. (2002). *A Survey of Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press. [Chapters 4–5.]
