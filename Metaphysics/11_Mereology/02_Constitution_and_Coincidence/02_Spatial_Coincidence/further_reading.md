# Further Reading: Spatial Coincidence

**Primary texts:**
- Sider, T. (2001). *Four-Dimensionalism*, ch. 5. Oxford University Press.
- Lewis, D. K. (1976). "Survival and Identity." In A. Rorty (ed.), *The Identities of Persons*. University of California Press.
- Thomson, J. J. (1983). "Parthood and Identity Across Time." *Journal of Philosophy*, 80(4), 201–220.

**Against coincidence (eliminativist):**
- Van Inwagen, P. (1990). *Material Beings*, chs. 8–9. Cornell University Press.
- Burke, M. B. (1992). "Copper Statues and Pieces of Copper." *Analysis*, 52(1), 12–17.

**For coincidence:**
- Gibbard, A. (1975). "Contingent Identity." *Journal of Philosophical Logic*, 4(2), 187–221.
- Yablo, S. (1987). "Identity, Essence, and Indiscernibility." *Journal of Philosophy*, 84(6), 293–314.

**Hylomorphic approaches:**
- Koslicki, K. (2008). *The Structure of Objects*. Oxford University Press.
- Fine, K. (1999). "Things and Their Parts." *Midwest Studies in Philosophy*, 23(1), 61–74.
