# Further Reading: Vagueness and Mereology

**On vagueness generally:**
- Keefe, R. (2000). *Theories of Vagueness*. Cambridge University Press. [Comprehensive survey and defense of supervaluationism.]
- Williamson, T. (1994). *Vagueness*. Routledge. [Classic defense of epistemicism.]
- Hyde, D. & Raffman, D. (2018). "Sorites Paradox." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.

**On ontic vagueness:**
- Evans, G. (1978). "Can There Be Vague Objects?" *Analysis*, 38(4), 208.
- Barnes, E. (2010). "Ontic Vagueness: A Guide for the Perplexed." *Noûs*, 44(4), 601–627.
- Williams, J. R. G. (2008). "Ontic Vagueness and Metaphysical Indeterminacy." *Philosophy Compass*, 3(4), 763–788.

**On vagueness and composition:**
- Sider, T. (2001). *Four-Dimensionalism*, ch. 4. Oxford University Press.
- Markosian, N. (1998). "Brutal Composition." *Philosophical Studies*, 92(3), 211–249.
- Hawley, K. (2002). *How Things Persist*. Oxford University Press.
