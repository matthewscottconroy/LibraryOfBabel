# Further Reading: Temporal Mereology

**Primary four-dimensionalist texts:**
- Lewis, D. K. (1986). *On the Plurality of Worlds*, sec. 4.2. Blackwell.
- Sider, T. (2001). *Four-Dimensionalism*. Oxford University Press.
- Hawley, K. (2001). *How Things Persist*. Oxford University Press.

**Stage theory:**
- Sider, T. (1996). "All the World's a Stage." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy*, 74(3), 433–453.
- Hawley, K. (2005). "Genuine Temporal Parts and Personal Identity." *Philosophical Perspectives*, 19(1), 153–167.

**Endurantist responses:**
- Thomson, J. J. (1983). "Parthood and Identity Across Time." *Journal of Philosophy*, 80(4), 201–220.
- Haslanger, S. (2003). "Persistence Through Time." In M. Loux & D. Zimmerman (eds.), *The Oxford Handbook of Metaphysics*. Oxford University Press.
- Haslanger, S. & Kurtz, R. (eds.) (2006). *Persistence: Contemporary Readings*. MIT Press.

**On temporary intrinsics:**
- Johnston, M. (1987). "Is There a Problem About Persistence?" *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society*, suppl. vol. 61, 107–135.
