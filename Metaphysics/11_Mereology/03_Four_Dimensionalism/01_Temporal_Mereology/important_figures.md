# Important Figures: Temporal Mereology

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** — Foundational defender of temporal parts and four-dimensionalism; the argument from temporary intrinsics in *On the Plurality of Worlds* (1986) is the starting point of contemporary debate.

**Theodore Sider (b. 1967)** — *Four-Dimensionalism* (2001) is the most comprehensive contemporary defense; systematizes the arguments and applies them to coincidence, vagueness, and personal identity.

**Katherine Hawley** — Author of *How Things Persist* (2001), which defends a sophisticated version of four-dimensionalism (the stage view) and surveys the whole debate carefully.

**Judith Jarvis Thomson (1929–2020)** — "Parthood and Identity Across Time" (1983) offered an influential endurantist critique of temporal parts.

**Ernest Sosa (b. 1940)** — "Surviving Matters" and related work defend endurantism against temporal-parts arguments.

**Cody Gilmore** — Has worked on the metaphysics of location and the precise formulation of the persistence debate, clarifying what "wholly present at a time" means.
