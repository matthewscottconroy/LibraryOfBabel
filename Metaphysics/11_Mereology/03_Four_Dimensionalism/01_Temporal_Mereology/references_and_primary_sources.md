# References and Primary Sources: Temporal Mereology

## Foundational Texts

- Lewis, David, "Survival and Identity," in *The Identities of Persons* edited by Rorty (1976). Defends temporal parts: persons persist by having distinct temporal stages; personal identity over time is analyzed through the relations between these stages.

- Sider, Theodore, *Four-Dimensionalism: An Ontology of Persistence and Time* (2001). The most systematic contemporary defense of four-dimensionalism; argues that temporal parts are as real as spatial parts and that the four-dimensional framework resolves major puzzles.

- Quine, W.V.O., "Identity, Ostension, and Hypostasis," in *From a Logical Point of View* (1953). Early analytic defense of temporal parts; argues that objects are four-dimensional entities extended in time as well as space.

## Contemporary Works

- Hawley, Katherine, *How Things Persist* (2001). A careful comparative examination of endurance (three-dimensionalism) and perdurance (four-dimensionalism), assessing their relative merits for handling the puzzles of persistence.

- Haslanger, Sally, "Persistence through Time," in *The Oxford Handbook of Metaphysics* (2003). A survey of the main positions on persistence and their relationship to temporal ontology.
