# Exercises: Perdurance vs. Endurance

1. **Define the views.** Give precise definitions of perdurantism and endurantism. What is the difference between "being wholly present at t" (endurantism) and "having a temporal part at t" (perdurantism)?

2. **Temporary intrinsics.** A banana is green on Monday and yellow on Tuesday. State Lewis's argument that endurantism cannot account for this change. Identify the hidden premise. Which of the three endurantist responses (time-indexed properties, property-as-relation, adverbialism) is most defensible? Why?

3. **Coincidence dissolved?** Explain how perdurantism dissolves the statue-lump coincidence problem. Is this a genuine solution or a relocation of the problem?

4. **Change and personal identity.** Consider a person who undergoes radical personality change (through accident, conversion, or brainwashing). Does perdurantism give a more or less satisfying account of whether this is "the same person" than endurantism? What moral implications follow?

5. **Relativistic spacetime.** Minkowskian spacetime treats space and time symmetrically. Does this support perdurantism over endurantism? Or can an endurantist give an equally natural interpretation of relativistic physics?

6. **Are temporal parts bizarre?** Lewis says temporal parts are no more strange than spatial parts. Do you agree? Develop the strongest case that temporal parts are genuinely more puzzling than spatial parts, and the strongest reply.
