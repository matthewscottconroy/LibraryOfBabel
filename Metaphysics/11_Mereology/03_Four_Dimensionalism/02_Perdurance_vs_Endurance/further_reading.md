# Further Reading: Perdurance vs. Endurance

**Classic statements:**
- Lewis, D. K. (1986). *On the Plurality of Worlds*, sec. 4.2. Blackwell. [The temporary intrinsics argument.]
- Johnston, M. (1987). "Is There a Problem About Persistence?" *Proc. Aristotelian Society*, suppl. 61, 107–135.

**Four-dimensionalist:**
- Sider, T. (2001). *Four-Dimensionalism*, chs. 3–4. Oxford University Press.
- Hawley, K. (2001). *How Things Persist*. Oxford University Press.

**Endurantist:**
- Haslanger, S. (1989). "Endurance and Temporary Intrinsics." *Analysis*, 49(3), 119–125.
- Merricks, T. (1994). "Endurance and Indiscernibility." *Journal of Philosophy*, 91(4), 165–184.
- van Inwagen, P. (1990). "Four-Dimensional Objects." *Noûs*, 24(2), 245–255.

**Surveys:**
- Haslanger, S. (2003). "Persistence Through Time." In Loux & Zimmerman (eds.), *Oxford Handbook of Metaphysics*.
- Haslanger, S. & Kurtz, R. (eds.) (2006). *Persistence: Contemporary Readings*. MIT Press. [Best anthology.]
