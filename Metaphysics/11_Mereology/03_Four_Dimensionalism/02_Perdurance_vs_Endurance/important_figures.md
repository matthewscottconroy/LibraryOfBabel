# Important Figures: Perdurance vs. Endurance

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** — The temporary intrinsics argument is the main entry point; see *On the Plurality of Worlds* and "Survival and Identity."

**Theodore Sider (b. 1967)** — Extended Lewis's case in *Four-Dimensionalism*; also explored the vagueness argument for perdurance.

**Mark Johnston** — "Is There a Problem About Persistence?" (1987) is a rigorous endurantist reply that challenges Lewis's argument; Johnston defends the time-indexed property view.

**Sally Haslanger (b. 1955)** — Has developed sophisticated endurantist positions, including an account of temporal predication that avoids Lewis's objections, and edited influential anthologies on persistence.

**Trenton Merricks (b. 1965)** — *Objects and Persons* (2001) defends a form of eliminativism about ordinary objects that bears on the perdurance debate.

**Peter van Inwagen (b. 1942)** — An endurantist who resists temporal parts; his defense of organicism entails that organisms endure while tables (which don't exist) do neither.

**Dean Zimmerman** — Has articulated influential objections to temporal parts from an endurantist perspective.
