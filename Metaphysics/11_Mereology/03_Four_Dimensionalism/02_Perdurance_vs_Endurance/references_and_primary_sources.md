# References and Primary Sources: Perdurance vs. Endurance

## Foundational Texts

- van Inwagen, Peter, "Four-Dimensional Objects," *Noûs* 24 (1990). Defends endurantism by arguing that temporal parts are conceptually problematic; objects persist wholly at each time without having temporal parts.

- Lewis, David, "Rearrangement of Particles: Reply to Lowe," *Analysis* 48 (1988). Defends perdurantism and temporal parts against objections; develops the standard perdurantist treatment of intrinsic properties through time.

- Haslanger, Sally, "Endurance and Temporary Intrinsics," *Analysis* 49 (1989). Examines the problem of temporary intrinsics as an argument for four-dimensionalism; argues that endurantists must relativize properties to times.

## Contemporary Works

- Sider, Theodore, *Four-Dimensionalism* (2001). The definitive contemporary defense of perdurance; chapters 4 and 5 address the problem of temporary intrinsics, coincident entities, and the argument from vagueness.

- Crisp, Thomas and Smith, Donald, "Temporal Parts," *Philosophical Quarterly* 55 (2005). A concise critical examination of the main arguments for and against temporal parts, with replies to standard objections.
