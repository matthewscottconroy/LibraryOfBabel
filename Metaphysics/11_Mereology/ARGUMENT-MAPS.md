## Map 2: Classical Mereology — Key Axioms and Their Consequences

```
CLASSICAL MEREOLOGY (Simons 1987)
              │
CORE AXIOMS:
   ├── PPT (proper part) is irreflexive, asymmetric, transitive
   ├── Weak supplementation: x PP y → ∃z (z PP y ∧ ¬z O x)
   ├── Strong supplementation: ¬(y PP x) → ∃z (z PP y ∧ ¬z O x)
   └── Unrestricted fusion: ∀φ ∃y (y = Σ{z: φz})
              │

   ├── UNIQUENESS OF COMPOSITION:
   │   If x and y have exactly the same parts, x = y
```
