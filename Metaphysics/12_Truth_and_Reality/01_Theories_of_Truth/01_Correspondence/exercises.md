# Exercises: Correspondence Theory

1. **The basic account.** State the correspondence theory of truth as precisely as you can. What are the two relata of the correspondence relation? Give three examples where the theory seems to work well, and three where it struggles.

2. **Negative truths.** "The unicorn is not in the room" is true. What does it correspond to? Evaluate the proposal that it corresponds to the absence of a unicorn-in-the-room. Is an absence a real feature of the world?

3. **Mathematical truth.** "The square root of 2 is irrational" is true. If correspondence theory is correct, what fact does it correspond to? Assess whether Platonism (abstract mathematical facts) is the right answer, and what alternatives exist.

4. **Wittgenstein's picture theory.** Explain the picture theory of the *Tractatus*. What is the "logical form" that a proposition shares with its corresponding fact? What happens to the picture theory for molecular propositions (conjunctions, disjunctions)?

5. **The regress argument.** Construct the regress argument against correspondence theory precisely. Does the deflationist response (that "corresponds to" is doing no real work) adequately meet it?

6. **Identity theory.** Jennifer Hornsby argues that a true proposition just *is* the fact it supposedly corresponds to—so correspondence collapses into identity. Evaluate this claim. What would it mean for correspondence theory?
