# Further Reading: Correspondence Theory

**Primary texts:**
- Russell, B. (1912). *The Problems of Philosophy*, ch. 12. Home University Library.
- Austin, J. L. (1950). "Truth." *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society*, suppl. vol. 24, 111–128.
- Wittgenstein, L. (1921/1961). *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus*. Routledge.

**Contemporary discussions:**
- Armstrong, D. M. (1997). *A World of States of Affairs*. Cambridge University Press.
- Fumerton, R. (2002). "Correspondence Truth." In *Realism and the Correspondence Theory of Truth*. Rowman & Littlefield.
- Vision, G. (2004). *Veritas: The Correspondence Theory and Its Critics*. MIT Press.

**Survey:**
- David, M. (2016). "The Correspondence Theory of Truth." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*. [Comprehensive survey.]

**Against correspondence:**
- Strawson, P. F. (1950). "Truth." *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society*, suppl. vol. 24, 129–156. [Classic critique of Austin.]
- Rorty, R. (1991). *Objectivity, Relativism, and Truth*. Cambridge University Press.
