# Exercises: Coherence Theory

1. **Coherence defined.** What does "coherence" mean in the coherence theory of truth? Is logical consistency sufficient? What other coherence relations might be required (explanatory support, probabilistic coherence)?

2. **The input objection.** The correspondence theorist says coherence theory severs truth from reality—a perfectly coherent fiction would count as true. State this objection precisely and evaluate the best coherentist replies.

3. **Idealism and coherentism.** Why did British Idealists find coherentism attractive? What is the connection between idealist metaphysics and a coherentist theory of truth?

4. **Multiple coherent systems.** Can there be two mutually incompatible but internally coherent belief systems? If so, does coherentism imply that both are true? How does the coherentist respond?

5. **Coherence as justification vs. truth.** Many contemporary philosophers accept coherentism as a theory of *justification* (coherence makes beliefs justified) while rejecting it as a theory of *truth*. Is this a stable position? Does a good justification theory need to match a true truth theory?

6. **Bradley's Absolute.** Bradley holds that the Absolute—a completely coherent whole of reality—is absolutely true. What is the relationship between partial truths and the Absolute? Is Bradley's account of degrees of truth plausible?
