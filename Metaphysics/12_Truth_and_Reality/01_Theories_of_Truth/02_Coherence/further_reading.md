# Further Reading: Coherence Theory

**Primary idealist texts:**
- Bradley, F. H. (1893). *Appearance and Reality*. Clarendon Press. [Part II, ch. 15.]
- Joachim, H. H. (1906). *The Nature of Truth*. Clarendon Press.

**Against coherence:**
- Russell, B. (1907). "The Monistic Theory of Truth." In *Philosophical Essays* (1910). Longmans.
- Moore, G. E. (1899). "The Nature of Judgment." *Mind*, 8(30), 176–193.

**Contemporary coherentism in epistemology:**
- BonJour, L. (1985). *The Structure of Empirical Knowledge*. Harvard University Press.
- Lehrer, K. (1990). *Theory of Knowledge*. Westview Press.

**Survey:**
- Young, J. O. (2018). "The Coherence Theory of Truth." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.

**Related:**
- Blanshard, B. (1939). *The Nature of Thought*, vol. 2. Allen & Unwin. [Systematic coherentist metaphysics.]
