# References and Primary Sources: Coherence Theory of Truth

## Foundational Texts

- Bradley, F.H., *Appearance and Reality* (1893). The idealist coherence theory: ultimate truth is the coherence of the whole; individual judgments are true to the degree they cohere with the total system of reality. Partial truths are abstractions from the absolute.

- Blanshard, Brand, *The Nature of Thought* (1939). The most systematic idealist coherence theory: truth is identical with a complete, comprehensive system of mutually implicating judgments; a judgment is true to the degree it coheres with the whole.

- Young, James O., *Truth and the Coherence Theory of Truth* (2001). A contemporary defense of coherence theory that attempts to avoid standard objections, arguing that coherence with the right set of beliefs provides the best account of truth.

## Contemporary Works

- Rescher, Nicholas, *The Coherence Theory of Truth* (1973). A sophisticated contemporary coherence theory: truth is coherence with an optimally comprehensive and self-consistent system; provides a decision procedure for truth.

- Walker, Ralph, *The Coherence Theory of Truth* (1989). A careful examination and defense of coherence theory, with responses to the main objections.
