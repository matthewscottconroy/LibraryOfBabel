# Further Reading: Deflationism

**Primary texts:**
- Horwich, P. (1990). *Truth*. Blackwell. [The standard minimalist reference; revised edition 1998.]
- Quine, W. V. O. (1970). *Philosophy of Logic*, ch. 1. Harvard University Press.
- Tarski, A. (1944). "The Semantic Conception of Truth." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research*, 4(3), 341–375.

**Prosentential theory:**
- Grover, D., Camp, J., & Belnap, N. (1975). "A Prosentential Theory of Truth." *Philosophical Studies*, 27(2), 73–125.

**Critical discussions:**
- Soames, S. (1999). *Understanding Truth*. Oxford University Press.
- Field, H. (1994). "Deflationist Views of Meaning and Content." *Mind*, 103(411), 249–285.
- Wright, C. (1992). *Truth and Objectivity*. Harvard University Press. [Argues deflationism understates truth's role.]

**On the Liar paradox:**
- Beall, J. C. (2009). *Spandrels of Truth*. Oxford University Press.
- Priest, G. (2006). *In Contradiction*. 2nd ed. Oxford University Press.
