# Further Reading: The Truthmaker Relation

**Primary texts:**
- Armstrong, D. M. (2004). *Truth and Truthmakers*. Cambridge University Press. [The essential reference.]
- Mulligan, K., Simons, P., & Smith, B. (1984). "Truth-Makers." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research*, 44(3), 287–321. [Early foundational paper.]

**Surveys and anthologies:**
- Beebee, H. & Dodd, J. (eds.) (2005). *Truthmakers: The Contemporary Debate*. Oxford University Press.
- Rami, A. (ed.) (2009). *Truth and Truth-Making*. Acumen. [Good collection of key papers.]

**Critical discussions:**
- Lewis, D. K. (2001). "Truthmaking and Difference-Making." *Noûs*, 35(4), 602–615.
- Restall, G. (1996). "Truthmakers, Entailment and Necessity." *Australasian Journal of Philosophy*, 74(2), 331–340.
- Rodriguez-Pereyra, G. (2005). "Why Truthmakers." In Beebee & Dodd (eds.).
