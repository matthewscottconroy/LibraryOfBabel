# Important Figures: The Truthmaker Relation

**D. M. Armstrong (1926–2014)** — The most systematic developer of truthmaker theory; *Truth and Truthmakers* (2004) and *A World of States of Affairs* (1997) are the key texts.

**C. B. Martin (1924–2008)** — Developed the idea of truthmakers in terms of particular property instances (tropes/particular qualities); influential precursor to contemporary truthmaker theory.

**Barry Smith (b. 1952)** — Applied truthmaker theory to formal ontology and developed connections to Husserl's work on states of affairs.

**Ross Cameron (b. 1979)** — *The Moving Spotlight* (2015) and related papers on sparse truthmaker theory; argues that not all truths need truthmakers.

**Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra** — Developed "truthmaker necessitarianism" and explored which truths require truthmakers.

**E. J. Lowe (1950–2014)** — Developed a four-category ontology in which essence and kind-nature ground truthmaking.
