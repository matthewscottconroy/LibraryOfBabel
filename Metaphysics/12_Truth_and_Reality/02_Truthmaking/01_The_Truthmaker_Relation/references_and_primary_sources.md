# References and Primary Sources: The Truthmaker Relation

## Foundational Texts

- Armstrong, David, *Truth and Truthmakers* (2004). The definitive contemporary treatment: truthmakers are states of affairs; truthmaking is a relation of necessitation; the truthmaker principle states that every truth has a truthmaker.

- Mulligan, Kevin, Simons, Peter, and Smith, Barry, "Truth-Makers," *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 44 (1984). The paper that introduced truthmaking as a technical concept in analytic philosophy; defends tropes (moments) as the fundamental truthmakers.

- Rodriguez-Pereyra, Gonzalo, *Resemblance Nominalism: A Solution to the Problem of Universals* (2002). Contains an important account of truthmaking and the truthmaker principle; argues for a modal necessitation account.

## Contemporary Works

- Merricks, Trenton, *Truth and Ontology* (2007). A critical examination of truthmaker theory; argues that the truthmaker principle is false—negative truths and modal truths have no suitable truthmakers.

- MacBride, Fraser, "Truthmakers," *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2020). A comprehensive survey of truthmaker theory, the main versions, and the central objections.
