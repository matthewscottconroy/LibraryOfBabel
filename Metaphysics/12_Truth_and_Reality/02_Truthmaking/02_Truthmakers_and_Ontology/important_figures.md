# Important Figures: Truthmakers and Ontology

**D. M. Armstrong (1926–2014)** — Defended states of affairs as the canonical truthmakers; *A World of States of Affairs* (1997) is the comprehensive account.

**Keith Campbell (1938–2020)** — *Abstract Particulars* (1990) developed trope theory as an alternative to universals and used tropes as truthmakers.

**C. B. Martin (1924–2008)** — Advocated tropes (particular qualities) as the fundamental ontological category and primary locus of truthmaking.

**Kit Fine (b. 1946)** — Connected essence to truthmaking and grounding; "Essence and Modality" (1994) and later work on grounding are foundational.

**E. J. Lowe (1950–2014)** — *The Four-Category Ontology* (2006) uses kinds, attributes, objects, and tropes in an interlocking system to ground truthmaking.

**Jonathan Schaffer (b. 1973)** — Developed "monist truthmaking" connecting truthmakers to the grounding debate.
