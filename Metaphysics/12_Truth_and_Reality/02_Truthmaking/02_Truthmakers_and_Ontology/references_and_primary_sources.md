# References and Primary Sources: Truthmakers and Ontology

## Foundational Texts

- Armstrong, David, *A World of States of Affairs* (1997). Develops the ontology of states of affairs as fundamental truthmakers; argues that states of affairs are the basic units of reality from which all facts derive.

- Lewis, David and Rosen, Gideon, "Postscript to 'Things qua Truthmakers'," in *Lewisian Themes* edited by Jacobs (2004). Lewis's late attempt to accommodate truthmaking within his ontology; argues that propositions are made true by the totality of the world rather than by individual states of affairs.

- Bigelow, John, *The Reality of Numbers* (1988). Applies truthmaker theory to mathematics: mathematical truths are made true by structural features of the actual world (universals and their instantiation patterns).

## Contemporary Works

- Schaffer, Jonathan, "Truthmaker Commitments," *Philosophical Studies* 141 (2008). Examines what truthmaker theory commits us to ontologically; argues for a sparse truthmaker theory that minimizes ontological extravagance.

- Cameron, Ross, *The Moving Spotlight: An Essay on Time and Ontology* (2015). Applies truthmaker theory to debates about presentism and four-dimensionalism; argues truthmaking can adjudicate between ontological theories.
