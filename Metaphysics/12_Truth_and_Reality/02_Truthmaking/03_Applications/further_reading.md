# Further Reading: Applications of Truthmaking

**Modal truthmakers:**
- Lewis, D. K. (1986). *On the Plurality of Worlds*. Blackwell.
- Armstrong, D. M. (1989). *A Combinatorial Theory of Possibility*. Cambridge University Press.
- Divers, J. (2002). *Possible Worlds*. Routledge. [Survey of all main theories.]

**Mathematical truthmakers:**
- Benacerraf, P. (1973). "Mathematical Truth." *Journal of Philosophy*, 70(19), 661–679.
- Field, H. (1980). *Science Without Numbers*. Blackwell.
- Shapiro, S. (1997). *Philosophy of Mathematics: Structure and Ontology*. Oxford University Press.

**Ethical truthmakers:**
- Mackie, J. L. (1977). *Ethics: Inventing Right and Wrong*. Penguin. [ch. 1]
- Shafer-Landau, R. (2003). *Moral Realism: A Defence*. Oxford University Press.
- Blackburn, S. (1993). *Essays in Quasi-Realism*. Oxford University Press.
