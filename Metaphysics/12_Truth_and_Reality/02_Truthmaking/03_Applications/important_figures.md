# Important Figures: Applications of Truthmaking

**David Lewis (1941–2001)** — Modal realism: possible worlds as truthmakers for modal truths; *On the Plurality of Worlds* (1986) is the key text.

**D. M. Armstrong (1926–2014)** — Combinatorialist account of modal truthmakers; *A Combinatorial Theory of Possibility* (1989).

**Hartry Field (b. 1946)** — Mathematical fictionalism: denies truthmakers for mathematical claims; *Science Without Numbers* (1980).

**Mark Balaguer** — *Platonism and Anti-Platonism in Mathematics* (1998) carefully examines the options for mathematical truthmakers.

**Russ Shafer-Landau (b. 1963)** — Moral realism with non-natural moral facts; *Moral Realism: A Defence* (2003).

**J. L. Mackie (1917–1981)** — Error theory: no moral truthmakers because no moral facts; *Ethics: Inventing Right and Wrong* (1977).

**Simon Blackburn (b. 1944)** — Quasi-realism: earns the right to speak of moral truths without positing full-blooded moral facts; *Spreading the Word* (1984).
