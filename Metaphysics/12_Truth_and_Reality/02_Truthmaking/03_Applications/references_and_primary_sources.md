# References and Primary Sources: Applications of Truthmaking

## Foundational Texts

- Armstrong, David, *Truth and Truthmakers* (2004), Chapters 5–9. Applies truthmaker theory to negative truths, mathematical truths, modal truths, laws of nature, and abstract objects; demonstrates the wide range of the truthmaking framework.

- Cameron, Ross, "Truthmakers and Ontological Commitment: Or How to Deal with Complex Objects and Mathematical Ontology Without Getting Into Trouble," *Philosophical Studies* 140 (2008). Applies truthmaking to reduce apparent ontological commitments: complex objects and numbers need not be posited if their truths are made true by simpler entities.

- Bigelow, John, *The Reality of Numbers* (1988). Mathematical truths are made true by structural features of the actual world; this provides a naturalistic account of mathematical truth without Platonism.

## Contemporary Works

- Briggs, Rachael, "The Metaphysics of Chance," *Philosophy Compass* 5 (2010). Applies truthmaking and grounding considerations to the metaphysics of probability and objective chance.

- Rodriguez-Pereyra, Gonzalo, "Truthmaking, Entailment, and the Conjunction Thesis," *Mind* 115 (2006). Examines the logical properties of the truthmaking relation, particularly the conjunction thesis and its implications for complex truths.
