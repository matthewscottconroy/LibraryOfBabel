# Further Reading: The Realism Debate

**Dummett:**
- Dummett, M. (1978). *Truth and Other Enigmas*. Harvard University Press.
- Dummett, M. (1991). *The Logical Basis of Metaphysics*. Harvard University Press.

**Contemporary realism:**
- Wright, C. (1992). *Truth and Objectivity*. Harvard University Press.
- Devitt, M. (1984). *Realism and Truth*. Princeton University Press.
- Boghossian, P. (2006). *Fear of Knowledge*. Oxford University Press.

**Anti-realism:**
- Putnam, H. (1981). *Reason, Truth and History*. Cambridge University Press.
- Van Fraassen, B. (1980). *The Scientific Image*. Oxford University Press.
- Goodman, N. (1978). *Ways of Worldmaking*. Hackett.

**Survey:**
- Miller, A. (2019). "Realism." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*. [Comprehensive.]
