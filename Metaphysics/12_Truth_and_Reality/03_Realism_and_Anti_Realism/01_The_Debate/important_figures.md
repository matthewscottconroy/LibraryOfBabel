# Important Figures: The Realism Debate

**Michael Dummett (1925–2011)** — Re-framed the realism/anti-realism debate as semantic; argued that realism is commitment to recognition-transcendent truth; *Truth and Other Enigmas* (1978) and *The Logical Basis of Metaphysics* (1991).

**Hilary Putnam (1926–2016)** — Developed internal realism as a middle path; later moved to pragmatic realism; "Two Conceptions of Rationality" and *Reason, Truth and History* (1981).

**Crispin Wright (b. 1942)** — *Truth and Objectivity* (1992) develops a nuanced pluralism about truth and realism; argues that different domains require different accounts.

**Paul Boghossian (b. 1957)** — *Fear of Knowledge* (2006) defends realism against constructivist and relativist challenges.

**Bas van Fraassen (b. 1941)** — *The Scientific Image* (1980) develops constructive empiricism, a form of anti-realism about theoretical entities.

**Nelson Goodman (1906–1998)** — *Ways of Worldmaking* (1978) argues for irreducible world-versions, each as legitimate as any other.
