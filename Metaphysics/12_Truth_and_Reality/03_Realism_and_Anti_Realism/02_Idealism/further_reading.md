# Further Reading: Idealism

**Primary texts:**
- Berkeley, G. (1710/1998). *A Treatise Concerning the Principles of Human Knowledge*. Oxford University Press.
- Kant, I. (1781/1998). *Critique of Pure Reason*. Cambridge University Press. [Transcendental Aesthetic and Analytic.]
- Bradley, F. H. (1893/1930). *Appearance and Reality*. Clarendon Press.

**On British Idealism:**
- Mander, W. J. (2011). *British Idealism: A History*. Oxford University Press. [Definitive survey.]
- Nicholson, P. P. (1990). *The Political Philosophy of the British Idealists*. Cambridge University Press.

**On Kantian idealism:**
- Allison, H. (2004). *Kant's Transcendental Idealism*. 2nd ed. Yale University Press.
- Strawson, P. F. (1966). *The Bounds of Sense*. Methuen. [Influential analytic reading of Kant.]

**Contemporary:**
- Sprigge, T. (1983). *The Vindication of Absolute Idealism*. Edinburgh University Press.
- Dunham, J., Grant, I. H., & Watson, S. (2011). *Idealism: The History of a Philosophy*. Acumen.
