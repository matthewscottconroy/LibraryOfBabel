# Further Reading: Pragmatism

**Classical texts:**
- Peirce, C. S. (1878). "How to Make Our Ideas Clear." *Popular Science Monthly*, 12, 286–302.
- James, W. (1907). *Pragmatism*. Longmans, Green. [Lectures 2 and 6 are central.]
- Dewey, J. (1938). *Logic: The Theory of Inquiry*. Henry Holt.

**Neo-pragmatism:**
- Rorty, R. (1979). *Philosophy and the Mirror of Nature*. Princeton University Press.
- Rorty, R. (1991). *Objectivity, Relativism, and Truth*. Cambridge University Press.
- Brandom, R. (1994). *Making It Explicit*. Harvard University Press. [Technically demanding but foundational.]

**Surveys:**
- Misak, C. (ed.) (2007). *New Pragmatists*. Oxford University Press.
- Hookway, C. (2013). "Pragmatism." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.

**Critical discussions:**
- Russell, B. (1910). "Pragmatism." In *Philosophical Essays*. Longmans.
- Putnam, H. (1995). *Pragmatism: An Open Question*. Blackwell.
