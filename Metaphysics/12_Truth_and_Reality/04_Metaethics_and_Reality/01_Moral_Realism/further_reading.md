# Further Reading: Moral Realism

**Primary texts:**
- Moore, G. E. (1903). *Principia Ethica*. Cambridge University Press.
- Shafer-Landau, R. (2003). *Moral Realism: A Defence*. Oxford University Press.
- Parfit, D. (2011). *On What Matters*, vols. 1–2. Oxford University Press. [Part VI.]
- Railton, P. (1986). "Moral Realism." *Philosophical Review*, 95(2), 163–207.

**Cornell realism:**
- Sturgeon, N. (1988). "Moral Explanations." In D. Copp & D. Zimmerman (eds.), *Morality, Reason and Truth*. Rowman & Littlefield.
- Brink, D. (1989). *Moral Realism and the Foundations of Ethics*. Cambridge University Press.

**Survey:**
- Sayre-McCord, G. (2023). "Moral Realism." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.

**General metaethics:**
- Miller, A. (2013). *An Introduction to Contemporary Metaethics*. 2nd ed. Polity Press.
- Fisher, A. (2011). *Metaethics: An Introduction*. Acumen.
