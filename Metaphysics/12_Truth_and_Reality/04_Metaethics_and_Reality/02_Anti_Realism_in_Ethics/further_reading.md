# Further Reading: Anti-Realism in Ethics

**Emotivism:**
- Ayer, A. J. (1936/2001). *Language, Truth and Logic*, ch. 6. Penguin.
- Stevenson, C. L. (1944). *Ethics and Language*. Yale University Press.

**Error theory:**
- Mackie, J. L. (1977). *Ethics: Inventing Right and Wrong*, ch. 1. Penguin.
- Joyce, R. (2001). *The Myth of Morality*. Cambridge University Press.
- Olson, J. (2014). *Moral Error Theory*. Oxford University Press.

**Quasi-realism:**
- Blackburn, S. (1984). *Spreading the Word*, chs. 5–6. Oxford University Press.
- Blackburn, S. (1993). *Essays in Quasi-Realism*. Oxford University Press.
- Gibbard, A. (1990). *Wise Choices, Apt Feelings*. Harvard University Press.

**The Frege-Geach problem:**
- Geach, P. T. (1965). "Assertion." *Philosophical Review*, 74(4), 449–465.
- Schroeder, M. (2008). *Being For: Evaluating the Semantic Program of Expressivism*. Oxford University Press.
