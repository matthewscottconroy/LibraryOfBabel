# Further Reading: Aesthetics and Reality

**Primary texts:**
- Hume, D. (1757/1985). "Of the Standard of Taste." In *Essays Moral, Political and Literary*. Liberty Classics.
- Kant, I. (1790/2000). *Critique of the Power of Judgment*. Cambridge University Press. [§§1–22 on aesthetic judgment.]
- Zangwill, N. (2001). *The Metaphysics of Beauty*. Cornell University Press.

**Response-dependence:**
- Wright, C. (1992). *Truth and Objectivity*, ch. 3. Harvard University Press.
- Johnston, M. (1992). "How to Speak of the Colors." *Philosophical Studies*, 68(3), 221–263.

**Anti-realism:**
- Sibley, F. (1959). "Aesthetic Concepts." *Philosophical Review*, 68(4), 421–450. [Foundational paper on aesthetic properties.]
- Levinson, J. (ed.) (2003). *The Oxford Handbook of Aesthetics*. Oxford University Press.

**Surveys:**
- Laetz, B. & Fisher, J. A. (2011). "Introduction to Aesthetics." In *A Companion to the Philosophy of Literature*. Wiley-Blackwell.
- Brady, E. & Levinson, J. (eds.) (2001). *Aesthetic Concepts: Essays after Sibley*. Oxford University Press.
