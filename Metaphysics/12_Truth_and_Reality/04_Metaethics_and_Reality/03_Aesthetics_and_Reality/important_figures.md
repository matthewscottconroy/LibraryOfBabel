# Important Figures: Aesthetics and Reality

**David Hume (1711–1776)** — "Of the Standard of Taste" (1757): aesthetic judgment involves both subjective taste and a standard grounded in ideal perception.

**Immanuel Kant (1724–1804)** — *Critique of Judgment* (1790): aesthetic judgments claim universal validity but are grounded in subjective feeling; the "free play of the faculties."

**Nick Zangwill (b. 1958)** — Contemporary aesthetic realist; *The Metaphysics of Beauty* (2001).

**Crispin Wright (b. 1942)** — Response-dependence theory applied to aesthetics; *Truth and Objectivity* (1992).

**Mark Johnston** — Response-dependence and "human world" properties; work on color and secondary qualities relevant to aesthetics.

**Kendall Walton (b. 1939)** — Influential philosophy of art and representation; *Mimesis as Make-Believe* (1990).

**Roger Scruton (1944–2020)** — Conservative aesthetic realism; *The Aesthetics of Music* (1997) and *Beauty* (2009).
