# Further Reading: Cosmological Arguments

**Primary texts:**
- Aquinas, T. (1265–1274/1948). *Summa Theologiae*, Ia, q. 2, a. 3. Benziger.
- Leibniz, G. W. (1714/1989). "The Principles of Nature and Grace." In *Philosophical Essays*. Hackett.
- Hume, D. (1779/1998). *Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion*, Parts IX–X. Oxford University Press.

**Kalam:**
- Craig, W. L. (1979). *The Kalam Cosmological Argument*. Macmillan.
- Craig, W. L. & Sinclair, J. P. (2009). "The Kalam Cosmological Argument." In Craig & Moreland (eds.), *The Blackwell Companion to Natural Theology*.

**Leibnizian:**
- Pruss, A. R. (2006). *The Principle of Sufficient Reason*. Cambridge University Press.
- Koons, R. C. (1997). "A New Look at the Cosmological Argument." *American Philosophical Quarterly*, 34(2), 193–211.

**Critical:**
- Mackie, J. L. (1982). *The Miracle of Theism*, ch. 5. Clarendon Press.
- Sobel, J. H. (2004). *Logic and Theism*, ch. 5. Cambridge University Press.
