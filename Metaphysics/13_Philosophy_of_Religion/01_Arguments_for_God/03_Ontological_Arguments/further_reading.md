# Further Reading: Ontological Arguments

**Primary texts:**
- Anselm. (c. 1078/1998). *Proslogion*, with Gaunilo's reply. In B. Davies & G. R. Evans (eds.), *Anselm of Canterbury*. Oxford University Press.
- Descartes, R. (1641/1996). *Meditations on First Philosophy*, Fifth Meditation. Cambridge University Press.
- Kant, I. (1781/1998). *Critique of Pure Reason*, A592–A602/B620–B630. Cambridge University Press.
- Plantinga, A. (1974). *The Nature of Necessity*, ch. 10. Clarendon Press.

**Surveys and debates:**
- Oppy, G. (2023). "Ontological Arguments." *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
- Sobel, J. H. (2004). *Logic and Theism*, ch. 2. Cambridge University Press.
- Leftow, B. (2011). "The Ontological Argument." In Flint & Rea (eds.), *The Oxford Handbook of Philosophical Theology*. Oxford University Press.

**Classic papers:**
- Malcolm, N. (1960). "Anselm's Ontological Arguments." *Philosophical Review*, 69(1), 41–62.
- Lewis, D. K. (1970). "Anselm and Actuality." *Noûs*, 4(2), 175–188.
