# Further Reading: Classical Theism and Divine Attributes

**Primary texts:**
- Aquinas, T. *Summa Theologiae* Ia, qq. 3–26. [On God's nature, simplicity, and attributes.]
- Anselm. *Proslogion*, chs. 5–23. [Derivation of divine attributes from the concept of greatest being.]
- Swinburne, R. (1977). *The Coherence of Theism*. Clarendon Press.

**On omnipotence:**
- Geach, P. T. (1973). "Omnipotence." *Philosophy*, 48(183), 7–20.
- Flint, T. P. & Freddoso, A. J. (1983). "Maximal Power." In A. Freddoso (ed.), *The Existence and Nature of God*. Notre Dame.

**On omniscience:**
- Plantinga, A. (1986). "On Ockham's Way Out." *Faith and Philosophy*, 3(3), 235–269.
- Zagzebski, L. (1991). *The Dilemma of Freedom and Foreknowledge*. Oxford University Press.

**On omnibenevolence:**
- Adams, R. M. (1973). "A Modified Divine Command Theory of Ethical Wrongness." In G. Outka & J. Reeder (eds.), *Religion and Morality*. Anchor.
- Wainwright, W. J. (1988). *Philosophy of Religion*, ch. 2. Wadsworth.
