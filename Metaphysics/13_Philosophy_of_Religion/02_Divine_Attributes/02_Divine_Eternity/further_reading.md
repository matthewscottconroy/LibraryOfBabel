# Further Reading: Divine Eternity

**Primary texts:**
- Boethius. (c. 524/1999). *The Consolation of Philosophy*, Book V, prose 6. Oxford University Press.
- Aquinas, T. *Summa Theologiae* Ia, q. 10 (on eternity) and q. 14 (on divine knowledge).

**For timelessness:**
- Leftow, B. (1991). *Time and Eternity*. Cornell University Press.
- Helm, P. (1988). *Eternal God*. Clarendon Press.
- Stump, E. & Kretzmann, N. (1981). "Eternity." *Journal of Philosophy*, 78(8), 429–458.

**For everlastingness:**
- Wolterstorff, N. (1975). "God Everlasting." In C. Orlebeke & L. Smedes (eds.), *God and the Good*. Eerdmans.
- Swinburne, R. (1994). *The Christian God*, ch. 4. Clarendon Press.

**Foreknowledge and freedom:**
- Zagzebski, L. (1991). *The Dilemma of Freedom and Foreknowledge*. Oxford University Press.
- Fischer, J. M. (ed.) (1989). *God, Foreknowledge, and Freedom*. Stanford University Press.
