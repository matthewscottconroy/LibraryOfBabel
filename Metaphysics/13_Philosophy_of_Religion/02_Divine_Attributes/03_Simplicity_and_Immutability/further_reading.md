# Further Reading: Simplicity and Immutability

**On divine simplicity:**
- Aquinas, T. *Summa Theologiae* Ia, q. 3.
- Plantinga, A. (1980). *Does God Have a Nature?* Marquette University Press. [Critical of simplicity.]
- Stump, E. & Kretzmann, N. (1985). "Absolute Simplicity." *Faith and Philosophy*, 2(4), 353–382. [Defense.]
- Davies, B. (2011). "Simplicity." In Flint & Rea (eds.), *The Oxford Handbook of Philosophical Theology*. Oxford University Press.

**On divine immutability:**
- Wolterstorff, N. (1975). "God Everlasting." In Orlebeke & Smedes (eds.), *God and the Good*. Eerdmans.
- Helm, P. (1988). *Eternal God*, ch. 3. Clarendon Press.

**On open theism:**
- Pinnock, C., et al. (1994). *The Openness of God*. InterVarsity Press.
- Sanders, J. (1998). *The God Who Risks*. InterVarsity Press.
- Helm, P. (2010). *Eternal God*. 2nd ed. Clarendon Press. [Against open theism.]
