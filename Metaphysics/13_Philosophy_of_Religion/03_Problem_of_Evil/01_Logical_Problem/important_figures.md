# Important Figures: The Logical Problem of Evil

**J. L. Mackie (1917–1981)** — "Evil and Omnipotence" (1955) is the canonical statement of the logical problem; *The Miracle of Theism* (1982) extends the critique.

**Alvin Plantinga (b. 1932)** — *God, Freedom, and Evil* (1974): free will defense defuses the logical problem; transworld depravity.

**Anthony Flew (1923–2010)** — Early analytic atheist; argued for the "presumption of atheism"; later controversially became a deist.

**Nelson Pike (b. 1930)** — *God and Evil* (1964): edited important early anthology; developed the formal structure of the logical problem.

**John Hick (1922–2012)** — *Evil and the God of Love* (1966): soul-making theodicy; Irenaean alternative to Augustinian theodicy.

**Peter van Inwagen (b. 1942)** — *The Problem of Evil* (2006): Gifford Lectures; defends a version of the free will defense and argues the evidential problem fails.
