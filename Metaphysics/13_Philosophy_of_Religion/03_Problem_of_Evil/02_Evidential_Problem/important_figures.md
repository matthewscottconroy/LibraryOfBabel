# Important Figures: The Evidential Problem of Evil

**William Rowe (1931–2015)** — "The Problem of Evil and Some Varieties of Atheism" (1979): definitive statement of the evidential problem; the fawn example.

**Paul Draper (b. 1957)** — "Pain and Pleasure: An Evidential Problem for Theists" (1989): comparative evidential argument using the Hypothesis of Indifference.

**Michael Bergmann (b. 1963)** — Major developer of skeptical theism; "Skeptical Theism and Rowe's Argument from Evil" (2001).

**Daniel Howard-Snyder** — Edited *The Evidential Argument from Evil* (1996); developed skeptical theist responses.

**Michael Rea (b. 1967)** — Works at the intersection of skeptical theism and theology of divine hiddenness.

**Stephen Wykstra (b. 1944)** — Introduced the CORNEA condition in "The Humean Obstacle to Evidential Arguments from Suffering" (1984); foundational skeptical theism paper.

**Marilyn McCord Adams (1943–2017)** — Horrendous evils; *Horrendous Evils and the Goodness of God* (1999).
