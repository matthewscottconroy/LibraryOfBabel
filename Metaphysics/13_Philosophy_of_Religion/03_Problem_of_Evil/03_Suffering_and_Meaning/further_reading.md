# Further Reading: Suffering, Hiddenness, and Meaning

**Divine hiddenness:**
- Schellenberg, J. L. (1993). *Divine Hiddenness and Human Reason*. Cornell University Press.
- Schellenberg, J. L. (2015). *The Hiddenness Argument*. Oxford University Press.
- Howard-Snyder, D. & Moser, P. (eds.) (2002). *Divine Hiddenness: New Essays*. Cambridge University Press.

**Meaning and suffering:**
- Frankl, V. (1946/1984). *Man's Search for Meaning*. Simon & Schuster.
- Adams, M. M. (1999). *Horrendous Evils and the Goodness of God*. Cornell University Press.
- Dostoevsky, F. (1879–1880/1990). *The Brothers Karamazov*, Book V ("Rebellion"). Northwestern University Press.

**Secular and existentialist:**
- Camus, A. (1942/1991). *The Myth of Sisyphus*. Vintage. [On confronting absurdity.]
- Wolf, S. (2010). *Meaning in Life and Why It Matters*. Princeton University Press.

**Survey:**
- Murray, M. (1999). "Deus Absconditus." In Murray (ed.), *Reason for the Hope Within*. Eerdmans.
