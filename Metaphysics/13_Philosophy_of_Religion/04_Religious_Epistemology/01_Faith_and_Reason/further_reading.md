# Further Reading: Faith and Reason

**Primary texts:**
- Clifford, W. K. (1877). "The Ethics of Belief." In *Lectures and Essays*. Macmillan.
- James, W. (1896). "The Will to Believe." In *The Will to Believe and Other Essays*. Longmans.
- Plantinga, A. (2000). *Warranted Christian Belief*. Oxford University Press.

**Natural theology:**
- Swinburne, R. (2004). *The Existence of God*. 2nd ed. Clarendon Press.
- Mackie, J. L. (1982). *The Miracle of Theism*. Clarendon Press. [Against natural theology.]

**Reformed epistemology:**
- Plantinga, A. & Wolterstorff, N. (eds.) (1983). *Faith and Rationality*. Notre Dame University Press. [The foundational collection.]
- Clark, K. J. (1990). *Return to Reason*. Eerdmans. [Accessible introduction to reformed epistemology.]

**Survey:**
- Penelhum, T. (2000). *Reason and Religious Faith*. Westview Press.
- Zagzebski, L. (1993). *Rational Faith: Catholic Responses to Reformed Epistemology*. Notre Dame University Press.
