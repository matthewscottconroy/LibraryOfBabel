# Important Figures: Faith and Reason

**Thomas Aquinas (1225–1274)** — Systematic distinction between natural theology (reason) and revealed theology (faith); both are sources of truth but serve different functions.

**W. K. Clifford (1845–1879)** — "The Ethics of Belief" (1877): the classic evidentialism statement; believing without sufficient evidence is always wrong.

**William James (1842–1910)** — "The Will to Believe" (1896): counter to Clifford; in genuine options with inadequate evidence, faith is permissible.

**Alvin Plantinga (b. 1932)** — Reformed epistemology; *Warranted Christian Belief* (2000); properly basic belief in God.

**Nicholas Wolterstorff (b. 1932)** — Co-developed reformed epistemology; *Reason Within the Bounds of Religion* (1976).

**Richard Swinburne (b. 1934)** — Probabilistic natural theology; *The Existence of God* (1979), *Faith and Reason* (1981).

**Karl Barth (1886–1968)** — Theological fideism; rejected natural theology; revelation alone provides knowledge of God.
