# Further Reading: Religious Experience

**Primary texts:**
- James, W. (1902). *The Varieties of Religious Experience*. Longmans. [Lectures 16–17 on mysticism.]
- Otto, R. (1917/1958). *The Idea of the Holy*. Oxford University Press.
- Alston, W. P. (1991). *Perceiving God*. Cornell University Press.

**Constructivism:**
- Katz, S. (ed.) (1978). *Mysticism and Philosophical Analysis*. Oxford University Press.
- Proudfoot, W. (1985). *Religious Experience*. University of California Press.

**Defense of evidential force:**
- Franks Davis, C. (1989). *The Evidential Force of Religious Experience*. Clarendon Press.
- Swinburne, R. (2004). *The Existence of God*, ch. 13. Clarendon Press.

**Neuroscience:**
- Newberg, A., d'Aquili, E., & Rause, V. (2001). *Why God Won't Go Away*. Ballantine. [Neural correlates of mystical states.]
- Barrett, J. L. (2004). *Why Would Anyone Believe in God?* AltaMira. [Cognitive science of religion.]
