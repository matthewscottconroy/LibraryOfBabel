# Important Figures: Religious Experience

**William James (1842–1910)** — *The Varieties of Religious Experience* (1902): typology of mystical states; religious experience as evidence for "something more."

**Rudolf Otto (1869–1937)** — *The Idea of the Holy* (1917): the *numinous* as the core religious experience; *mysterium tremendum et fascinans*.

**William Alston (1921–2009)** — *Perceiving God* (1991): the most rigorous analytic account of religious experience as perception.

**Steven Katz (b. 1944)** — Constructivist critique of the "perennial philosophy"; *Mysticism and Philosophical Analysis* (1978).

**Walter Stace (1886–1967)** — *Mysticism and Philosophy* (1960): "universal core" of mystical experience; phenomenological commonality across traditions.

**Caroline Franks Davis** — *The Evidential Force of Religious Experience* (1989): systematic analytic account of when religious experience constitutes evidence.

**Wayne Proudfoot (b. 1939)** — *Religious Experience* (1985): critical historical and psychological account; religious experience is interpretation all the way down.
