# Further Reading: Pluralism, Exclusivism, Inclusivism

**Religious pluralism:**
- Hick, J. (1989). *An Interpretation of Religion*. Yale University Press. [Comprehensive pluralist account.]
- Hick, J. & Knitter, P. (eds.) (1987). *The Myth of Christian Uniqueness*. Orbis Books. [Pluralist perspectives.]

**Exclusivism defended:**
- Plantinga, A. (1995). "Pluralism: A Defense of Religious Exclusivism." In T. Senor (ed.), *The Rationality of Belief and the Plurality of Faith*. Cornell University Press.
- Alston, W. P. (1988). "Religious Diversity and Perceptual Knowledge of God." *Faith and Philosophy*, 5(4), 433–448.

**Inclusivism:**
- Rahner, K. (1966). "Christianity and the Non-Christian Religions." In *Theological Investigations*, vol. 5. Darton, Longman & Todd.
- D'Costa, G. (1986). *Theology and Religious Pluralism*. Blackwell.

**Survey:**
- Meeker, K. & Quinn, P. (eds.) (2000). *The Philosophical Challenge of Religious Diversity*. Oxford University Press.
