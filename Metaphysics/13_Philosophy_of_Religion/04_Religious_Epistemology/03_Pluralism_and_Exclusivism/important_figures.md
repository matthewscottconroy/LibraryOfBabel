# Important Figures: Pluralism, Exclusivism, Inclusivism

**John Hick (1922–2012)** — *God and the Universe of Faiths* (1973), *An Interpretation of Religion* (1989): the most developed pluralism; the Real and its many expressions.

**Alvin Plantinga (b. 1932)** — "Pluralism: A Defense of Religious Exclusivism" (1995): Reformed epistemological defense of exclusivism under conditions of diversity.

**Karl Rahner (1904–1984)** — "Anonymous Christians" and Vatican II Catholic inclusivism; *Theological Investigations*, vol. 5.

**Gavin D'Costa (b. 1958)** — Critical engagement with pluralism from a Catholic inclusivist perspective; *Theology and Religious Pluralism* (1986).

**Paul Knitter (b. 1939)** — Pluralist theologian; *No Other Name?* (1985) and *Without Buddha I Could Not Be a Christian* (2009).

**Wilfred Cantwell Smith (1916–2000)** — Comparative religion; *Towards a World Theology* (1981): faith as a universal human category; specific beliefs as secondary.
