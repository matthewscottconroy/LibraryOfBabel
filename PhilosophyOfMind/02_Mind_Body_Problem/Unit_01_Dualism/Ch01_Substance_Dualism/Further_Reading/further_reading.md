# Further Reading: Substance Dualism

**Primary Sources**

- **Descartes, *Meditations on First Philosophy***, especially Meditations II and VI — The canonical formulation of substance dualism and the interaction problem.
- **Elisabeth of Bohemia, Correspondence with Descartes** — Elisabeth's acute objections to Cartesian interactionism are philosophically essential reading alongside the *Meditations*.
- **Swinburne, *The Evolution of the Soul*** (1986) — The most rigorous contemporary defense of substance dualism.

**Secondary Literature**

- **Howard Robinson (ed.), *Objections to Physicalism*** (1993) — Collects major anti-physicalist arguments, including dualist ones.
- **John Foster, *The Immaterial Self*** (1991) — Defends Cartesian substance dualism with careful attention to contemporary objections.
- **Jaegwon Kim, *Mind in a Physical World*** (1998), Chapter 1 — Clear statement of the main problems facing dualism, including the exclusion problem.
- **Simon Blackburn, "Filling in Space"** — A useful critique of how substance dualism fails to provide a genuine explanation.
- **Daniel Stoljar, *Physicalism*** (2010) — Balanced assessment that treats dualism as a serious option while arguing for physicalism.
- **C.D. Broad, *The Mind and Its Place in Nature*** (1925) — Classic survey of mind-body positions including a careful treatment of interaction dualism.
