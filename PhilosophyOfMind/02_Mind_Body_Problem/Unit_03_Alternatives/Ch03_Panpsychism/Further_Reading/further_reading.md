# Further Reading: Panpsychism

**Foundational Papers**

- **Thomas Nagel, "Panpsychism"** in *Mortal Questions* (1979) — Nagel's restrained defense of panpsychism as the least bad option given the hard problem.
- **David Chalmers, "Panpsychism and Panprotopsychism"** (2015) — Clear taxonomy distinguishing full panpsychism (experience at every level) from panprotopsychism (proto-experiential properties at the micro-level).
- **Galen Strawson, "Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism"** (2006) — Influential argument that physicalism, if taken seriously as a claim about consciousness, is committed to panpsychism.

**Book-Length Treatments**

- **Philip Goff, *Consciousness and Fundamental Reality*** (2017) — Rigorous academic defense of panpsychism; best entry point for the academic literature.
- **Philip Goff, *Galileo's Error*** (2019) — Accessible popular defense; argues that Galileo's exclusion of qualities from science is the source of the hard problem.
- **Giulio Tononi and Christof Koch, "Consciousness: Here, There and Everywhere?"** (2015) — IIT as a form of panpsychism.
- **William Seager (ed.), *The Routledge Handbook of Panpsychism*** (2019) — Comprehensive anthology covering all aspects of the debate.

**Critical Perspectives**

- **Eric Schwitzgebel and Fiery Cushman, "Expertise in Moral Reasoning?"** — On the problematic aspects of panpsychism as a philosophical position.
- **Daniel Dennett and Philip Goff, debate** (various venues) — Engaging exchange between physicalist and panpsychist perspectives.
