# Section 11.1: Newton's Scholium and Absolute Time

---

## Section Introduction

The Scholium to the Definitions in Newton's *Principia Mathematica* (1687) is a brief but enormously consequential text. Newton is aware that "time," "space," "place," and "motion" are familiar words that everyone uses but that are typically understood in ways that are technically inadequate for the purposes of mathematical physics. He wants to introduce precise technical senses of these terms before proceeding to his laws. In the process, he offers a philosophical account of the nature of time and space that went far beyond the needs of mechanics alone.

Section 11.1.1 examines Newton's Scholium and the concept of absolute time. Section 11.1.2 examines what absolute time implies and why Newton believed it was necessary.
