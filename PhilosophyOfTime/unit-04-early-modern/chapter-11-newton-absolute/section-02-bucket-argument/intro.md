# Section 11.2: The Bucket Argument

---

## Section Introduction

Newton's bucket thought experiment is one of the most famous arguments in the philosophy of physics. Simple enough to grasp in a few minutes, it raises a puzzle that took two centuries to adequately address: what determines whether a body is "really" rotating? If rotation cannot be defined purely in relational terms — as rotation relative to some surrounding matter — then it seems that absolute space (and therefore absolute time) must be real.

Section 11.2.1 presents the argument carefully. Section 11.2.2 examines Mach's critique and its implications. Both sections raise questions that contemporary philosophy of physics has not fully resolved.
