# Section 14.1: The Block Universe

---

## Section Introduction

The block universe is a picture before it is a theory. Before examining the arguments for it or the objections against it, you need to have the picture clearly in mind. This section develops the block universe thesis in sufficient detail that you can hold it stably — so that it feels not just like a slogan but like a genuine metaphysical vision.

The section then turns to the most important semantic claim that accompanies the picture: the claim that "now" is an indexical expression, not a metaphysically privileged designator. Getting clear on what it means for "now" to work like "here" is essential to understanding what the block universe view actually says and what it does not say.
