# Section 14.2: Arguments for Eternalism

---

## Section Introduction

The block universe is not just an escape from McTaggart's regress — it has independent positive arguments in its favor. Three are especially compelling and have driven much of the contemporary literature: the argument from special relativity, the truthmaker argument, and the parsimony argument. Each presses from a different direction, and together they constitute a formidable cumulative case.

This section presents each argument in sufficient detail to evaluate it. The relativity argument is perhaps the most powerful, drawing on the actual physics of spacetime. The truthmaker argument is more purely metaphysical but surprisingly forceful. The parsimony argument is methodological but not merely formal — it reflects a genuine advantage of B-theory in terms of theoretical economy.
