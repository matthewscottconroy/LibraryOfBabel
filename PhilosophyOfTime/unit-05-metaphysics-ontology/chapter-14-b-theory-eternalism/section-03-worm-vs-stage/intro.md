# Section 14.3: Worm Theory and Stage Theory

---

## Section Introduction

Suppose you accept the block universe: the universe is a four-dimensional manifold, and all temporal positions are equally real. What, then, are ordinary objects? What are *you*? You persist through time — you exist now, and you existed yesterday, and (let us hope) you will exist tomorrow. But how does this persistence work on an eternalist picture?

This section examines the two main answers that B-theorists have developed: perdurantism (worm theory) and stage theory. These are not just technical metaphysical positions; they bear directly on questions of identity, change, and what it means to be a person who endures through time.
