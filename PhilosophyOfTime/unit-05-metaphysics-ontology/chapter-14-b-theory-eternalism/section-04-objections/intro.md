# Section 14.4: Objections to B-Theory

---

## Section Introduction

The block universe is a powerful and parsimonious metaphysical picture, but it faces serious objections — objections that many philosophers find compelling enough to motivate the various forms of A-theory examined in Chapter 15. The two most persistent objections concern (1) the passage of time and (2) the phenomenology of temporal experience.

These are not merely aesthetic complaints. They are targeted at what the B-theorist claims about the relationship between the formal structure of the temporal manifold and the lived experience of being in time. This section presents both objections carefully and surveys the best B-theoretic responses.
