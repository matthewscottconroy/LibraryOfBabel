# Section 14.5: The Block Universe and Free Will

---

## Section Introduction

If the block universe is true — if the past, present, and future all exist with equal ontological status — then the future is "already there," laid out in the four-dimensional manifold. Does this mean the future is fixed? Does it mean that free will is an illusion?

This is perhaps the most common and emotionally urgent question people raise about the block universe view. It deserves a careful answer — and the careful answer is more nuanced than either "yes, the block universe eliminates free will" or "no, the block universe is completely compatible with free will." This section untangles the relevant distinctions.
