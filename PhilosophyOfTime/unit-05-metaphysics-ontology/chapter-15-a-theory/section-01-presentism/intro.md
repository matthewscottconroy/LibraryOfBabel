# Section 15.1: Presentism

---

## Section Introduction

Presentism is, in one sense, the most natural view of time. Ask anyone on the street whether the past still exists, and most people will say no — it's gone. Ask whether the future exists yet, and most will say no — it hasn't happened. Only the present is real. Presentism elevates this common intuition into a metaphysical thesis and defends it rigorously.

This section develops presentism through three subsections. We begin with the thesis itself and the arguments in its favor. We then examine the truthmaker problem — the challenge of grounding past-directed truths when the past no longer exists. Finally, we confront the challenge from special relativity, which many philosophers regard as the most serious obstacle to any presentist view.
