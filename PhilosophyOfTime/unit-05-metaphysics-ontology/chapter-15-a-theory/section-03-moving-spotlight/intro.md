# Section 15.3: The Moving Spotlight Theory

---

## Section Introduction

The moving spotlight theory represents an attempt to combine the ontological comprehensiveness of the block universe with the A-theorist's insistence on an objective, moving present. On this view, all times exist — the four-dimensional manifold is real and complete — but there is, in addition, an objectively moving "spotlight" of the present that singles out one time at a time as genuinely present. The present is not merely perspectival; it is a primitive, mind-independent, non-relational feature of reality.

The view is intellectually attractive but faces distinctive difficulties, including a notorious problem about the rate of the spotlight's movement and a regress that parallels McTaggart's. This section examines the view's formulation and its problems.
