# Section 15.4: The Open Future and Aristotle's Sea Battle

---

## Section Introduction

Running through all forms of A-theory is a concern with the openness of the future: the sense in which what is future is genuinely undetermined, genuinely open, genuinely available to be otherwise. This section examines the connection between this metaphysical intuition and the logical question of bivalence — the question of whether every proposition has a determinate truth value. Aristotle's famous discussion of the sea battle in *De Interpretatione* 9 is the classical locus for this question, and it remains philosophically productive.
