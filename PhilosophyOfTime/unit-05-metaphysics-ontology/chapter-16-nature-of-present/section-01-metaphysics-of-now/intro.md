# Section 16.1: Is Nowness Objective or Perspectival?

---

## Section Introduction

The metaphysical debate about the present has two fronts: the ontological question (do past and future events exist?) and the conceptual question (is "nowness" an objective feature of reality, or merely a perspectival one?). These questions are connected but distinguishable. You can accept that the present is metaphysically real while remaining undecided about whether "nowness" is perspectival or objective. This section focuses on the conceptual question.
