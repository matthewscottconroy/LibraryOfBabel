# Section 16.2: The Specious Present

---

## Section Introduction

The present of metaphysics — the "now" of philosophical debate — is typically understood as a durationless instant: a mathematical point on the timeline with no positive temporal extent. But this is not how the present feels from the inside. Conscious experience has what William James called a "specious present" — an interval of experienced time that feels like an extended "now" rather than a vanishing point. This section examines the specious present, its empirical character, and the philosophical debate about how to account for it.
