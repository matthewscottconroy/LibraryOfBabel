# Section 16.3: Simultaneity and Relativity

---

## Section Introduction

The relativity of simultaneity in Einstein's special relativity does not only bear on the ontological debate about what exists (Chapter 14.2.1, Chapter 15.1.3). It bears directly on the concept of the present: if simultaneity is observer-relative, then the "present moment" — understood as what is simultaneous with the observer's current temporal stage — is also observer-relative. This section examines what this means for the metaphysics of the present, and explores Reichenbach's conventionalist analysis of simultaneity.
