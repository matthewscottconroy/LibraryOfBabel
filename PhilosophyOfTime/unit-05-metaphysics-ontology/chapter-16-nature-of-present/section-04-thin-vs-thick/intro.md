# Section 16.4: The Thin Present and the Thick Present

---

## Section Introduction

Two radically different pictures of the present emerge from the preceding discussions. Physics models the present as a *thin* present: a durationless instant, a mathematical point on the real number line with zero temporal extension. Phenomenology offers the *thick* present: the specious present, a temporal interval of several seconds duration, the "saddle-back" of James's description. Can these two pictures be reconciled? Does the conflict between them reveal something important about the relationship between physics and phenomenology?
