# Section 17.1: Linear, Circular, and Branching Time

---

## Section Introduction

Three fundamental models for the topology of time dominate philosophical and physical discussion: linear time (the standard model), circular time (recurring cycles), and branching time (multiple futures). Each has a distinct mathematical structure, distinct philosophical consequences, and distinct connections to scientific and cultural thought about time.

This section develops each model in turn. The goal is to be precise enough about the structural features of each model that you can evaluate the philosophical claims made about it — to distinguish, for instance, what actually follows from circular time and what is merely dramatized in Nietzsche's rhetoric about eternal recurrence.
