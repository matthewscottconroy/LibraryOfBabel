# Section 17.2: Discrete, Dense, and Continuous Time

---

## Section Introduction

The topology of time addresses not only its global shape (linear, circular, branching) but also its local structure: what is the texture of time between any two temporal positions? Is there always another moment between them? Or are there "adjacent" moments with nothing between? This section examines the three main mathematical options: discrete time, dense time, and continuous time.

This is not merely a mathematical question. The choice between discrete and continuous time has consequences for Zeno's paradoxes, for quantum gravity, and for some of the most fundamental questions about the nature of change and motion.
