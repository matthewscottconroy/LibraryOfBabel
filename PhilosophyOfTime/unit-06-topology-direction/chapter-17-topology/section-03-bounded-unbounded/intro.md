# Section 17.3: Bounded and Unbounded Time

---

## Section Introduction

Does time have a beginning? An end? These questions are among the oldest in philosophy — Kant considered them antinomies, apparently undecidable by pure reason alone — and they have taken on new dimensions in the light of modern cosmology. The Big Bang provides what looks like a physical beginning of time, but what this means philosophically is less clear than it might seem. The Hartle-Hawking "no boundary" proposal in quantum cosmology adds a further twist: the question of whether time has a beginning may not have a yes-or-no answer.
