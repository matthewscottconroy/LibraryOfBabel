# Section 18.1: The Puzzle of Temporal Asymmetry

---

## Section Introduction

The puzzle of temporal asymmetry begins with an observation that takes a moment to see clearly: the laws of physics are symmetric in time, but the world we observe is profoundly asymmetric. This section develops both halves of the puzzle and introduces the concept of multiple "arrows" of time that must be explained or unified.
