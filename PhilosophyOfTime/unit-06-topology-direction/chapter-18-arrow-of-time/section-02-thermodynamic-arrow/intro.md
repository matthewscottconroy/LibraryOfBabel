# Section 18.2: The Thermodynamic Arrow

---

## Section Introduction

The thermodynamic arrow is the most important and best-understood of the temporal asymmetries. It derives from the Second Law of Thermodynamics and from Boltzmann's statistical mechanical interpretation of entropy. This section presents the Second Law, Boltzmann's statistical interpretation, and the deep puzzle about why entropy always increases in the past-to-future direction rather than the future-to-past direction.
