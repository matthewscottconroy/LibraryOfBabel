# Section 18.3: The Past Hypothesis

---

## Section Introduction

David Albert's "Past Hypothesis" is the most influential contemporary attempt to explain the thermodynamic arrow of time. The idea is simple but has far-reaching consequences: the thermodynamic arrow derives from the extraordinarily low entropy of the universe's initial state, and this initial state must be added as an additional postulate alongside the dynamical laws. This section presents Albert's formulation and examines the explanatory status of the Past Hypothesis.
