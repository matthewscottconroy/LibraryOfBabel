# Section 18.4: The Boltzmann Brain

---

## Section Introduction

Boltzmann's attempt to explain the low entropy of our universe as a fluctuation from thermal equilibrium generates one of the most vertiginous problems in the philosophy of physics: the Boltzmann Brain. If low-entropy states arise by fluctuation, then the most "efficient" way to produce any given observer with their current experiences is a minimal fluctuation — not the enormous cosmic low-entropy state we posit. This section develops the worry and examines the main responses.
