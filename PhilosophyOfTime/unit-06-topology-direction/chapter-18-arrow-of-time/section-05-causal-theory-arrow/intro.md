# Section 18.5: The Causal Theory of the Arrow

---

## Section Introduction

One alternative approach to explaining the arrow of time is to ground it in the asymmetry of causation rather than in entropy. The causal theory of the arrow, associated with Hans Reichenbach and developed further by David Lewis, holds that the direction of time is constituted by the direction of causal processes. This section examines Reichenbach's conjunctive fork and Lewis's asymmetry of overdetermination.
