# Section 18.6: The Psychological Arrow

---

## Section Introduction

The psychological arrow — the asymmetry between memory (of the past) and anticipation (of the future) — is perhaps the most immediately relevant arrow of time for our everyday lives. This section examines why we have this asymmetry, whether it derives from the physical arrow or is independent, and what Lewis's account of records implies about the relationship between the psychological and physical arrows.
