# Section 19.1: Hume and the Temporal Priority of Causes

---

## Section Introduction

The relationship between time and causation begins with Hume. Hume's analysis of causation in the *Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding* remains the starting point for any serious philosophical engagement with the topic, and his claim that temporal priority of cause before effect is one of the defining features of causal relations raises fundamental questions about whether this priority is conceptually necessary or merely empirically common.
