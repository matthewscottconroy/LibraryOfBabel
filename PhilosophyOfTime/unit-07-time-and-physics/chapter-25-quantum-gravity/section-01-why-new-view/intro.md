# Section 1: Why Quantum Gravity Requires a New View of Time

The attempt to build a quantum theory of gravity is not merely a technical challenge in mathematical physics. It requires a conceptual revolution, because the two theories it must unify — quantum mechanics and general relativity — embody incompatible conceptions of time. Until this incompatibility is understood clearly, it is impossible to appreciate why the problem of time in quantum gravity is so deep.

This section examines the fundamental conflict: in QM, time is a fixed, classical external parameter; in GR, time is a dynamical field, part of the spacetime metric that is determined by the distribution of matter and energy. Quantizing gravity means making the metric a quantum object — but if the metric is quantum, then time is quantum too. What can it mean for time to be in a superposition?
