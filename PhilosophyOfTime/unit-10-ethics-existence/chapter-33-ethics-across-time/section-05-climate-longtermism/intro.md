# Section 33.5: Climate, Longtermism, and the Far Future

---

## Section Introduction

Section 33.3 established that the welfare of future generations deserves moral weight and that pure time preference is indefensible. But a more radical conclusion has been gaining philosophical traction: that the moral weight of the far future is so enormous — given the potential number of people who might live in it — that improving humanity's long-run trajectory is among the most pressing moral priorities of our time.

This view, known as *longtermism*, has been developed by philosophers William MacAskill and Toby Ord, among others. It has attracted both serious philosophical attention and significant criticism. This section examines the longtermist argument, its philosophical foundations, and the major challenges it faces.
