# Section 34.1: What Is Time Travel?

---

## Section Introduction

Before we can assess the paradoxes and possibilities of time travel, we need to know what we are talking about. "Time travel" is used loosely to cover many different phenomena — relativistic time dilation, hypothetical warp drives, magic wardrobes that skip you to the past, and everything in between. Not all of these are interesting in the same way. The task of conceptual analysis is to identify the core features that any genuine case of time travel must exhibit, and to distinguish those features from superficially similar but philosophically different phenomena.

David Lewis's 1976 paper "The Paradoxes of Time Travel," published in the *American Philosophical Quarterly*, provides the clearest definition available. It is the natural starting point for any philosophical treatment of the subject.
