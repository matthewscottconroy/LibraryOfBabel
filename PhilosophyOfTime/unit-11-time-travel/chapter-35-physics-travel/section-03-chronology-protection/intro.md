# Section 35.3: The Chronology Protection Conjecture

---

## Section Introduction

The existence of CTC-containing solutions to the field equations and the theoretical possibility of wormhole time machines raised the question: does quantum physics, which was not included in the classical general relativistic analysis, change the picture? Stephen Hawking addressed this question in his 1992 paper proposing the Chronology Protection Conjecture.

The conjecture holds that the laws of physics conspire to prevent the formation of closed timelike curves — that quantum vacuum fluctuations diverge near the Cauchy horizon of a prospective time machine, generating an enormous energy density that destroys the time machine before it can be completed. If true, the conjecture would explain why we do not observe time travelers from the future: it is physically impossible to create the conditions for time travel. This section examines the conjecture, the evidence for it, and its current status.
