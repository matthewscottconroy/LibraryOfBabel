# Section 35.4: The Novikov Self-Consistency Principle

---

## Section Introduction

While Hawking's Chronology Protection Conjecture suggests that time machines cannot form, the Novikov self-consistency principle operates at a different level: it says what happens *if* CTCs exist. Rather than preventing time travel, the Novikov principle constrains it — it holds that only self-consistent solutions to the physical equations are realized in spacetimes with CTCs, automatically eliminating paradoxical histories.

This section examines the Novikov principle in its physical formulation, the "billiard ball" model that illustrates it, and the philosophical implications of a universe that is constrained to be self-consistent.
