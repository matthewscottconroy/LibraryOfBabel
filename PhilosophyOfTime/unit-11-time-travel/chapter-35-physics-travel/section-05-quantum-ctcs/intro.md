# Section 35.5: Quantum Mechanics and Closed Timelike Curves

---

## Section Introduction

The classical analysis of CTCs — through the Novikov principle and the chronology protection conjecture — leaves several questions open. The most pressing is: how does quantum mechanics, with its characteristic features of superposition, entanglement, and measurement, interact with the strange causal structure of spacetime containing CTCs?

Deutsch's (1991) analysis of CTCs in quantum mechanics is the landmark contribution to this question. By replacing the classical consistency requirement with a quantum fixed-point condition on density matrices, Deutsch shows how the grandfather paradox is resolved in a quantum setting — but at the cost of some surprising and philosophically significant implications about information. This section examines Deutsch's model, an alternative model by Lloyd et al. (2011), and the philosophical implications of both.
