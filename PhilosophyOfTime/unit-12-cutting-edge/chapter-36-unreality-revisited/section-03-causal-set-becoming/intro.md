# Section 36.3: Causal Set Theory and Discrete Becoming

---

## Section Introduction

Not all approaches to quantum gravity eliminate time. Causal set theory takes the opposite position: far from being derivative or illusory, causal structure — the ordering of events by the possibility of causal influence — is *more* fundamental than the smooth spacetime manifold of general relativity. On this view, spacetime emerges from a discrete causal structure, and the causal structure grows by the accretion of new elements — an intrinsic form of becoming that is built into the fundamental physics.

This section examines causal set theory as a framework for thinking about time, with particular attention to its treatment of temporal becoming and its relationship to A-theory and growing block views examined in earlier chapters.
