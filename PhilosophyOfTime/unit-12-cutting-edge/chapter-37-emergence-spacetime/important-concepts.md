# Chapter 37 Important Concepts: The Emergence of Spacetime

---

**AdS/CFT Correspondence**
The anti-de Sitter / conformal field theory correspondence, proposed by Maldacena (1997): a string theory in (d+1)-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime is exactly dual to a conformal field theory on the d-dimensional boundary. The bulk spacetime (with gravity) is "encoded in" the boundary field theory (without gravity). The most mathematically precise realization of holographic duality and a key piece of evidence for the emergence of spacetime from quantum information.

**Anti-de Sitter Space (AdS)**
A solution to Einstein's equations with a negative cosmological constant, characterized by a constant negative spatial curvature (like a higher-dimensional saddle). AdS space has a boundary at spatial infinity. In AdS/CFT, the string theory lives in the bulk AdS space while the dual CFT lives on the boundary.

**Causal Dynamical Triangulation (CDT)**
An approach to quantum gravity that defines the path integral over all spacetime geometries as a sum over discrete triangulations (simplicial complexes) with a causal constraint (no topology changes). The approach has produced encouraging numerical results suggesting that a smooth 4-dimensional spacetime emerges at large scales from the quantum superposition of triangulations.

**Conformal Field Theory (CFT)**
A quantum field theory that is invariant under conformal transformations (angle-preserving transformations, including scalings). CFTs are characterized by the absence of a fixed length scale and play a central role in both string theory and condensed matter physics. In AdS/CFT, the CFT lives on the boundary of the AdS space.

**Emergent Spacetime**
The view, supported by various approaches to quantum gravity, that the four-dimensional spacetime of general relativity is not fundamental but emerges from a more basic, non-spatiotemporal substrate (such as spin networks, a matrix model, or the entanglement structure of a boundary CFT). Emergent spacetime challenges the assumption, widespread in analytic philosophy, that spatiotemporal structure is the basic arena within which all other phenomena occur.

**Entanglement Entropy**
A measure of the quantum entanglement between two subsystems of a quantum system. If a quantum state is divided into two parts A and B, the entanglement entropy S_A = -Tr(ρ_A log ρ_A) quantifies the degree of entanglement between them. In AdS/CFT, the Ryu-Takayanagi formula relates the entanglement entropy of a boundary region to the area of a minimal surface in the bulk — a quantitative link between quantum information and spacetime geometry.

**ER = EPR**
The conjecture by Maldacena and Susskind (2013) that Einstein-Rosen bridges (wormholes) connecting entangled black holes are identical to the EPR (Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen) quantum entanglement between them. The conjecture implies that spatial connectivity (through wormholes) and quantum correlations (entanglement) are two descriptions of the same underlying reality, suggesting that spacetime topology is constituted by quantum entanglement.

**Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG)**
An approach to quantum gravity developed by Rovelli, Smolin, and others, which quantizes the geometry of spacetime directly without assuming a background metric. The fundamental structures of LQG are spin networks — quantum graphs representing discrete quanta of volume and area. The smooth spacetime of general relativity is expected to emerge from spin networks at large scales.

**Ryu-Takayanagi Formula**
The formula S_A = Area(γ_A) / 4G_ℏ derived by Ryu and Takayanagi (2006) within AdS/CFT, relating the entanglement entropy S_A of a boundary region A to the area of the minimal surface γ_A in the bulk that is anchored on the boundary of A. The formula establishes a precise quantitative connection between quantum information (entanglement) and spacetime geometry (area), supporting the idea that spacetime is built from entanglement.

**Spacetime Functionalism**
The philosophical position, proposed by Huggett and Wüthrich, that spacetime structure is whatever plays the "spacetime role" in the physical theory — providing a framework for spatial and temporal relations, supporting the laws of physics, etc. On this view, whether there is spacetime depends on whether the fundamental physics contains something that plays the relevant functional role, not on whether the substrate has the familiar spatiotemporal properties at the fundamental level.

**Spin Network**
A quantum graph whose nodes represent quanta of volume and whose edges represent quanta of area, proposed in loop quantum gravity as the fundamental structure from which smooth spacetime emerges. Spin networks are combinatorial and algebraic structures — not themselves spatiotemporal — that carry discrete quantum geometric information.

**Strong Emergence**
Emergence in which a macro-level property cannot, even in principle, be derived from a complete micro-level description. Strong emergence would imply that the macro-level property is ontologically additional to the micro-level facts. Chalmers (2006) argues that consciousness is strongly emergent; most philosophers and physicists prefer to explain apparent strong emergence as complex weak emergence. Whether spacetime emergence from a non-spatiotemporal substrate involves strong or weak emergence is an open question.

**Weak Emergence**
Emergence in which a macro-level property is in principle derivable from a micro-level description, even if the derivation is complex or computationally intractable. Temperature, pressure, and most macroscopic physical properties are weakly emergent from microphysics. The standard assumption in the quantum gravity research program is that spacetime is weakly emergent from non-spatiotemporal substrates.
