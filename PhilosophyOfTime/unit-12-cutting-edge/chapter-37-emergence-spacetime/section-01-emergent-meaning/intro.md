# Section 37.1: What Does "Emergence" Mean?

---

## Section Introduction

The claim that spacetime "emerges" from something more fundamental is frequently made in theoretical physics and philosophy of physics, but its precise meaning is rarely spelled out. "Emergence" is a term with multiple senses, and the sense in which spacetime might be emergent has significant implications for how seriously we should take the claim.

This section distinguishes strong and weak emergence, applies the distinction to the case of spacetime, and asks what either form of emergence would imply for the metaphysics of time.
