# 37.2.2 Philosophical Implications of AdS/CFT

---

## Huggett and Wüthrich's Analysis

Nick Huggett and Christian Wüthrich (2018: 1–21) provide the most careful philosophical analysis of the implications of AdS/CFT for the metaphysics of spacetime. Their central claim is that AdS/CFT provides genuine evidence for the emergence of spacetime: if the correspondence is correct, then the bulk spacetime structure — including its temporal structure — is not fundamental but emergent from the boundary CFT.

The key philosophical question they address is: what does "emergent" mean precisely, and does AdS/CFT really establish emergence rather than merely equivalence? Their answer is that the duality is not a trivial notational equivalence (like expressing temperature in Celsius vs. Fahrenheit) but a substantive duality between a theory with spacetime and a theory without spacetime. The boundary theory has no dynamical bulk spacetime; it lives on a fixed background. The bulk spacetime — with its dynamics, its gravitational waves, its temporal structure — is genuinely additional information that emerges from the dynamics of the boundary theory.

## The Ryu-Takayanagi Formula and Entanglement Entropy

One of the most striking specific results within AdS/CFT concerns the relationship between entanglement entropy in the boundary theory and geometric areas in the bulk. Ryu and Takayanagi (2006) showed that the entanglement entropy of a region A in the boundary CFT is proportional to the area of the minimal surface in the bulk that is anchored on the boundary of A:

S_A = Area(γ_A) / 4G_ℏ

This is a precise, quantitative relationship between a purely quantum informational quantity (entanglement entropy on the boundary) and a geometric quantity (area in the bulk spacetime).

The philosophical implication Huggett and Wüthrich draw is significant: if the geometry of the bulk spacetime is encoded in the entanglement structure of the boundary theory, then the "building blocks" of spacetime are, in some sense, entanglement relations. The spatial structure of the bulk is built from the pattern of quantum entanglement in the boundary theory. If we extend this to temporal structure as well — the time coordinate of the bulk is also encoded in the boundary — then temporal structure too is built from quantum information.

This connects to the broader speculation in quantum gravity that spacetime is "made of" quantum entanglement — a slogan sometimes expressed as "entanglement = geometry" (Van Raamsdonk 2010: 2323–2329). The Ryu-Takayanagi formula provides a specific quantitative realization of this slogan.

## Implications for Temporal Ontology

The AdS/CFT correspondence has several implications for temporal ontology.

*The derivation of time*: if the bulk temporal structure is derived from the boundary theory, then temporal structure — at least in the bulk — is not primitive. It is constituted by features of the boundary theory that are more fundamental. Temporal relations in the bulk (earlier, later, simultaneous) are grounded in features of the boundary theory that are not themselves temporal relations in the bulk.

*The fragility of temporal structure*: the bulk temporal structure can break down (at singularities) while the boundary theory remains well-defined. This suggests that temporal structure is not a necessary feature of physical reality — it can be absent even while the underlying reality (the boundary theory) remains intact. This is a concrete physical realization of the possibility that time is emergent and potentially illusory at a deeper level.

*The nature of the substrate*: the boundary theory is a quantum field theory on a fixed background spacetime (the boundary of AdS). So the boundary theory does have a temporal structure — boundary time. The sense in which the bulk temporal structure is emergent is not that it emerges from a completely atemporal substrate, but that it emerges from a simpler temporal structure (boundary time) plus quantum field theory dynamics. This is weaker than Barbour's claim that time emerges from a completely atemporal substrate, but it is still a significant form of temporal emergence.

## Caveats and Limitations

Several important caveats are needed. The AdS/CFT correspondence is a conjecture — it has not been rigorously proved as a mathematical theorem. It applies specifically to anti-de Sitter spacetimes, which have a negative cosmological constant; our universe has a positive cosmological constant (de Sitter-like). Whether a dS/CFT correspondence exists with comparable properties is an active and unresolved research question.

Moreover, Huggett and Wüthrich are careful to note that the philosophical interpretation of AdS/CFT is contested. Some physicists regard the duality as a mathematical trick without deep ontological significance; others regard it as a window into the fundamental structure of reality. The philosophical analysis is appropriately cautious about drawing stronger conclusions than the physics warrants.
