# Section 37.2: The AdS/CFT Correspondence

---

## Section Introduction

The AdS/CFT correspondence (anti-de Sitter / conformal field theory) is the most mathematically precise example of holographic duality available. Proposed by Juan Maldacena in 1997, it has become one of the most cited papers in the history of physics — and one of the most philosophically consequential.

The correspondence states that a string theory formulated in (d+1)-dimensional anti-de Sitter space is equivalent (dual) to a conformal field theory (CFT) living on the d-dimensional boundary of that space. The two theories describe the same physics but using entirely different variables: one uses strings and gravity in a curved (d+1)-dimensional bulk spacetime; the other uses quantum fields on a flat d-dimensional boundary with no gravity.

The boundary theory has one fewer spatial dimension than the bulk. The bulk spacetime — including its temporal structure — is in some sense encoded in the boundary theory. This suggests that the bulk spacetime, and its temporal structure, are emergent from the boundary theory.
