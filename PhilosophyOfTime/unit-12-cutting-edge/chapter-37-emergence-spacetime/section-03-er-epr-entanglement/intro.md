# Section 37.3: ER = EPR: Entanglement and Wormholes

---

## Section Introduction

One of the most striking conjectures in contemporary theoretical physics is the ER=EPR proposal by Maldacena and Susskind (2013): that the Einstein-Rosen bridge (a wormhole) connecting two entangled black holes is physically equivalent to the EPR (Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen) entanglement between them. Wormholes are identical to entanglement. This conjecture, if correct, implies that two features of spacetime topology that were thought to be unrelated — the connectedness of spacetime through wormholes, and quantum correlations across spatial separation — are actually two descriptions of the same thing.

The philosophical implications are significant: if quantum entanglement is more fundamental than spacetime topology, then the spatial (and temporal) structure of our universe may be, in a deep sense, built from quantum correlations.
