# Section 37.4: Philosophical Implications of Non-Spatiotemporal Ontology

---

## Section Introduction

The physical programs examined in this chapter — AdS/CFT, ER=EPR, and the broader quantum gravity program — all point toward the possibility that spacetime is not fundamental. If spacetime is emergent, then the "building blocks" of reality are something else: quantum information, entanglement structures, spin networks, simplicial complexes, or some other non-spatiotemporal substrate. This section examines the philosophical implications of this possibility and introduces "spacetime functionalism" as a response.
