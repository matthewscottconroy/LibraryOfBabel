# Section 38.3: Landauer's Principle

---

## Section Introduction

Rolf Landauer's 1961 paper established a remarkably simple but philosophically significant result: erasing one bit of information necessarily dissipates at least k_B T ln 2 of heat into the environment, where k_B is Boltzmann's constant and T is the temperature of the environment. This is Landauer's principle. It shows that information processing has physical consequences — that the logical operation of erasing information has a thermodynamic cost.

The philosophical significance of Landauer's principle extends beyond thermodynamics. It demonstrates that information is not merely a mathematical abstraction or a description of physical reality from outside; it is physically instantiated in ways that have measurable, irreversible consequences. Information has, in a specific sense, physical mass and energy.
