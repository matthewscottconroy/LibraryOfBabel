# Section 39.1: Unsolved Problems in the Metaphysics of Time

---

## Section Introduction

The central metaphysical debates about time are not resolved. After more than a century of analytic philosophy of time, after the development of tense logic, modal metaphysics, and four-dimensionalism, the deepest questions remain genuinely open. The arguments are sharper, the positions more clearly articulated, but consensus is elusive.

This section surveys three of the most important unsolved metaphysical problems: the passage problem (is the flow of time real, and if so, what is it?), the presentism-relativity impasse (can the view that only the present exists be reconciled with special relativity?), and the ontological status of the future (is the future real, and what determines its status?).
