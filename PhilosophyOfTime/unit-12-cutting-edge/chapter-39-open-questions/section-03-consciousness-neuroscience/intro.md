# Section 39.3: Time, Consciousness, and Neuroscience

---

## Section Introduction

The experience of time is among the most intimate and philosophically fertile features of conscious life. We experience time flowing, events as past or present or anticipated, durations as longer or shorter. These experiences are philosophically significant in themselves — they raise questions about the relationship between phenomenal consciousness and physical time — and they also interact with the deepest problems in the philosophy of mind.

This section surveys the frontier where the philosophy of time meets consciousness studies and neuroscience. The questions here combine empirical research (about the neural basis of temporal experience) with philosophical analysis (about the relationship between phenomenal and objective time) in ways that neither neuroscience nor philosophy can address alone.
