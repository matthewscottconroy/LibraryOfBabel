# Section 39.5: Emerging Methodological Questions

---

## Section Introduction

The philosophy of time is pursued with many different methods: formal logic, analytic metaphysics, phenomenology, philosophy of physics, and comparative philosophy. Each method has characteristic strengths and limitations, and debates about method are often implicit in substantive philosophical debates. This section surfaces those methodological debates explicitly, asking what role each approach can play in advancing our understanding of time and where the boundaries of each approach lie.
