# Section 1.6: Maxwell's Equations in Dielectric Media

## Orientation

Free space is not where photonic computing happens. Light in a photonic chip propagates through silicon, silicon dioxide, silicon nitride, lithium niobate, indium phosphide, and other materials. Each material has a characteristic electromagnetic response that determines how fast light travels through it, how much is absorbed, and how the field is distributed.

This section develops the macroscopic description of electromagnetic fields in linear dielectric media, derives the complex refractive index and its physical meaning, and examines what happens in conducting media (important for metals used in electrodes and plasmonic devices).

The boundary conditions derived in Section 1.3.4 will be applied here: they determine the conditions under which guided modes are supported by waveguide structures.
