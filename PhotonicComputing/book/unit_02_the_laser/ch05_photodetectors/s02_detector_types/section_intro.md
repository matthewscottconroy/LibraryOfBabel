# Section 5.2: Detector Types

The five detector types covered here span a dynamic range of sensitivity, speed, and integration from the workhorse of fiber optics (p-i-n diode) to the quantum-limited detector for single photons (SNSPD). Each occupies a specific niche in the photonic computing landscape.

- **5.2.1** — p-i-n Photodiodes
- **5.2.2** — Avalanche Photodiodes (APDs)
- **5.2.3** — Single-Photon Avalanche Detectors (SPADs)
- **5.2.4** — Superconducting Nanowire Single-Photon Detectors (SNSPDs)
- **5.2.5** — Germanium-on-Silicon Photodetectors
