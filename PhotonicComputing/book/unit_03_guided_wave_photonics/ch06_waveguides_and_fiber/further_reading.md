# Further Reading: Chapter 6 — Waveguides and Fiber

**Saleh, B.E.A., & Teich, M.C. (2019). *Fundamentals of Photonics*, 3rd ed. Wiley.** — Chapters 8–9 (waveguides) and 10–11 (fiber) provide the clearest introductory treatment at this level.

**Agrawal, G.P. (2019). *Nonlinear Fiber Optics*, 6th ed. Academic Press.** — Definitive on fiber nonlinearities, solitons, and amplification.

**Agrawal, G.P. (2021). *Fiber-Optic Communication Systems*, 6th ed. Academic Press.** — Comprehensive on WDM system design, dispersion management, amplification, and modulation.

**Marcuse, D. (1991). *Theory of Dielectric Optical Waveguides*, 2nd ed. Academic Press.** — The rigorous treatment of waveguide mode theory, including radiation modes, leaky modes, and coupling.

**Desurvire, E. (1994). *Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifiers: Principles and Applications*. Wiley.** — Comprehensive EDFA textbook.

**Primary papers**:
- Kao, K.C., & Hockham, G.A. (1966). *Proc. IEE*, 113(7), 1151. [Original low-loss fiber proposal]
- Miya, T., et al. (1979). *Electronics Letters*, 15(4), 106. [First <0.2 dB/km fiber]
- Desurvire, E., et al. (1987). *Optics Letters*, 12(11), 888. [First practical EDFA]
- Reed, G.T., et al. (2010). *Nature Photonics*, 4(8), 518. [Silicon optical modulators review]
