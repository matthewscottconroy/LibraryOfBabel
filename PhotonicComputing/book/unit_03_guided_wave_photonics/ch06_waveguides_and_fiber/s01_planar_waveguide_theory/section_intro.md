# Section 6.1: Planar Waveguide Theory

The planar (or slab) waveguide is the simplest guided-wave structure: a dielectric slab of thickness $d$ and refractive index $n_1$, sandwiched between two semi-infinite media of index $n_2 < n_1$ (the claddings). Despite its simplicity, it captures all the essential physics of mode guidance, cutoff, and the field distribution, which carries over qualitatively to fibers and integrated photonic waveguides.

In this section we develop the full modal analysis of the planar waveguide: the ray optic picture (which motivates TIR guidance but cannot predict mode profiles or cutoff conditions), the wave optic eigenvalue equation (which does), and the confinement factor (which connects mode theory to device performance).
