# Section 6.4: Optical Amplifiers

Without optical amplification, long-haul fiber transmission would require electro-optic regeneration every ~50–100 km: detect, retime, reshape, retransmit. For a 10,000 km submarine cable with 100 WDM channels, this means 200 electronic regenerators × 100 channels = 20,000 high-speed transceiver pairs. The erbium-doped fiber amplifier (EDFA) replaced all of this with a simple coil of Er-doped fiber pumped by a 980 nm diode laser — an amplifier that simultaneously boosts all WDM channels without O/E/O conversion.

This section develops EDFA physics, noise figure, and the engineering of amplifier chains for long-haul transmission.
