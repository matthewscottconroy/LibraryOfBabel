# Chapter 11: Further Reading and References

## Foundational Texts

**Goodman, J.W. (2017).** *Introduction to Fourier Optics* (4th ed.). W.H. Freeman.
*The definitive text on Fourier optics. Chapters 5–7 cover the lens Fourier transform, coherent optical systems, and frequency analysis of imaging systems. Anyone working with 4f processors or coherent optical systems must read this.*

**Goodman, J.W. (1985).** *Statistical Optics*. Wiley.
*The companion to Fourier Optics, covering coherence, speckle, and partially coherent imaging. Essential for understanding the noise limitations of optical analog processors.*

**Saleh, B.E.A., & Teich, M.C. (2019).** *Fundamentals of Photonics* (3rd ed.). Wiley.
*Chapter 4 covers Fourier optics; Chapter 24 covers photonic computing. A good complement to Goodman for the photonic computing context.*

---

## Optical Correlators and Pattern Recognition

**VanderLugt, A. (1964).** "Signal detection by complex spatial filtering." *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*, 10(2), 139–145.
*The matched filter paper. Essential primary source.*

**Javidi, B. (ed.) (1994).** *Optical Pattern Recognition*. SPIE Press.
*Edited volume covering optical correlator systems; includes practical performance data and comparisons to digital correlation.*

**Pepper, D.M. (1986).** "Applications of optical phase conjugation." *Scientific American*, 254(1), 74–83.
*Accessible discussion of phase conjugation, which is related to optical correlation. Good background for understanding the VanderLugt filter.*

---

## Microwave Photonics

**Urick, V.J., McKinney, J.D., & Williams, K.J. (2015).** *Fundamentals of Microwave Photonics*. Wiley.
*The comprehensive reference text. Written by researchers at the Naval Research Laboratory; technically rigorous and practically oriented.*

**Capmany, J., & Novak, D. (2007).** "Microwave photonics combines two worlds." *Nature Photonics*, 1, 319–330.
*The field-defining review paper. Essential reading.*

**Marpaung, D., Yao, J., & Capmany, J. (2019).** "Integrated microwave photonics." *Nature Photonics*, 13, 80–90.
*2019 review of the on-chip microwave photonics era. Covers integrated channelizers, beamformers, and signal processors.*

---

## Photonic ADC

**Walden, R.H. (1999).** "Analog-to-digital converter survey and analysis." *IEEE JSAC*, 17(4), 539–550.
*The Walden plot paper defining the performance envelope of electronic ADCs. Reference baseline for photonic ADC comparisons.*

**Coppinger, F., Bhushan, A.S., & Jalali, B. (1999).** "Photonic time stretch." *IEEE Trans. MTT*, 47(7), 1309–1314.
*The time-stretch ADC invention paper.*

**Khilo, A., et al. (2012).** "Photonic ADC: Overcoming the bottleneck of electronic jitter." *Optics Express*, 20(4), 4454–4469.
*Comprehensive photonic ADC performance data from MIT; demonstrates 7-bit ENOB at 40 GHz input. Best-published performance for photonic ADC in 2012.*

---

## Optical Logic

**Miller, D.A.B. (2010).** "Are optical transistors the logical next step?" *Nature Photonics*, 4, 3–5.
*The clearest statement of why optical transistors for Boolean logic don't make sense. Required reading.*

**Nozaki, K., et al. (2010).** "Sub-femtojoule all-optical switching using a photonic-crystal nanocavity." *Nature Photonics*, 4, 477–483.
*The record-low switching energy (0.42 fJ) for optical logic. Context: still 10–20× above CMOS, and slower.*

**Leuthold, J., et al. (2004).** "All-optical wavelength conversion and regeneration." *Proceedings of the IEEE*, 92(11), 1633–1652.
*Comprehensive treatment of SOA-based optical processing. The best reference for XGM and XPM in SOAs.*

**Feldmann, J., et al. (2019).** "Calculating with light using a chip-scale all-optical abacus." *Nature Communications*, 10, 1256.
*PCM-based optical logic; primary reference for Section 11.3.3.*

---

## Historical Perspective

**Goodman, J.W., Dias, A.R., & Woody, L.M. (1978).** "Fully parallel, high-speed incoherent optical method for performing discrete Fourier transforms." *Optics Letters*, 2(1), 1–3.
*Early optical DFT paper; demonstrates the parallel processing capability of optical Fourier systems.*

**Psaltis, D. (2006).** "Optics in the 21st century." *Science*, 314(5798), 419–420.
*Psaltis' perspective after the failure of optical logic; redirects toward optical analog computing and holographic memory. Prescient.*

**McAulay, A.D. (1991).** *Optical Computer Architectures: The Application of Optical Concepts to Next Generation Computers*. Wiley.
*A time capsule from the height of optical computing optimism; reading it with the benefit of hindsight illustrates which predictions were right and which were wrong.*
