# Section 23.1: Cleanroom Fundamentals

Every nanofabrication process is a sequence of three repeated operations: *pattern* (lithography), *remove* (etching), and *add* (deposition and implantation) — executed in a cleanroom because a single dust particle is larger than a waveguide. This section covers the unit operations from which every photonic process flow is assembled, with emphasis on the parameters that photonic designers inherit: minimum feature size, sidewall quality, film absorption, and thermal budget.
