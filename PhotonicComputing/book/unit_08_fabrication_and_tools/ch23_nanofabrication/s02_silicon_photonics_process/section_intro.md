# Section 23.2: The Silicon Photonics Foundry Process

The unit operations of Section 23.1 are assembled by foundries into standardized process flows, accessed by designers through a process design kit (PDK) and paid for — at research scale — through shared multi-project wafer (MPW) runs. This section covers the flow itself, the economics and logistics of getting fabricated, and the statistics of what comes back: process variation and yield, which for analog photonic computing are not quality-control footnotes but first-order design inputs.
