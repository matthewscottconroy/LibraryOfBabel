# Section 23.3: III-V Integration

Silicon's indirect bandgap makes it a hopeless light emitter (Chapter 7), so every silicon photonic system must import its gain medium from the direct-bandgap III-V semiconductors — GaAs and InP families for 850/1310/1550 nm. The engineering question is *where the III-V meets the silicon*: in a separate package, on the chip surface as a bonded die, as a printed micro-coupon, or grown directly on the wafer. This section works up that ladder, because the answer determines the cost, the coupling loss, the thermal behavior, and ultimately the wall-plug efficiency of a photonic computing system's light supply.
