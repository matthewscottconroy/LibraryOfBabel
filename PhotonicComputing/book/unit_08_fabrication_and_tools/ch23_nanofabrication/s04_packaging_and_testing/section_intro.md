# Section 23.4: Packaging and Testing

A photonic die that cannot be coupled to fibers, driven electrically, kept at temperature, and tested economically is not a technology — and in real products, packaging and test routinely account for half or more of total module cost, often quoted as high as 80% [Carroll et al., *Applied Sciences*, 2016]. This section covers the two interfaces that dominate the problem: the optical interface (fiber-chip coupling) and the electrical/thermal interface (co-packaging with electronics, thermal management, and the test and reliability flows that qualify a part for deployment).
