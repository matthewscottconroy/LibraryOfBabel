# Section 26.1: Photonic AI Hardware Companies

This section examines the companies attempting to sell photonic hardware into the AI computing market. We treat them not as a catalog but as a controlled experiment run by the market itself: several teams, similar physics, different bets on what to build — and a consistent outcome in which optical *interconnect* products found customers faster than optical *compute* products. Each subsection extracts the engineering and economic reasons behind that outcome.
