# Section 26.2: Quantum Photonics Companies

The quantum photonics industry is running several mutually exclusive architectural bets in parallel — discrete-variable fusion-based computing at foundry scale, continuous-variable computing with squeezed light, and modular component specialization — and the field is young enough that a student can still watch the selection process happen. This section profiles the principal companies as embodiments of those bets, with the loss budgets and resource-state economics of Unit VII as the constant scoring function. Funding figures and roadmap dates are a circa-2025 snapshot; the architectural logic is the durable content.
