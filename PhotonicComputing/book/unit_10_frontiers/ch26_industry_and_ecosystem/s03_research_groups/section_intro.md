# Section 26.3: The Academic Research Landscape

Behind every company in this chapter stands a university group — usually several. This section maps the academic ecosystem whose papers fill this book's reference lists, organized geographically: the Americas, Europe (with Israel), and Asia-Pacific. Two disclaimers govern everything that follows. First, any such list is incomplete the day it is written and more incomplete every year after; treat it as a starting graph for literature searches, not a ranking. Second, people move — between universities, into companies, across oceans — so verify affiliations before writing to anyone. What is durable is the *structure*: a few dozen groups, densely interconnected by co-authorship and alumni, through which essentially all of the field's talent flows.
