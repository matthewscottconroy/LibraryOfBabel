# Section 27.1: Photonic Ising Machines

This section examines the most developed instance of "computing by physics" in photonics: machines whose optical dynamics relax toward low-energy configurations of an Ising Hamiltonian, thereby acting as heuristic solvers for NP-hard combinatorial optimization. We develop the problem class and its mappings, the coherent Ising machine (CIM) built from networks of optical parametric oscillators, and — with particular care — the open questions of benchmarking, scaling, and whether these machines beat well-implemented classical algorithms.
