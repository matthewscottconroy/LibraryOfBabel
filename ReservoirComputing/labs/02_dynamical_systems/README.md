**Corresponds to:** Chapter 2 — Dynamical Systems: Attractors, Chaos, and the Geometry of Time (Unit 1)

**Prerequisites:** Basic calculus and linear algebra; familiarity with differential equations; Chapter 1 (the feedforward illusion)

**Learning Objectives:**
- Integrate the Lorenz system and visualise its strange attractor in phase space
- Measure sensitive dependence on initial conditions by tracking trajectory separation
- Estimate the maximum Lyapunov exponent λ_max empirically and interpret it as a predictability horizon
- Generate and interpret bifurcation diagrams for the logistic map
- Understand how the Feigenbaum constant emerges from the period-doubling cascade
