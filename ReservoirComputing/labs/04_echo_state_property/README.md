**Corresponds to:** Chapter 6 — The Echo State Property: Stability, Convergence, and the Critical Boundary (Unit 2)

**Prerequisites:** Chapter 5 (ESN basics), Chapter 2 (dynamical systems), spectral theory of matrices

**Learning Objectives:**
- Demonstrate the echo state property experimentally: show that all initial conditions converge to the same driven trajectory when ρ < 1
- Understand why the spectral radius ρ(W_rec) controls convergence, and why ρ < 1 is sufficient but not necessary for the ESP
- Visualise the convergence of spread across initial conditions as a function of time and spectral radius
- Observe the failure of the ESP for ρ > 1 and connect this to practical instability
- Distinguish between the echo state property (driven case) and stability of the autonomous system (undriven case)
