**Corresponds to:** Chapter 7 — Spectral Radius and Reservoir Dynamics: The Critical Parameter (Unit 2)

**Prerequisites:** Chapter 6 (echo state property), eigenvalue theory, Chapter 5 (ESN basics)

**Learning Objectives:**
- Conduct a systematic sweep of spectral radius ρ and measure its effect on task performance
- Understand the tradeoff between memory (higher ρ) and stability (lower ρ)
- Identify the empirical optimal ρ for different tasks and explain why it varies
- Connect ρ to the Lyapunov exponent of the driven reservoir system
- Visualise how the distribution of reservoir states changes with ρ
