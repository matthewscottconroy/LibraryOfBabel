**Corresponds to:** Chapter 9 — Benchmark Tasks: NARMA, Mackey-Glass, and the Standard Suite (Unit 3)

**Prerequisites:** Chapter 5 (ESN basics), Chapter 8 (memory capacity), NARMA-10 definition

**Learning Objectives:**
- Implement and evaluate an ESN on the NARMA-10 benchmark (10th-order nonlinear autoregression)
- Understand why NARMA-10 requires exactly 10 steps of memory and a mild nonlinear interaction
- Measure NRMSE and connect it to the theoretical minimum determined by the task's complexity
- Sweep over reservoir hyperparameters (ρ, N, α) and identify the sensitivity of NARMA-10 performance
- Compare ESN performance to published benchmarks from Jaeger (2002) and subsequent literature
