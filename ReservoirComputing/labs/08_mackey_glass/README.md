**Corresponds to:** Chapter 9 — Benchmark Tasks: NARMA, Mackey-Glass, and the Standard Suite (Unit 3)

**Prerequisites:** Chapter 5 (ESN basics), Chapter 7 (spectral radius), delay differential equations (conceptual)

**Learning Objectives:**
- Generate the Mackey-Glass time series and understand its origins as a delay differential equation model of physiological feedback
- Apply an ESN to 1-step-ahead prediction and evaluate NRMSE on the standard tau=17 version
- Explore how the delay parameter tau affects the attractor dimension and prediction difficulty
- Understand why Mackey-Glass is harder than NARMA-10 despite lower apparent order
- Compare predictions from washout-trained vs. continuously-adapted reservoirs
