**Corresponds to:** Chapter 10 — Hyperparameter Selection: The Art and Science of Reservoir Design (Unit 3)

**Prerequisites:** Chapter 5-9 (core ESN material), grid search and cross-validation concepts, matplotlib

**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand the principal hyperparameters of an ESN: spectral radius ρ, leak rate α, input scaling σ_in, and ridge regularisation λ
- Conduct a systematic grid search and visualise hyperparameter interaction surfaces
- Understand why the leak rate α and spectral radius interact: the effective spectral radius of the leaky ESN is 1-α+α·ρ
- Identify hyperparameter sensitivity: which parameters matter most for which tasks?
- Develop principled heuristics for hyperparameter selection without full grid search
