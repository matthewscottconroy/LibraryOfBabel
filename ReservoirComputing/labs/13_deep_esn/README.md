**Corresponds to:** Chapter 14 — Deep Reservoir Computing: Hierarchical Timescales (Unit 5)

**Prerequisites:** Chapter 5-8 (core ESN material), Chapter 10 (hyperparameters), impulse response theory

**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand the deep ESN architecture: stacked reservoirs with decreasing leak rates create a hierarchy of timescales
- Analyse the impulse response of each layer and verify the theoretical time constant τ ≈ -1/ln(1-α)
- Demonstrate that deep ESNs outperform shallow ESNs on multiscale tasks by decomposing the signal hierarchy
- Explore Graph ESN as an extension to non-Euclidean structured data
- Conduct a hyperparameter search over depth L and leak rates, and understand their interaction with spectral radius
