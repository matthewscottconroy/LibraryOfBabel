# Overlap Implies Product (GEM)

**Statement**: In General Extensional Mereology, if $O(x, y)$ (x and y share a
common part), then the product $x \sqcap y$ — the greatest common part of $x$
and $y$ — exists.

The proof applies Unrestricted Fusion to the condition "is a common part of
$x$ and $y$" and uses Strong Supplementation to show the fusion is itself a
common part. Together with unrestricted fusions (arbitrary joins), this gives
GEM the structure of a complete Boolean algebra with the bottom element
removed.
