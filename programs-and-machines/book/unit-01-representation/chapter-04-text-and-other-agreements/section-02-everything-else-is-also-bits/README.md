# Everything Else Is Also Bits

Two lessons to close the unit.

The first takes color, sound, and images in turn and shows that each is the same
problem you have now watched solved three times: decide what to measure, decide
how finely, and accept what falls between the measurements. The numbers are
different; the shape is identical.

The second stands back and states the shape explicitly. Having seen five
instances — integers, fractions, text, color, sound — the general question comes
into focus, and it is the question the rest of the book keeps asking in new
costumes.
